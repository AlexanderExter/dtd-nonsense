### Core Mechanic

**XkY:** Roll X dice, keep and sum Y dice. 10s explode and count as one die.

Characters get a **Raise** for every 5 points rolled above the TN. Some free Raises only apply to an already successful roll.

Characters get a **Check** for every 5 points under the TN.

### Stunting

**One-die stunts** require a good description of an action. The character may perform feats that border on impossible (running across the heads of a crowd, deflecting a blade barehanded).

**Two-die stunts** require the character to interact with the NPCs or environment in a notable fashion and may perform limited dramatic editing to place convenient ropes and such.

**Three-die stunts** are singular acts of greatness, stunning bravado and visual poetry, defined by their capacity to leave the other players slack-jawed in astonishment. These feats allow for the same measure of dramatic editing as two-die stunts.

### Fear Tests

Fear: Test during scenes of atrocity and horror, battle against terrifying aliens, insane perversions of science, and facing nightmarish entities of the warp. **Willpower** vs. TN.

| Severity             | TN  | Example            |
| -------------------- | --- | ------------------ |
| Fear 1 - Disturbing  | 15  | Zombie Horde       |
| Fear 2 - Frightening | 20  | Angry Dragon       |
| Fear 3 - Horrifying  | 25  | Greater Daemon     |
| Fear 4 - Terrifying  | 30  | The King in Yellow |

On a failed Fear Test roll on the Shock Table at +1 per Check on the initial Fear Test. In combat apply the effects immediately. Non-combat situations apply a -1k0 to anything requiring concentration, and if the Fear Test was failed by 2 or more Checks the character also gains +1d5 Insanity Points.

Some Shock effects allow the character to "snap out of it". Test Composure against the initial Fear TN at the beginning of their turn until they succeed.

### Hero Points

(these can be used on other people's turns)

- Reroll a failed test, once
- Reduce a TN by 5 before rolling
- Add +5 to a successful test
- Count as having rolled a 10 for initiative (this can be used on other people's turns)
- Instantly recover from being Stunned

> Note: A Hero Point may be burned to preserve a character from certain death.

### Resource Points (used as free actions)

- Gain +1k0 to a skill test
- Recover from being Stunned
- Recover from being Dazzled
- Gain an additional Reaction Action
- Heal 1 HP out of combat

> Note: Exalts have unique uses and recovery methods for Resource Points.

### Insanity

If a character gains 10 Insanity Points during a session they may suffer mental trauma. Test Composure vs. 15 + 1 per 5 Insanity Points. Failure rolls on the Trauma table at +1 per check.

## Quick Reference Sheet 2: Combat Turns

### Combat Sequence

0. **Surprise** — Attackers get a free Raise to hit. Surprised characters cannot act in the first round.
1. **Initiative** — 1d10 + dots in Dexterity & Composure.
2. **Participants take turns.**
3. **Round ends.**
4. **Repeat as necessary.**

### Movement

- **Walking (Narrative):** 20× Speed per min., km in Speed per hr., double with a TN 10 + 5 × hr. Athletics test or gain Fatigue.
- **Shift:** Dexterity in meters without provoking opportunity attacks as a Half Action.
- **Move:** Speed in meters as a Half Action or twice that as a Full Action.
- **Withdraw:** Speed in meters leaving melee combat as a Full Action without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Run:** 6× Speed in meters as a Full Action with opponents' attacks at -2k0 ballistics & +2k0 melee.
- **Climbing:** 1/2 Speed + 1m per Raise on an Athletics test. If both hands free, 1+ in Athletics, and not in combat then automatic success. Free climbing the Cliffs of Insanity, engaging in combat, or using a cell phone while climbing is TN 15.
- **Swimming:** Athletics to move 1/2 Speed + 1m per Raise, failure is no progress, 2 or more checks indicate sinking. Normal conditions: Automatic success. Hazards, combat, and racing: TN 10. Medium to Extreme armor or carrying heavy loads: TN 20.
- **Jumping:** Athletics + Strength at TN 10 with a 4m running start or TN 15 without. Horizontal: Strength in meters + 1m per Raise, 1/2 Strength in meters on a failure. Vertical: 1/2 horizontal distances.
- **Falling Damage:** 1k1 per 2m + (Size)k0 & 1/2 AP, not including spikes. Terminal velocity per round (OK for Sizes 1-7): Size × 100m + 300m. Precise total distances for Size 4 falling people: 468m, 1169m, 1876m, 2583m, 3290m, 3997m. 1st turn: total 400m, 2nd turn: 1200m more, 3rd turn: 2000m more, 4th turn: 2600m more in Earth normal atmosphere.

### Social Combat Effects

- May attempt to build or erode someone's disposition toward yourself or another, or to compel a behavior.
- Targets spend Resolve to negate successful attacks.
- After 2 (or Fellowship) Resolve are spent, targets stop listening completely and cannot be successfully attacked. Stunts are required to make further successful social attacks.
- Characters @ zero Resolve and prevented from regaining full Resolve for days = Devotion have broken will.
- Characters with broken will may be compelled to act against their Alignment.
- Recover Resolve daily each morning by testing Composure TN 10 gaining 1 + 1/raise.
- At SM discretion, stunts furthering Alignment may recover Resolve.

## Quick Reference Sheet 4: Space Combat

### Spelljammer Rolls

Roll assigned Crew number, up to 10 Crew, and keep Crew Quality. Bridge Officers may use their skill rating in place of Crew Quality. Ships that are not moving are automatically hit by anything attacking them. Ship Static Defense is Maneuver + 3× Crew Quality.

### Space Combat Sequence

0. **Surprise** — Surprised ships cannot act in the first round.
1. **Initiative** — 1d10 + ship's Sensor Rating & Acceleration.
2. **Participants take turns.** Each ship must take a Maneuver action or be helpless.
3. **Round ends.**
4. **Repeat as necessary.**

- **Defense:** Block 1 Boarding Party, sacrifice 1 minion to block 1 weapon hit, work as Evasive Maneuvers vs. enemy fighter squadrons.

## Quick Reference Sheet 5: Space Travel

### Warp Travel Steps

1. **Leave a Crystal Sphere** — Active Augury TN 20 to find a portal, +10 with a map, automatic for active Syrneth portals & beacons. OR drop and modulate shields, Active Augury TN 25 + 5 per shield level to create a portal.
2. **Turn on Geller Field** — No test, but you die in the next step if you don't do it.
3. **Enter the Warp** — Automatic at Syrneth Relay Station or Navigator tests Arcana + Intelligence TN 15. Failure is 1d10 Fatigue.
4. **Chart a Course** — Navigator tests Divination + Wisdom, adding ship's Sensors, TN 20.
5. **Get Going** — Lead Pilot tests Piloting + Wisdom, + ship Acceleration, vs. chart TN. Fail rolls Warp Encounters at cumulative +1s until the Piloting roll is made..
6. **Exit the Warp** — Automatic at Syrneth Relay Station or Navigator tests Arcana + Intelligence TN 15. Failure is a potential crash. Failure with checks is damage and 1d10 Fatigue for the Navigator and a Warp Encounter.
7. **Enter a Crystal Sphere** — As in Step 1.

### Surface Bombardment

Assign crew to Maneuvers & Gunnery, optionally Engineering.

- TN 30 Maneuver Action to hold location over the target.
- Shoot at a city block or armored battalion, TN 30.
- Misses scatter 1k2 km + 1k0 km per check on the shooting test.
- Roll damage & apply to cities, etc.

### Silent Running

- Opposed Arcana Crew Action every time the ship changes course or the enemy scans the area. This is the only Arcana action you can make.
- Reduce Speed by half.
- Add +10 to all non-Arcana ship action TN.
- Failed Maneuver tests break stealth.

### Zero-G Combat

**Situations:** Braced or Out of Control.

- TN 15 to recover from being out of control OR change direction without right tool/vehicle.
- TN 10 to stop without an appropriate tool. Naturalized critters may not have to roll.
- TN 0 to catch on something & brace before changing direction.
- Melee strike @ 1/2 Str unless braced. -1k0 cumulative per attack roll unless braced.
- Explosion = out of control unless braced & Athletics+Str or Acrobat+Dex, TN = damage.

## Quick Reference Sheet 9: Foot Chases & Mounts

### Foot Chase Sequence

- **Step 0:** Follow the Leader. Leader is whoever is in front and trying to open up distance or lose the pursuers. A Follower is 1 of those after the Leader.
- **Step 1:** Roll Initiative. 1d10 + dots in Dex + dots in Com.
- **Step 2:** Winner's Turn. Winners decide what to do, pick a TN and an action. Losers react.
- **Step 3:** Loser's Turn. Losers decide what to do, picking a TN and action. Winners react.
- **Step 4:** Fatigue Test. All who move more than their normal max Speed this turn test Con + Will (keep the higher attribute) against the TN of their Test. Failure inflicts a level of Fatigue.
- **Step 5:** Check Distances. Update distances and have the SM decide if the chase scene continues.
- **Step 6:** Return to Step 1 if necessary.

### Foot Chase Actions

| Action               | Effect                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| More Speed           | +1 speed/2 raises, fail = -1/2 move.                                                                                                                                            |
| Sharp Turn           | +1/5 TN initiative, fail = damage or -1/2 move.                                                                                                                                 |
| Risky Move           | +1 spd & +1/5 TN init, 2nd TN of 5× spd: +1 spd & +1/raise.                                                                                                                     |
| Duck Out of Sight    | Reaction to opposed perception, failure -1/5 TN initiative.                                                                                                                     |
| Create Obstacle      | Reaction to evade or -1/5 move, checks = -all move.                                                                                                                             |
| Attack While Running | 1/2 move & called shot or multi-attack OR full move & standard or full auto, -2k0 replace all bonus, must hit TN & defense, dodge/parry reaction must beat only one of the TNs. |
| Serpentine           | Replace Static Defense with roll, +1 initiative per Raise, failure 1/2 defense & -1 speed per check.                                                                            |
| Help Out             | Reaction to let target reroll 1 die & +5/raise to target's roll.                                                                                                                |

### Vehicle Chase Actions

Choose a TN, opponents use a Reaction to make same test or you gain an advantage. Failed tests make you go Out of Control.

- **Drift:** Move any direction without changing facing.
- **Handbrake Turn:** At end of your move change facing to any direction.
- **Vault the Curb:** Ignore difficult and impassible terrain for this turn.
- **Slip By:** Move through "occupied spaces."
- **Barrel Roll:** Add +5 Static Defense this turn.
- **Puchilev's Cobra:** Decrease Momentum by up to 5 points without stalling flying vehicles.

### Mount Control

**TN** = 5× Level of the mount. If not aggressive or combat trained, attacks require extra raise.

- **Control Mount (Half or Full Action):** Mount performs Half or Full Action as instructed.
- **Special:** May use control Half Action to have mount repeat same Full Action as last turn.
- **Special:** Rider may use a Full Action as a Half Action after taking a Control Mount action but cannot then take Reactions until their next turn.
- **Special:** Rider uses own Reactions to stop mount from taking its Reactions.

**Roll when:**

- The rider is mounting up and taking control if the mount was uncontrolled, panicking, or free-running in combat.
- The mount fails a Fear test or the rider is trying to get the mount to shake off fear effects (use the Fear test TN, untrained mounts require an extra raise).
- The rider or mount takes critical damage (it may not matter depending on the damage, three long jump, special trick, etc.), either at the rider's command or on their own.
- Two characters are contesting for control. If the "mount" is the one contesting for control (cyborg T-Rex are touchy and prone to eating riders) it should roll Level + Willpower or its Brawl attack test.

**Do NOT roll when:**

- The rider is just mounting up and there's nothing really unusual about the situation, even mounting a warhorse during combat doesn't take a roll.
- The rider is just doing normal maneuvers, even in combat.
- The mount takes a minor nick or a level of Fatigue. Half its Hit Points is not "minor", though.
- The rider and mount are in total agreement (running away from the 30m tall spiked-tentacle-rape daemons).
- They've already rolled once for the action. "Do something" is one roll, "do something, then fail a fear test, then take a sucking chest wound critical" can be two or three rolls.

## Quick Reference Sheet 10: Vehicle Rules

### Vehicle Static Defense

`Base Vehicle Static Defense: 10 - (2× Size) + Maneuver`

| Momentum | Speed Bonus     |
| -------- | --------------- |
| @ 0      | 0               |
| @ 1-5    | Base + Speed    |
| @ 6-9    | Base + 2× Speed |
| @ 10+    | Base + 3× Speed |

Untrained: Use the lesser of Intelligence or Dexterity.

Control Test: Skill + Attribute, +Maneuver & -Momentum.

**If nobody takes a Maintain Control action the vehicle goes Out of Control.**

### Actions

| Action               | Type        | TN / Notes                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maintain Control     | Half Action | TN usually automatic. Accelerate, decelerate, or turn up to 90° if Maneuver > 0.                    |
| Punch It - Speed     | Half Action | TN 3× Momentum. +/-1 additional Momentum or Acceleration until next turn.                           |
| Punch It - Turn      | Half Action | TN 3× Momentum. Turn and accelerate or decelerate.                                                  |
| Punch It - Hard Turn | Half Action | TN 10 + 2× Momentum. 180° turn, maintaining current Momentum.                                       |
| Fire Weapon          | Half/Full   | Uses the regular Ballistics, Weaponry, Brawling rules.                                              |
| Full Auto Burst      | Half Action | A vehicle may fire a Full Auto Burst weapon as a Half Action but then cannot use Evasive Maneuvers. |
| Evasive Maneuver     | Reaction    | Reduce Momentum by 1, 1/2 the result of the Control test is added to the vehicle Static Defense.    |
| Stop Falling         | Special     | TN 10 + Size as part of the Maintain Control action.                                                |
| Ramming              | Half Action | Special, see RAMMING.                                                                               |

### Terrain

- **Difficult Terrain:** 1/2 speed & at Momentum 6+ Control Test vs. TN 5 + 2× Momentum or go Out of Control.
- **Impassible Terrain:** Crash: As Out of Control #10 or Ramming into the terrain.

### Out of Control Vehicles

Flying vehicles do not roll on the chart. They begin Falling.

| Roll | Result        | Effect                                                                   |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1-4  | Straight Edge | Vehicle continues in straight line this turn.                            |
| 5-7  | Swerve        | Turns randomly. Roll direction & amount.                                 |
| 8-9  | Wild Stallion | As "Swerve" & change Momentum by Acceleration. 50/50 Momentum is + or -. |
| 10   | Turn Over     | Vehicle flips. Lose HP = Momentum, turns on its back, Momentum is now 0. |

### Falling

A flying vehicle that goes Out of Control or loses 2+ HP begins falling.

- 1st turn: 400m, 2nd turn +1200m, 3rd turn +2000m, 4th turn +2700m.
- Terminal velocity: Size × 100m per turn. (Earth normal grav & atmo)
- **Pull out:** TN 10 + Size as part of Maintain Control action.
- **Hit the Ground:** Damage = (Size)k10 + Speed + Drive Rating, each extra Momentum over Size adds +5 damage.

### Ramming

- Control Test vs. Static Defense to hit or automatic if Out of Control.
- Dodge or Acrobatics vs. TN vehicle Speed + Momentum + Drive Rating.
- **Damage:** (Size)k(Momentum) + Speed + Drive Rating, excess Momentum adds +5 each.
- **Control test:** Skill + Composure or Constitution vs. TN = damage.
    - **Success:** -2 Momentum.
    - **Failure:** -3 Momentum & vehicle is Out of Control.
