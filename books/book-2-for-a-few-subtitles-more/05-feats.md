### Acceptable Losses

A Captain has to be able to sacrifice lives for the greater good. While serving as Captain on a ship, once per turn you may give any ship action a `+1k0` bonus. At the end of the turn your ship loses 1 Crew.

### Air of Authority

You have a natural affinity for playing crowds. While in social combat, you can influence everyone who hears you and is paying attention.

### Animal Companion

Druids can call animals from the wild around them to aid them, often developing close friendships with them. You get a minion or a critter. It is either a single minion with a Threat Rating of 1 and a Damage Rating of 1, or a Level 1 animal. If it is killed or set free, the minion can be replaced after 24 hours with a simple ritual.

See Appendix L for more information on animal companion options. If choosing the minion animal companion, it begins as a single critter with Threat and Damage ratings of 1.

### Arcane Blade

Your magical skills allow you to keep arcane fields of power around your blades. Your Weaponry attacks all count as magic.

### Arcane Mark

When you strike an opponent with a weapon, you may make an `Arcana + Intelligence` Test against their Static Defense. If it succeeds, you may brand them with your Arcane Mark, a magical mark that allows you to track their position. As long as you remain in the same Crystal Sphere (or outside of one, if you and your target are on the Astral Plane) you can sense the presence of your target well enough to track them to a general location. While in combat your target doesn't benefit from Ganging Up on you and you treat their Static Defense as being 5 points lower. You can only have one target marked in this way at a time - if you brand a new target with your Arcane Mark the old target is freed.

### Bear Hug

You can grab people and hold them with the strength of a Grizzly. If you are grappling, your strength is considered one point higher for the purpose of your and your opponents attempts to Break Free. Your opponent's Slip Free TN is increased by 5.

### Beastmaster

Level up your animal companion! The size of your Animal Companion minion squad is increased to your Level +1, and it gains +1 both Threat and Damage, up to a maximum of 5. If you have a critter it's Level is equal to yours, it gains +1 to two attributes, and the SM picks a feat or trait for it.

> **Clarification:** See Appendix L for more information. Suggested feats for animal companions include: Devastating Critical, Fearless (Angry), Improved Weapon Focus, Iron Jaw, Swift Attack, True Grit. Suggested traits: Armor(4), Aura(4), DarkSight, Regeneration(1), Unnatural Toughness.

### Binary Chatter

The character can communicate with computers in the most basic, primal way - by making modem noises right out of his mouth hole. In addition to reminding older immortals of what things used to be like when they were on 56k, they can also interface with computers merely by speaking, and get a `+2k0` on all Tech-Use tests involving computers.

### Bodge Together

You know how to make your supplies last longer, even when you run out of self-sealing stem bolts. While serving as Chief of Engineering on a ship, your ship may make an additional Extended Repairs test between resupply stops at port.

### Chem Geld

With chemical treatments (or extensive mental training and discipline), you can mostly ignore the temptations of the flesh. Your Mental Defense is considered 10 higher for the purpose of defending against people attempting to seduce or otherwise persuade you with, ah, pleasure instead of business.

### Combat Sense

Your finely-honed senses allow you to see exactly where things are going. You may use Wisdom in place of Dexterity when using the Dodge action or in place of your Level on attacks that benefit from the Aim action.

> **Clarification:** When using Wisdom + Acrobatics to Dodge, the maximum Dexterity limit of armors does not apply (because you are not using Dexterity). Aimed attacks may use Wisdom + Skill instead of Level + Skill.

### Courtier's Privilege

A courtier can quickly rise through the ranks of an organization, find friends, or simply become very famous. You gain an additional rank in Allies, Backing, Contacts, Fame, Followers, or Status, all to a maximum of 5.

### Crushing Bear

You no longer grab people like a Grizzly. You grab them like TWO Grizzlies! While grappling someone, you automatically deal your unarmed attack damage every turn in addition to your other Grapple actions.

### Daggerspell Stance

You have been able to blend your spell casting into your sword fighting well enough to keep yourself well defended. Your Focus Power tests no longer provoke Opportunity Attacks.

### Detailed Analysis

Your scans run deep and detailed. While serving as Chief Arcana Officer on a ship, you gain a free Raise on Active Augury tests.

### Diamond Body

Your body is protected well against the mundane weapons of the world. Your Wholeness of Body provides an additional +4 bonus to your armor, which is ignored by magic weapons.

> **Open Question:** The SM must decide whether this armor works like physical armor for the purpose of abilities such as the Paladin class track's "+1 to AP" features.

### Discipline

Your training has given you the edge in social situations. You gain +1 Resolve.

### Druid's Oath

You swear not to wear metal armor and must at least attempt to respect nature. As long as you abide by your oath, you may reroll a Warp Phenomenon roll once per round as long as you are casting spells from the Healing or Transmutation schools.

> **Play Tip:** This trade-off is more valuable than it may appear. See Appendix B for the Safe Magic & Warp Rolls discussion.

### Elemental Shot I

Choose one of the following three elemental types. Once per day you may summon a minor elemental spirit of the appropriate type and bind it to your weapon (as a Half Action). Your next attack with the weapon gains the listed effect. You may take this feat multiple times, choosing a different element each time.

- **Fire** - Your weapon deals E damage and the target is Set On Fire.
- **Ice** - Your weapon deals I damage and gains the Snare property.
- **Bolt** - Your weapon deals E damage and has the Shocking property.

> **Open Question:** When a character gains extra uses of Elemental Shot feats, the SM must decide: do they gain more uses of that specific level of the feat, or more uses of any Elemental Shot feat they possess?

### Elemental Shot II

Choose one of the following three elemental types. Once per day you may summon a minor elemental spirit of the appropriate type and bind it to your weapon (as a Half Action). Your next attack with the weapon gains the listed effect. You may take this feat multiple times, choosing a different element each time.

- **Aero** - Your weapon deals I damage and anyone hit by the attack is moved up to 10 meters by a sudden gust of wind.
- **Aqua** - Your weapon deals R damage and gains Proven (3).
- **Geo** - Your weapon deals I damage and anyone hit by the attack is knocked prone as the ground shatters and breaks. Flying enemies are immune to the knockdown effect.

### Elemental Shot III

Choose one of the following three elemental types. Once per day you may summon a minor elemental spirit of the appropriate type and bind it to your weapon (as a Half Action). Your next attack with the weapon gains the listed effect. You may take this feat multiple times, choosing a different element each time.

- **Holy** - Your weapon deals X damage and the target must roll for Psychic Phenomenon. For every damage die that explodes add +5 to the roll.
- **Gravity** - Your weapon deals X damage, gains Tearing, and treats those hit by it as if they had one less Resilience.

### Engineering Team Leader

With the right training, you learn how to get by with fewer people. While serving as Chief of Engineering on a ship, you gain an additional Crew to use for Engineering Actions only. This Crew cannot be removed by critical damage.

### Expert Tracker

You are an expert at tracking people - almost like you have the nose (or ears, or possibly other attributes) of a bloodhound. The TN for you to track someone is reduced by 5. There are tracking rules in advanced rules

### Extracurricular Study

You can learn almost anything if you put your mind to it. You gain a rank in a Sword School, Gun Kata, or Magic School, up to a maximum of half your level, rounded up.

### Feather Step

You can walk on any surface, even if it would not normally support your weight. This allows you to walk on thin rope, cloth awnings, and even mud, quicksand, or water.

### Ferric Lure

The character may cause an unsecured metal object within his field of vision to fly into his hand. The object may weigh up to the character's Willpower in kilograms and must lie within 10 meters. Using this talent takes a Full Action.

### Ferric Summons

The character may cause a metal object within his field of vision to fly around. The object may weigh up to four times the character's Willpower in kilograms and must lie within 40 meters. For purposes of throwing stuff around or tearing it up the character has an effective Strength equal to their Willpower, but no Dexterity to speak of. Using this talent takes a Full Action.

### Gain Access

You have crypto-keys and Icepicks that can get you into all kinds of trouble. You get `+1k0` to all Tech-Use tests made to hack into systems or otherwise break into electronic databases. While serving as Chief Arcana Officer on a ship, you get this bonus to Jam Communications tests and tests to break through jamming or interference.

### Gun Blessing

You may make an Arcana Test (TN 20) as a Half Action to unjam your gun, make it count as a magic weapon for one scene, or improve its quality by one step for one scene. You cannot stack the improvements and they don't work on Artifacts, but unjamming a gun is always OK.

### Hailing Frequencies

While serving on a ship, even if you aren't Captain, when the Hail action is used the target is forced to respond. If this action is used in combat, combat immediately ends and social combat begins. Then you can at least find out why they're shooting at you and perhaps even ask them to stop before they open fire again.

### Headstrong

Some people think they can outsmart you. Maybe. But you can outsmart a bullet. You are immune to Pinning.

### Improved Animal Companion

Either you've gotten a new companion of a more dangerous type or you've got an exceptional specimen. If you have minions their Threat or Damage Rating increases by one, and the number of minions in the squad is equal to your Level. If you have a critter it gets +1 dot to any attribute but Intelligence, a feat selected by the SM, and it's level is one less than your Level. If your animal companion dies, the replacement has the same improvements.

### Improved Wild Shape

You can change into more dangerous animals. When using Wild Shape you may move an additional point from one physical stat to another, and may choose to gain one additional trait from the list of: Armor Plating(3), DarkSight, Flyer, or Aura(3).

See Appendix L for more information on available animal forms.

### Improvisational Master

You can use even a tin cup to go through light armor. When you use Improvised Weapons, the weapon gains a +2 bonus to it's Penetration.

### Improvisational Savant

Improvised weapons you use gain a special quality that is appropriate for the item. A trash can lid might be Defensive, a length of chain might get Flexible, a ladder could have Reach, and so forth. The SM has final say on what special qualities are appropriate or not.

### Improvisational Warrior

You can put the hurt on someone with even the most innocuous of items. When you use Improvised Weapons, they deal an additional `+1k0` damage.

### Iron Curtain

You can create a powerful, poorly understood shield of energy around your body, always with some accompanying visual effect - some people darken to almost black, some glow bright red or blue. You may spend a Hero Point to ignore all damage until the start of your next turn.

### Iron Fist

You have mastered the sacred and powerful art of the Iron Fist. Your unarmed strikes get +4 Pen and deal Explosive damage.

> **Clarification:** This stacks with the Weapon Focus and Weapon Specialization feats.

### Iron Tower

There is a mystical, mysterious power in steel. You may use a melee weapon as though it were an Implement for the purpose of all feats or class abilities.

### Jerry Rig

Sometimes you need someone who knows how to cross some wires and keep things working even while bits are missing or exploding. While serving as Chief of Engineering on a ship, whenever you take the Emergency Repair action to repair Hull Integrity, you restore an additional `1d5` points of Hull Integrity.

### Just As Planned

You may spend a Hero Point to have retroactively prepared or taken any action within the last ten minutes that would just now come into play, such as making a discrete comm hail for assistance or turned on a small recording device. Note that retroactively taking this action cannot change anything else that has already happened, though it may be addressed as normal now. And you clearly planned this all in advance, you devious bastard.

### Ki Strike

Your punches are pure magic. Your unarmed and natural attacks count as magical weapons.

### Lead Fingers

You may use your gun to push or manipulate objects at a distance. You can only use this at distances up to the weapon's Short Range, and are somewhat limited in what you can do. You can press buttons, flip switches, push objects, and otherwise do anything you can manage with one finger. This ability works only with physical projectiles, directed energy weapons won't work. The SM has final say on what will work and what won't.

> **Clarification:** The SM also has final say on how much you damage the equipment. The Clay Pigeon gun kata pairs well with this feat. At the Story Master's option, you may have to roll damage or roll 1d10 less than the weapon's Armor Penetration value to determine if the equipment being shot is damaged.

### Lend Expertise

When you use the Picard Speech action, you get additional bonuses. If you have at least 3 ranks in Tech-Use, Arcana, or Ballistics, the Crew Quality is increased by two (instead of the normal one) for Engineering, Arcana, or Tactical actions respectively.

### Luminen Blast

By reciting the proper litany, the character channels some of the energy stored in his Potentia Coil down augmetic conduits paralleling the bones of his arms to direct it at his enemies. The character gains a ranged attack that deals `0k2` E damage, `+1k0` per point of Willpower the character has, with a 10m range and the Shocking quality. The character must pass a Constitution test (TN 15 + 5 times how often the ability has been used in the scene) when he uses this ability or gain a level of Fatigue. If the character is immune to Fatigue then he must spend a Resource Point in order to use this Ability.

> **Play Tip:** For example, after the first shot test Constitution vs. TN 15 or gain a Fatigue. After the second shot test Constitution vs. TN 20, and so on. A decent custom gun is often simpler for sustained ranged damage.

### Luminen Charge

The character has mastered the union between his mechanical elements and his mortal flesh, allowing the former to power the latter. With about a minute of the correct meditations and prayers, the character can recharge batteries by taking Fatigue. Small items like weapon power packs take one level of Fatigue. Jump-starting vehicles takes two levels, and larger things like powering ship actions take three or more levels of Fatigue as decided by the SM. Characters that are immune to Fatigue must spend twice as many Resource Points as they would have taken Fatigue.

### Mana Generator

You may use the Aid Another action to give another spell caster a free Raise on their next successful Focus Power test. This bonus is lost if they don't make a Focus Power test before your next turn.

### Master of Bombardment

While serving as Tactical Officer on a ship, you may reduce the TN to hit a planetary object by 5. If you miss the target, you may reroll the direction and distance of the scattered shot. You may choose which result to use.

### Master of Disguise

As long as you aren't being observed, or at least not being observed by anyone that you want to be fooled, you can disguise yourself with a Full Action without this haste affecting the quality of your disguise. You are also always considered to have at least adequate materials to make a disguise.

### Match Frequency

While serving as Tactical Officer on a ship, once per scene you may double the disruption of all the ship's weapons on a target that has been subject to your ship's Active Augury since your ship's last turn.

### Mechadendrite Use

It takes extensive training to make use of limbs that you weren't born with. You may take and use Mechadendrite implants.

### Meditation

Once per session, the character may meditate quietly for an hour to regain one spent (but not burned) Hero Point.

### Mechanicus Implants

The character is a servant of the Machine-God, and an initiate into the mysteries of the Priesthood of the Omnissiah and as such is granted the following implants:

- **Electro-Graft:** A small port that is grafted into the nervous system. This allows him to interface with machine data ports and certain types of data nets. Electro-grafts function as a poor quality Mind-Impulse Unit.
- **Electoo Inductors:** Palm-sized metal skin grafts that appear much like tattoos to the uninitiated. They are wired into the nervous system and derive power from the sacred mysteries of the Potentia Coil. They can be used to emit or siphon power in many ways.
- **Respirator Unit:** Tubes, wires, vox-grills, or other cybernetic parts replacing the neck and upper chest. This unit acts as a common bionic respirator unit. The unit also contains a synthesizer capable of transmitting the voice in a variety of ways.
- **Cyber-Mantle:** A framework of metal, wires, and impulse transmitters bolted onto the spine and lower rib cage. This mantle acts as a sub-dermal anchorage point.
- **Potentia Coil:** Within the Cyber-Mantle is a power unit known as the Potentia Coil. This mass can store energy and produce various types of fields. Coils come in many types, from small crystal stack affairs to bulky electrical galvinators.

> **Design Note:** Originally this feat had no mechanical rules or benefits and existed solely as a prerequisite tax for the Tech-Priest classes. The implants listed above were added to give it standalone value.

### Miracle Worker

While serving as Chief of Engineering on a ship, whenever you take the Emergency Repair action to restore damaged systems you may attempt to restore two damaged systems at once (making two separate Tests).

### Naturalize

By spending a Hero Point you may create a magical burst that disrupts technology in an area. This creates a 3-meter radius burst within 10 meters. All actions utilising technology, including firing non-Primitive ranged weapons, Tech-Use tests, and any physical actions attempted while wearing power armor or employing cybernetics, suffer a `-2k0` penalty. Melee weapons with technological components count as merely Hand Weapons or whatever other weapon the SM determines is most appropriate.

> **Clarification:** This does turn off powered melee weapons like chain-axes and power swords, reducing them to mundane Hand Weapons.

### Nature Sense

You have a kind of sixth sense that allows you to sense animals and natural hazards like quicksand and poison ivy within 10 meters. This doesn't allow you to sense unnatural dangers (like undead, Modrons, or worse - assuming worse than Modrons is even a thing).

### Nekomimi Mode

Instead of turning fully into an animal, you can choose to only partially shift. When using Nekomimi Mode, your equipment doesn't meld into your form, allowing you to use it, and you retain a humanoid appearance. However, this partial shift is unstable, and so you may only maintain it for five rounds. After those five rounds are up, you return to your normal form and cannot use Wild Shape for one hour.

> **Open Question:** The SM must decide whether cybernetics count as equipment that melds into the form, or whether they function more like the werewolf shapeshifting where everything still works.

### Nerves of Steel

You've got balls of steel. I mean nerves. Anyway, you don't scare easy, and are immune to fear effects. In addition, your Mental Defense is considered 10 higher when someone attempts to intimidate you.

### Paranoia

You know danger lurks behind every corner. You gain a +2 bonus on initiative rolls and the SM may secretly test to notice hidden threats.

### Perfect Self

You no longer need to eat, drink, or sleep as long as you can find time to meditate for an hour every day. You gain Aura equal to your Wisdom.

### Pinball Wizard

Not only can you put holes in people, you can shoot right through firewalls. Up to your gun's short range you can use it to manipulate electronics. This can be used to open or close electronic locks, shut down machinery, and anything else the SM deems dramatically appropriate.

> **Clarification:** This can be treated as a variant of the Lead Fingers feat. Unlike Lead Fingers, it can work with energy weapons if you accept some level of damage to the equipment. Shooting out an electronic lock with a blaster to jam a door will work, though since that destroys the controls it is generally irreversible.

### Protocol

You automatically know enough about local customs that you don't get tripped up by matters of protocol. This acts something like Common Sense, except the character uses `Common Lore + Intelligence` (TN 15) in place of Common Sense's straight Wisdom test.

### Raven's Eye

You find it easy to pick out your target in a crowd. You can automatically spot someone you're looking for in a crowd of people, though if they're disguised you'll have to see through it first.

### Redshirt Shield

While you serve as Captain on a ship, the crew will go to great lengths to protect you. As long as your ship is in port your crew tends to be close at hand and ready to help. The first time you'd take damage in a session, a random crewman appears from as if out of nowhere and takes the blow, dying horribly and saving you.

### Research

You automatically know the local rulers and general trivia about them in any place you go to, as long as such knowledge is something that can be found after a few hours of research - you don't automatically know the ruler of a newly-discovered Crystal Sphere, for example.

### Rock and Roll

If you use Full Auto Burst, you get a free Raise on the attack roll for every consecutive round in which you've used Full Auto Burst. (i.e.: You use Full Auto Burst in round 1. In round two you use it again and get a free Raise. In round three you use it again and get two free Raises. In round 4 you stop to reload. In round 5 you use Full Auto Burst and get no Raises.)

### Rotate Shield Frequency

While serving as Chief Arcana Officer on a ship, once per scene, as an Arcana Action you may make a TN 20 Arcana Test. If it succeeds, you may remove all disruption that your shields take on that round.

### Spell Bullet

You may prepare a bullet, arrow, bolter round, etc., to hold a single spell. You must cast the spell while preparing the ammunition, making any needed Focus Power tests. You may only have one Spell Bullet prepared in this way at a time. When the round is fired the spell affects, or is centered on, whatever the shot hits. This ability does not work with energy weapons.

### Spell Parry

By spending a Hero Point you may attempt to Parry a spell that targets you and only you. The TN for the Parry attempt is equal to the result of the Focus Power test of the spell.

### Spell Shield

You can create a mystical shield with your free hand that protects you from spells. As long as you have one hand free and make the appropriate warding gestures, you gain a bonus to your Aura equal to your ranks in Arcana.

> **Clarification:** The warding gestures are generally obvious to observers. You are not maintaining them constantly while conscious—you can start making the gestures as a free action on your turn or as a reaction at any other time.

### Steel Rain

Some stay dry but others feel the pain. People trying to dodge your Full Auto Bursts take a penalty to the Dodge roll equal to twice the number of rounds you fire.

### Storm of Iron

You know how to pump the trigger to get ten percent more bullet out of your gun. If you fire a weapon on Full Auto, you may increase its rate of fire by 1.

### Stunning Fist

When you hit someone, you can get them right in the death spots. You may spend a Resource Point or gain a level of Fatigue to give your unarmed strikes the Shocking quality until the end of your turn.

> **Optional Rule:** The SM may want to allow this feat to be used once per scene without cost, in order to make it more useful or attractive.

### Sword Beam

When you make an All Out Attack you may choose to make a Sword Beam attack in place of its usual benefits. Your melee attack gains the Reach property and can strike at opponents up to 10m away. You suffer all the normal penalties for using an All Out Attack.

### Tachyon Beam

While serving as Chief Arcana Officer on a ship, you may use an Arcana Action in place of any other action once per session, via the power of technobabble. Make sure to reverse something, possibly get some quantum in there, and to never use the same explanation twice.

### Trance

You may enter a deep trance state, able to go a long time without moving, eating, drinking, or even blinking. You can remain in this trance for a number of days equal to your Constitution and come out of it instantly - you're still aware of everything that's going on around you. Once you've come out of the trance you need to get some food and a decent night's rest before you can go back into it.

### Unarmed Master

You have mastered a form of martial arts. Or at least you punch really good. Your unarmed strikes deal an additional `+0k1` damage.

### Unarmed Warrior

You study martial arts and your fists are now even more dangerous. Your unarmed strikes deal an additional `+1k0` damage.

### Upgraded

Every time the character takes this feat, the character gains a cybernetic implant. This feat will always list the maximum rarity of the implant that can be gained with this feat. If no rarity is listed, treat it as having a maximum rarity of Uncommon.

### Wholeness of Body

You gain an Armor rating equal to your Wisdom. This works like any other regular source of armor, and does not stack, as usual.

### Wild Empathy

You can speak with animals. This isn't quite speech as it is between two people, but more of a general understanding and pantomime type of thing. This allows you to use social skills as normal with animals, though. Unfortunately, it allows them to do the same to you assuming they have any.

### Wild Shape

You can turn into an animal. This allows you to shift your physical stats around slightly, taking one point from one of your physical stats (to a minimum of 1 - if you only have 1 in a stat you cannot remove that last point from it) and put it on a different physical stat. You may also choose to gain one of Quadruped, Crawler, or Amphibious. While using Wild Shape you appear as a normal animal of whatever type you've chosen to turn into, and like most animals you cannot cast spells or manipulate objects particularly well. Your equipment melds into your new form and you lose any effects it gives. Using Wild Shape to turn to or from an animal form is a Full Action, and you can remain in animal shape as long as you wish.

### Worf Barrage

While serving as Tactical Officer on a ship, you may reroll critical damage dealt by your ship's weapons once per scene. You must use the result of the second roll, even if it is worse.

### Worf Effect

Whenever an enemy can target you with an attack, they will do so unless they can make an Intelligence Test at TN 15. Once an enemy has passed this test, they may attack other targets normally for the rest of the encounter.

### Zen Shooting

You can use your Perception in place of your Ballistics skill with any weapon you are proficient with.

### Racial Feat Descriptions

### Ace Pilot [Kenku]

Kenku are natural pilots, able to push their machines harder than anyone else. When they serve as Helmsman for a ship, the ship's Maneuver is increased by +5. Any vehicle they pilot also gets +5 to Maneuver.

### Ancestral Recall [Eldarin]

Eldarin have a deep connection to the mysteries of the warp that is second only to the ancient Syrneth. You have a more direct connection than most. Gain `+1k0` to all Focus Power tests and +10 on Warp Phenomena rolls.

### Beneficial Mutation [Tiefling]

Tieflings are not made to any particular plan, their bodies can continue to mutate throughout their lives, often in response to great failures or injury. Gain +2 dots in your lowest characteristic, and -1 dot in another characteristic. If you have more than one lowest characteristic, pick one.

### Developed Wings [Kenku]

While all Kenku have wings, not every Kenku's wings are strong enough to allow them to actually fly. Yours, however, are that strong. Your Wing-Aided Movement ability is upgraded to Flyer, at your normal Speed.

### Elder Wyrm's Fire [Dragonborn]

Some dragonborn have stronger breath weapons than the average, often with more impressive visual effects like blue fire or bolts of lightning. Any breath weapon you have deals `+1k1` damage.

### Escape Artist [Halfling]

Halflings are pretty much dead if they can't move. You aren't likely to die from that anytime soon. You roll twice and take the better result to resist Immobilization. If the effect does not allow a roll to resist it you can roll `Level + Dexterity` to resist it.

### Explorer [Gnome]

Gnomes can find the strangest things, and are especially fascinated with forgotten ruins and hidden Crystal Spheres. You may serve as Navigator aboard a Spelljammer without dots in Divination magic, and you count your journey as one step lower on the Voyage by Time and Difficulty table.

### K'sten'mannav [Kobold]

Because of the respect most kobolds have for the K'sten'mannav and their upholding the ideals of their race, kobolds will not willingly attack one unless he attacks them first. Of course, this doesn't extend to anyone he is with, but he might be able to get a few words in while his friends are getting stabbed. You gain Peer (Kobolds) and may force an encounter with kobolds to begin with social combat instead of physical combat. Additionally because of their long-ingrained distrust for others, they gain the Paranoia feat.

### K'vend'l [Kobold]

K'vend'l say they are the greatest merchants in the Wheel. They keep close connections to each other and can find nearly anything, even things that others think impossible to locate at market. The maximum TN of an item you can find in a location is increased by 5, and you reduce the TN of Wealth Tests by 5 when buying stuff from other kobolds.

> **Clarification:** This feat only really works for acquiring stuff from kobolds or groups that are heavily influenced by kobolds. When combined with the Ventrue vampire asset the discount became too broadly powerful, so the kobold limitation is intentional.

### Experienced Miner [Kobold]

Almost every kobold is born and raised in a mine, deep underground and safe from the prejudices and attacks of other races. You gain `+2k1` to all tests with underground and mining stuff like spelunking, secret doors, stone traps, etc.

### Lightning Bug [Thri-Kreen]

You have a natural bio-luminescence that allows you to produce light like a lantern at will. With effort you can vary the color and intensity of the light through different shades of red, yellow, and green. You can also expend your internal energy to shoot a bolt of bio-electricity. You gain the Luminen Blast feat.

### Made of Mettle [Aasimar]

Aasimar do not like having weaknesses. Most Aasimar will train hard to eliminate whatever they feel is their greatest weakness. +1 to your lowest characteristic.

### Mandibles [Thri-Kreen]

All Thri-Kreen have a mildly toxic bite, but for most it doesn't cause more than a rash. Your bite is significantly more dangerous. You gain a natural weapon with the following profile: `1k1` R, pen 4, Brawling, Toxic, Razor-Sharp.

### Matron [Dryad]

Unlike the majority of Dryad adventurers, you are in your third stage of life, a huge treelike being with skin like bark and arms like strong branches. Your attributes are different from the average Dryad. You lose a point of Fellowship and another from Dexterity, but gain a point of Strength. You lose the Dryad's racial power of Pheromones and gain +2 Size. You also gain the Armor Plating (4) trait and your Brawling attacks deal `+2k0` damage thanks to your club-like fists.

> **Open Question:** If the SM has decided to use the Not An Animal alternate racial power for all Dryads (instead of Pheromone Pollen), the "lose the Pheromones" portion of this feat needs to be adjusted or replaced.

### Mixed Heritage [Human]

Some humans will have sex with anything, and one of your parents did. Choose a race. You may take racial feats that require that race. If the racial feat references or improves a racial ability you do not have (such as Elven Accuracy), you may not take that feat.

### Noisy Cricket [Thri-Kreen]

Thri-Kreen are naturally suited towards long jumps thanks to their powerful legs. You gain Jumping specialties in Acrobatics and Athletics. Whenever you make a Test to jump, every Raise you get counts as two Raises.

### No One Tougher [Squat]

You may use Constitution instead of Dexterity for determining your Static Defense.

### Photosynthetic [Dryad]

As plants, all Dryads can gain some nourishment from sunlight, but you can gain everything you need from the light of the sun. You no longer need to eat food as long as you spend at least two hours basking in direct sunlight, and you heal one hit point per hour you spend in direct sunlight. Vampire Dryads do not gain these benefits, but instead lose a vampire's Sunlight Weakness.

### Precise Technique [Elf]

Elves are good at everything, especially when they cheat. Use an Elven Accuracy reroll with any skill Test once per Level per session.

### Recluse [Dark Eldarin]

Dark Eldarin often spend decades making themselves inured to toxins and poisons, either on purpose to better survive their harsh culture or on accident as part of life-long drug abuse. You are immune to all poisons and the Toxic weapon effect. The TN to resist any toxins you personally craft is 5 higher than usual.

### Silent Arcana [Tau]

Tau are not naturally good at spell casting, but they can suppress a lot of the pomp and circumstance that others use when they get around to trying their hand at it. You can increase the TN of a Focus Power Test by 10. If you do, it loses the Verbal, Somatic, and Material keywords and gains the Subtle keyword if it doesn't already have it.

### Teacher [Kenku]

Kenku are great teachers, and even a few words of advice can give people great insight into an area the kenku knows well. Once per session per skill, a Kenku can use Aid Another on anyone they can speak with (including through comm-link, radio, etc.) and to use one of the kenku's Specialties on the aided test that person makes.

### Trapmaster [Kobold]

Kobolds are lousy at standing up and fighting fair, but they're excellent at fighting dirty. You gain free Traps Specialties in Crafts, Perception, Scrutiny, and Tech-Use.

### Treestrider [Dryad]

One of the most famous powers of the Dryads is their ability to appear anywhere in a forest. While not every Dryad can actually do this, you can. Once a scene you may, as a Full Action, enter one living tree or other large plant and then exit another large living plant anywhere within one kilometer per Level. These plants need to be at least as big as you are. You don't have to know your exact destination, just a general distance and direction, and you can't use other dryads as the plants. You gain an additional use of this power each scene at Levels 3 and 5.

### WAAAAAGH CRY! [Ork]

If there is anything an ork does well, it's motivating other orks to krump things harder. If you're especially good, you can even motivate non-orks in the same way. When ganging up on an enemy, you and your allies get an additional `+1k0` to hit and damage with melee attacks.

## Exalt Assets

### Dragonblood Assets

### Adamic Dragon

There are certain extremely rare dragons, that are to other dragons as those dragons are to men. These primordial beasts are infused with power beyond any other, ancient super-weapons created by the Syrneth and locked away for all time. Somehow, some way, you have a tiny fraction of their power. Choose one of the following to replace your normal Blood Quickening choices:

- **Metal:** The Dragon Blooded gains a +1 to Resilience. By spending a point of Breath as a Half Action they gain a stacking bonus to their Armor equal to their Aspect, until the start of their next turn. Their claws have Pen equal to their Aspect and gain the Razor Sharp quality.
- **Void:** The Dragon Blooded gains a +1 to Composure. They do not need to breathe and can survive without a void suit in the vacuum of space. Wounds dealt by the Hero's claws cannot be healed by spending Resource Points, Regeneration, magical or other forms of accelerated healing. Their claws have Pen equal to their Aspect and gain the Proven weapon quality with a rating equal to half their Level rounded up.
- **Heart:** The Dragon Blooded gains a +1 to Fellowship. By spending two points of Breath as a Free Action, they may treat any one die rolled on a social attack or with a social skill as a 10, which then explodes normally. Their claws have Pen equal to their Aspect and gain the Power Field quality.
- **Warp:** The Dragon Blooded gains a +1 to Willpower. They gain one rank of the Evocation magic school and `+0k1` on Focus Power tests to cast Evocation spells. The Hero may buy ranks in this school as if it were part of any class they belong to. Their claws have Pen equal to their Aspect and gain the Orgone Array weapon quality.

### Blood of Bahamut

The Dragon Blooded's claws, other natural weapons, and any breath weapons they have deal `+1k1` damage, gain +2 Pen, and the damage type is changed to X.

### Blood of Io

The Chosen of Io may regain their Breath more quickly by consuming valuable objects like gems and coins. They may spend a Full Action during combat to gulp down a handful of precious metal and wealth to regain 2 points of Breath.

> **Optional Rule:** The character can be assumed to begin play with a number of these handfuls of wealth equal to their Wealth background. Acquiring more is a TN 15 Wealth test for a single handful, TN 20 for two handfuls, and TN 25 for four handfuls. It is generally ill-advised to attract attention to possession of large amounts of loose, unsecured loot. Making such a purchase more than once every month or two in any particular metropolis risks attracting special attention or activating Hindrances like Enemy, Hunted, and Grim Servant O' Death.

### Blood of Tiamat

Once per day, the Dragon Blooded may perform an hour long ritual that allows them to change their Blood Quickening.

> **Optional Rule:** The SM may want to limit this asset. Consider excluding the attribute change from Blood Quickening when switching. The Adamic Dragon quickenings should be off limits to this ability.

### Double Dragon

You have the bloodline of not just one, but two dragons (or more, if you're a dragonborn - in which case you are some kind of triple dragon at this point) flowing through your veins. Choose an additional Blood Quickening (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, or Wood). You gain the benefits of that Blood Quickening, with the exception of that Blood Quickening's characteristic bonus.

Silence is the end of life, the rotting of wood, and the wraiths who can command its power cause the spirits that hover around them to possess the bodies of the recently deceased, zombies springing up from the death around them in a mockery of birth. > **Play Tip:** Individual Walking Dead are weak on their own. The real power of this ability comes from building a horde—organize your zombies into minion squads as your Synergy grows to get meaningful combat impact.

If they have two Checks on the Test they lose their entire turn.
