# Appendices and Advice

---

> **Clarification:** Tandem Charge is a vehicle-only weapon property, but when used against a person, the "extra critical hits" effect means that four wounds also inflicts a crit-4 to the rolled body location. Being hit with an anti-tank missile is seriously bad for your health.

> **Clarification:** The grenade launcher in the main book should presumably have this property as well — it was likely an oversight that it wasn't included.
