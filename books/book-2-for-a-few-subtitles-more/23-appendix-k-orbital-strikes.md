# Appendix K: Orbital Strikes

## How to Survive an Orbital Strike

Don't piss off people with big space guns.
**Actually Useful Stuff**

So your players decided the best thing to do was to take off and nuke a city from orbit, or
maybe there's just one person in the city they really don't like and they don't care about
collateral damage. Your question now is "Who lived, and just how pissed off are they?". Well
we've tried to do some calculations for you on the
'who' part of that.
First, who is how tough?
We charted a Level/Size breakdown and chose an
'average' Resilience-by-Level which turned out to match a Size 4 creature with no special Resilience
enhancements.
Second, how many of them are there?
Assumption ? 70% per Level, therefore 70% of the population is Level 1, then 70% of the
remaining 30% is Level 2, etc.
Third, how many hit points do they have?
Assumption ? 70% per dot > 1, therefore 70% have Con + Will of 4, then 70% of the remaining
30% have a Con + Will of 6, etc.
We aren't considering the people with 1s in Constitution and Willpower. The usual setting
of DUNGEONS THE DRAGONING generally indicates that they're going to be an insignificant
fraction of the population. It helps that we do a lot of rounding, we just assume they're lost in all that.
Fourth, rounding and adding happens, we multiply HP by Resilience to get a damage
threshold. This gives us how much damage it takes to run someone out of hit points and what
fraction of the population these people are.
At this point we should note that Exalts, by virtue of their doubled hit points are in the
top 10% of the population, if not the top 1%.
You can use this chart to find the chance that any particular person survived a
particular bombardment. Multiply their hit points by their Resilience, rounding up or down
based on armor, feats, traits, and your estimation of their circumstances. Check your damage
amount from the bombardment. If it's in the range of the numbers you can roll percentage
dice for their survival; if the bombardment damage higher than that in the 10% column
then you can give them a 1% chance to survive
(that's being nice).
These numbers are for non-exalts and normal people. So not stuff that doesn't
bleed, stun, fatigue, have double HP or inherent armor, has Con/Will/Level stats
at 6+ dots, has Resilience or Size at 7+, gets Hero Points, etc. We also aren't
considering being in a building, under cover, behind a car, etc., because a lot
of that stuff is going to fall down, explode, and catch fire. We just assume
that the resulting wreckage kills as many people as being under cover saves.
