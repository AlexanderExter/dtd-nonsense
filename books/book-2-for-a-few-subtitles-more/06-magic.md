# Chapter VI: Magic

## Abjuration

### Voidskin

**Abjuration 1**
**Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic
**Duration:** One Scene

You become immune to the ravages of space. While the spell is active it provides you with all the air you need to breathe and protects you against pressure and radiation. Voidskin also works as a diving suit, Dune still-suit, lava walking suit, hazmat suit, or any other sort of extreme environment survival suit.

### Endure Elements

**Abjuration 2**
**Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** 24 hours

You may comfortably exist in most natural environments. You don't suffer penalties to your actions from weather conditions, and you're comfortable even in very hot or cold weather. The spell also halves damage from the appropriate energy sources (fire, lasers, plasma, electricity, cold, etc., but NOT acid, disintegration, warp vortexes, psychic pain, etc.) even if they are magical or spell effects. Apply this halving after the damage is reduced by Armor or Aura.

### Globe of Invulnerability

**Abjuration 3**
**Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** One Scene

The caster of the spell becomes immune to spells of Level 1 and 2. The caster cannot be targeted by such spells, and if caught in the area of effect of such a spell simply ignores its effect. However, they are not immune to spells that don't directly affect them - their attacks can still be parried with Shield, enemies under the effect of Boon still get their bonus against the caster, and so forth.

### Ruby Ray

**Abjuration 4**
**Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Reaction Action or Half Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Touch
**Duration:** Instant

A Ruby Ray is effectively a more powerful and limited form of Dispel. It can be used in one of two ways - as a Reaction to a spell, or to set up a shield in advance. When used as Half Action to shield someone it reflects the next spell cast at the shielded target before the end of the Ruby Ray caster's next turn. It only reflects spells that target a single target, not those that effect everyone in an area or a burst effect like Energy Ball. If it is used as a Reaction Action then it can only protect the spell caster. The reflected spell rebounds to strike the original spell caster if they are within 200m, otherwise the spell dissipates harmlessly.

### Disjunction

**Abjuration 5**
**Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Reaction Action
**Keywords:** Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

This spell functions as Dispel with a 500m range, but if you succeed on the counter-spelling attempt, the target cannot cast spells until the end of your next turn, plus an additional turn per Raise on the Focus Power test. If you get five or more raises on the Focus Power test and successfully dispel the opponent's spell then they may not cast any more spells until the end of the scene.

## Conjuration

### Web

**Conjuration 1**
**Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Verbal, Somatic, Material, Saving Throw
**Duration:** Instant

When you cast Web, choose a point within 25m. All targets within 10m of the point save Arcana + Dexterity or they are Immobilized for a number of rounds equal to your Caster Level. Creatures entering the area do not get a saving throw. Stuck creatures may test Strength or Size against the Focus Power Test to move through at half speed.

Material: Cobwebs or sticky candy

### Obscuring Fog

**Conjuration 2**
**Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic, Combo-OK
**Duration:** Instant

You create a bank of fog, up to three 5m x 5m x 5m cubes per Caster Level. Within this bank of fog, visibility is reduced to 1m. The fog dissipates at a normal rate, vanishing in about five minutes in normal conditions, less if there's wind, bright sunlight, and so forth.

### Cloudkill

**Conjuration 3**
**Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
**Duration:** Instant

You create a cloud of acidic poison gas, up to one 5m x 5m x 5m cube per Caster Level. Within this cloud of gas visibility is reduced to 2m. The cloud deals magical E type damage with rolled dice equal to the caster's Level plus their dots in Conjuration when they cast the spell, and kept dice equal to the caster's Level (Level + Conjuration, keep Level). The damage is dealt every time someone starts or ends their turn in the cloud. Breathing inside the cloud requires a Constitution vs. 15 test or take a wound as though from a Toxic weapon (crits go to the gizzards). The cloud lasts 3 minutes plus another minute per Raise on the Focus Power test.

### Black Blade of Disaster

**Conjuration 4**
**Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
**Duration:** One Scene

This spell creates a dimensional rift to a zerospace of utter destruction, a realm of void and emptiness so hungry that it has a kind of terrible anti-life. The rift takes the form of a large sword that functions as a Power Sword. It does an additional `+1k0` damage per Raise on the Focus Power test and has the following additional properties: Balanced, Toxic, and Shocking. The sword ignores Armor, its damage is reduced by Aura, but that Aura is reduced by the sword's penetration rating. The caster is automatically proficient with the blade and may choose to use their dots in Arcana in place of their Level for any tests made with the sword (attacks, parries, etc.). If the blade leaves the hands of the caster it dissipates at the start or end of the caster's turn, or as soon as the caster wants it to, whichever comes first.

### Incendiary Cloud

**Conjuration 5**
**Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
**Duration:** Instant

You create a roiling cloud of fire. Treat this as the Obscuring Fog spell except as follows: There are 5 cubes per Conjuration dot and the cloud is unaffected by anything that would normally dissipate the Obscuring Fog clouds. The cloud does magical E damage with rolled dice equal to the caster's Level plus their dots in Conjuration at the time of the Focus Power test and kept dice equal to the caster's dots in Conjuration at the time of the Focus Power test (Level + Conjuration, keep Conjuration). The damage is done when anything starts or ends its turn in the cloud and each time it enters the cloud. In addition the cloud sets everything On Fire at the end of the caster's turn. Even stuff that can't be set on fire or is unaffected by being on fire is on fire. The cloud lasts one minute plus one additional minute per Raise on the Focus Power test.

## Divination

### Detect Thoughts

**Divination 1**
**Test:** Divination + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Subtle, Combo-OK, Mind-Affecting
**Duration:** One Round

You are able to detect surface thoughts and get a general idea of a person's emotions using this spell. If your Focus Power test exceeds the target's Mental Defense you can detect more specific thoughts. If you get a number of Raises equal to the target's Willpower, you can get the answer to a single question - assuming the target knows the answer. If he doesn't, or is misinformed, you'll just get the same wrong answer that he has.

### Arcane Eye

**Divination 2**
**Test:** Divination + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Material, Verbal, Non-Combat
**Duration:** One hour per Level, plus one hour per Raise on the Focus Power test.

You create a small magical sensor by turning an eyeball invisible and making it fly around. It flies at Speed 8 and can go any distance from you. The sensor has visual capabilities equal to the creature from which it came. Bionic eyes won't work for this spell. The eyeball is used up and destroyed when the spell ends. If it takes any damage, it is destroyed.

Material: An eye from any creature.

### Legend Lore

**Divination 3**
**Test:** Divination + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Non-Combat, Somatic, Touch
**Duration:** Instant

By touching an item you gain some insight into its history. You learn its general history, all of its abilities, and any famous owners it has had. If you get three or more raises on the Focus Power test you may see into the past with this spell. You may see historical events surrounding the object as if you were there. You may choose specific important events (if you know something about them), a date and time, or simply specify a certain amount of time in the past.

### Foresight

**Divination 4**
**Test:** Divination + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal, Non-Combat
**Duration:** Instant (E)

You gain insight into the near future. Upon successfully casting this spell, you gain a vague idea of the next significant threat you will probably face. While the SM is not required to tell you what it is, she should at least give you an idea of how to prepare for it. When you encounter this danger (or manage to avoid it - no SM is perfect and the future is always in flux) the Foresight spell triggers again and lets you know that the event has occurred or been prevented. If the triggering event is a combat then you cannot be surprised and automatically act before everyone else (i.e. you win initiative) in addition to the other effects of this spell. You may only have one instance of this spells in effect at a time.

### True Knowledge

**Divination 5**
**Test:** Divination + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal, Focus
**Duration:** One round per level + one round per Raise in the Focus Power test.

Any action that involves rolling a skill or attribute adds additional rolled dice equal to the number of dots of Divination used in the Focus Power test. For rolls that do not get those additional dice (such as Alignment tests and Perils of the Warp rolls) you roll twice and use the better result. You count as always having at least one dot in all attributes and skills.

Focus: A very large and unabridged encyclopedia of everything. An encyclopedia loaded on a high quality data-slate is preferred by some younger sorcerers but traditionalists like the humongous old books. An encyclopedia that is linked to through a digital communications network is sufficient as long as the data connection is fast, stable, completely uninterrupted, and does not have false information injected by a hack.

## Enchantment

### Shock and Awe

**Enchantment 1**
**Test:** Enchantment + Charisma
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

The sorcerer picks a point within 20m. Everyone within 10m of that point loses 5 from their initiative score. If they have already acted this round and this change in the initiative order would give them an additional turn, they skip that turn.

### Confusion

**Enchantment 2**
**Test:** Enchantment + Charisma
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Verbal, Combo-OK, Mind-Affecting
**Duration:** One Scene

The chosen target who can hear this spell makes an Arcana + Intelligence saving throw. If it is not successful, at the beginning of each of his turns, roll `1d10`. On a roll of 1-4, the target hurts himself (taking 1 Wound). On a roll of 5-6, the target does nothing. On 7-9, the target may act normally. If the target rolls a 10, the spell is broken. A target cannot be under the effects of more than one confusion effect at once. A megaphone is suggested to maximize the range.

### Instill Desire

**Enchantment 3**
**Test:** Enchantment + Charisma
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Subtle, Non-Combat, Verbal, Mind-Affecting, Language-Dependent
**Duration:** Concentration (Half Action)

A target within 30m must save Arcana + Composure or desperately want the thing that the caster names. The target gains an extra raise on the save if what they are told to desire goes against their personality or Alignment. What they want can be a thing, a person, an action, or just about anything a person could wish for. The target's immediate goal will change to be getting what they want. If they want a thing they may try to buy or steal it. A person may be interacted with or abducted. If they want safety they may flee or surrender. If they want someone dead they will usually attack. The method of action is left up to the target, but they act to fulfill their desire almost immediately.

### Enchant an Item

**Enchantment 4**
**Test:** Enchantment + Charisma
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic, Focus, Non-Combat
**Duration:** Indefinite (E)

The caster enchants an object with powerful magic that can have a variety of effects. The caster defines a physical interaction with the object (eat the apple, open the book, draw the gun) and the next person to perform that action with the enchanted object is affected by the spell. The effect may be any Enchantment spell effect or any non-instant self or touch range spell of first through third level (Geas: Sleep forever and Transformation: mouse are traditional). Make the Focus Power test for the effect when this spell is cast, but all effects and side effect (like Psychic Phenomena rolls) occur when the enchantment is triggered. The target may save Arcana + Willpower and the effect's usual save to resist the effect. The caster may only have as many enchanted items at one time as they have dots in Enchantment and any Resource points or Hero points used in the enchanting spell cannot be regained until after the spell has been expended.

### Amnesia

**Enchantment 5**
**Test:** Enchantment + Charisma
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Non-Combat, Verbal, Somatic, Touch, Language-Dependent, Mind-Affecting
**Duration:** Instant

You may erase and rewrite a target's memories.
If the target fails an Arcana + Intelligence saving throw the target's memories of an event, chosen by the caster, are erased. The caster may tell the target in as much or as little detail as they desire what happened, after which the target will remember what the caster told them, filling in any gaps by themselves. If the target gets two checks on their save the caster can rewrite minor aspects of their personality (favorite ice cream, willingness to take bribes). If the target gets four checks on their save then the caster can rewrite their Alignment with all the associated drama that involves. An alternate use of this spell is to add or subtract traumatic events to the target's memory. In this case the caster may add or subtract Insanity Points equal to the number of Raises on the Focus Power test.

## Evocation

### Battering Ram

**Evocation 1**
**Test:** Evocation + Charisma
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Combo-OK, Attack, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

One target within 30m saves Arcana + Strength or else they are pushed 2m, and another 2m for each check on save. If the target is pushed back further than it's Speed, it is knocked prone. Flying targets are pushed twice as far but not knocked prone. The push can be in any direction but is not strong enough to cause harm on its own (that's what the spiked pit is for, falling).

### Defenestration

**Evocation 2**
**Test:** Evocation + Charisma
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Combo-OK, Attack, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

The caster chooses one target within 100m and a wall within 5m of the target. The target is thrown violently into the wall, and both he and the wall take `4k2` damage +2 per Raise on the Focus Power test. If the damage is twice wall's AP, or more, the target is thrown through the wall instead, automatically destroying a section of the wall.

### Lightning Ring

**Evocation 3**
**Test:** Evocation + Charisma
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Combo-OK, Ranged Attack, Verbal
**Duration:** One Scene

The sorcerer creates an aura that deals Xk2 damage to everyone in melee range at the end of her turn. X is equal to the caster's Level plus the number of Raises she had on the Focus Power test. As a Half Action the caster may fire a bolt of lightning from the ring, making a ranged attack against an enemy within 40m that deals `5k3` magic E damage. Every time she fires a bolt from the ring, X is reduced by 1. If the caster so wishes she may replace the Ballistics attack with an Arcana + Dexterity saving throw.

### Telekinesis

**Evocation 4**
**Test:** Evocation + Charisma
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Somatic
**Duration:** 5 Rounds + Concentration (Half)

Something within 60m of the caster is pushed or pulled in a direction chosen by the sorcerer for a distance of 4m plus an additional 4m per check on the saving throw. Creatures and large or fixed objects may save Arcana + Strength or Size to remain unmoved, the save is repeated every time the sorcerer attempts to move them. If an object is moved more than 90m away from caster it is freed from the effect. The spell lasts at five rounds plus as long as the caster keeps concentrating on the spell. The sorcerer may change the target of this spell when they concentrate on it. A falling target being pushed down adds extra falling damage at `1k1` for every 4m of push. When pushing up the spell automatically overcomes the local gravitational pull (this saves some math) and pushes half the distance. The sorcerer can throw things at targets with a non-proficient Ballistics test. Such objects do `0k2` damage plus rolled dice equal to their Size.

### Reality Maelstrom

**Evocation 5**
**Test:** Evocation + Charisma
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

The caster chooses a spot within 50m. Everyone within 30m of that point rolls (individually) on the Psychic Phenomena tables with a +5 on the roll for every Raise on the Focus Power test. The caster also makes a roll on the Perils of the Warp table and applies that effect to everything in the affected area. An alternate use of this spell is for the caster to create a Warp Vortex `1d10` meters in diameter at a point within 50m. The vortex will then move 2 to 20 meters (`2d10`) in a semi-random direction (roll twice and choose a direction that puts the Vortex through or closer to people). It causes `10k7` magic damage (use the Energy critical hit chart) to everything it goes through or past. Victims of the Vortex may attempt an Acrobatics + Dexterity test as a Reaction against TN 35 to avoid the Vortex. The Vortex will last `1d10` rounds plus a number of rounds equal to the number of the caster's dots in Evocation used for the Focus Power test.

## Healing

### Shield Other

**Healing 1**
**Test:** Healing + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Focus, Touch, Somatic, Verbal, Non-Combat
**Duration:** One Day

As you cast this spell, touch a target. For the next 24 hours, whenever the target would lose Hit Points, you may lose that many Hit Points instead.

Focus: A small doll.

### Death Ward

**Healing 2**
**Test:** Healing + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Five Full Actions
**Keywords:** Touch, Somatic, Verbal, Non-Combat
**Duration:** One Day

The touched target is warded against death. The next time the target would die during the duration of the effect, he survives. This does not protect against continuous effects that would kill someone, such as being dropped into the Astral Sea unprotected, walking out of an airlock on Venus in your underwear, or falling into molten lava.

### Atonement

**Healing 3**
**Test:** Healing + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Touch, Somatic, Verbal, Non-Combat, Material
**Duration:** Instant

Atonement is used to heal some of the woes brought on by Degeneration. On a successful casting of Atonement, the degeneration at the target's highest Devotion level is removed (For example, if a target has Degenerations at Devotion 5, 4, and 3, this would remove the Degeneration at Devotion 5). Once you have benefited from Atonement, you cannot do so again until you raise your Devotion at least one point. As an alternate use of this spell you can cast it as a week long series of rituals with a target number equal to the target creature's Insanity score that will remove Insanity Points equal to the target's Devotion minus `1d5`.

### Heal

**Healing 4**
**Test:** Healing + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Touch, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

The target is healed for one Hit Point plus another Hit Point for every Raise on the Focus Power test. The caster may spend as many Resource Points as they wish after making the Focus Power test and the target is healed two Hit Points for every Resource Point spent. This spell removes all poisons, toxins, drug effects, and the conditions; blinded, dazzled, deafened, bleeding, diseased, on fire, lost eye(s), immobilized, stunned, suffocating, and unconscious. It also removes levels of Fatigue equal to the caster's Level.

### Divine Power

**Healing 5**
**Test:** Healing + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal, Focus
**Duration:** One Scene

You are filled with the power of the gods. You gain +2 Strength, +2 Size, +4 Static Defense, and +1 Resilience. You regenerate 1 Hit Point each round at the start of your turn, gain Aura equal to the dots in Healing that you used for the Focus Power test, you have an additional Reaction each turn, and you gain the Stuff of Nightmares trait. If the focus of this spell is damaged or destroyed you have to make an immediate Alignment test. The factored attributes are +2 Resilience, no change in Static Defense, and `+2k0` on Strength tests and melee damage.

Focus: Your holy symbol, proudly displayed. Traditional

## Illusion

### Blur

**Illusion 1**
**Test:** Illusion + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Combo-OK, Somatic
**Duration:** Concentration (Reaction)

You can distort your own image, making it more difficult to hit you. Your Static Defense increases by 3 and you gain another +2 for each raise on the Focus Power test. In addition it is difficult to identify you, observers have a penalty of `-2k1` to perceive details about you beyond size, color, and general armament.

### Illusory Object

**Illusion 2**
**Test:** Illusion + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** See text
**Keywords:** Somatic, Material, Non-Combat
**Duration:** Indefinite, see text.

This spell creates a single object that appears real to all senses. A book, gun, corpse, reflecting mirror, etc. Things with senses will treat the object as real; it appears on video feeds, robots & zombies treat it as real. You can't fool the physical laws of the universe though, loaded bullets fall through a gun and fake parachutes don't help. It takes as long to cast the spell as it takes to paint or draw the object. Minutes for a simple or hand held items, hours for a large or complicated items. The object may have moving parts but that makes it take at least `1d5`+1 hours, possibly much more for something like a novel that can be read. The casting must be uninterrupted, otherwise the illusion is incomplete. Creatures directly interacting with the illusion (reading the book, eating the food) test Arcana + Intelligence versus the higher of the Focus Power test or a Craft test. Impossible interactions reveal the nature of the object instantly; being pushed through an illusion of a wall or turning on a flashlight (no light). Partially complete illusions are revealed if the incomplete portions are obvious. Using the item to harm someone reveals it as an illusion. The illusion will last for one day per level of the caster and then for as long as someone continues to perceive it. Undead and androids are useful for maximizing the duration of the spell.

Material: Paints, crayons, pencils, perfumes, and sound recordings.

### Improved Image

**Illusion 3**
**Test:** Illusion + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic
**Duration:** Concentration (Half)

Treat this as the Image spell with the following differences. The area that the illusion covers can be up to 5m x 5m x 5m, includes full animation, and has sound up to, and including, intelligible speech. When the caster concentrates on the spell to maintain it she can also cause it to move up to 20m to any place within 200m of her. The spell fades as soon as the caster fails to concentrate on maintaining it during her turn.

### Mislead

**Illusion 4**
**Test:** Illusion + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Combo-OK, Subtle, Somatic
**Duration:** One round per dot of Illusion plus another round per Raise on the Focus Power test.

When you cast this spell, you create an image of yourself and turn invisible at the same time. You may then move up to your speed, leaving the image behind. To all observers, it appears as though nothing happened at all. When you make an attack or your image is successfully attacked, the spell ends, revealing your real location. You may spend a Reaction Action to have the illusion move, react, dodge, appear to take damage, or speak as though the caster standing there. The illusionary duplicate exists in all five senses and it possesses animations and reactions sufficient to fool normal observation.

### Weird

**Illusion 5**
**Test:** Illusion + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Verbal, Attack, Saving Throw
**Duration:** Concentration (Reaction) + 5 rounds.

Terrible hallucinations fill an area 20m in radius around a point within 200m of the caster. In the area vision is limited to 5m and all Perception test results are halved. Everyone in or entering the area rolls a saving throw using Arcana + Composure. On a success they have to make a Fear(3) test. On a failure the victim rolls on the Shock table at +2 per check on the saving throw, and they are considered blind and deaf while in the area of effect. The fear and shock effects are considered to be Mind-Affecting.

### False Life

**Necromancy 1**
**Test:** Necromancy + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** 24 Hours

The caster gains one temporary Hit Point, plus one for every two raises on the Focus Power test. These temporary Hit Points are lost first and do not stack with themselves or other sources of temporary Hit Points.

### Burning Blood

**Necromancy 2**
**Test:** Necromancy + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Somatic
**Duration:** One round per level

You taint a living creature's blood with a hot, corrosive infusion. This spell has no effect on creatures without blood, like robots, Modrons, elementals, ghosts, or the undead. The caster picks a target within 10m per Level, who must save Arcana + Constitution. If the target fails it loses 1 hit point at the end of each of its turns. The effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the caster's dots in Necromancy used in the Focus Power test.

### Horrid Wilting

**Necromancy 3**
**Test:** Necromancy + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

This spell drains the moisture from the body of every creature except the caster within 20m, giving them a level of Fatigue. It also kills a normal plant life within that area, causing it to wither and dry out. Creatures that do not have, or do not depend, on internal fluids are unaffected. At the SM's option certain extraordinarily damp creatures like slugs and jellies may take a Wound for every Raise on the Focus Power test.

### Avasculate

**Necromancy 4**
**Test:** Necromancy + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** Instant

Choose one target creature within 30m that possesses a heart. The target of this spell makes a saving throw with Arcana + Constitution. If the saving throw fails, the target loses half of his remaining Hit Points, rounded up.

### Create Greater Undead

**Necromancy 5**
**Test:** Necromancy + Intelligence
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Three Full Actions
**Keywords:** Non-Combat, Verbal, Somatic, Focus
**Duration:** Instant

One touched corpse becomes an improved version of the usual animated corpse. The new undead loses any ability to cast spells, Resource or Hero Points, naturally heal or regenerate, and all biological functions like reproduction or producing venom. It has Intelligence and Fellowship ratings of zero. The creature gains the Undead, Dark-Sight, and Mindless traits. It is otherwise a fully functional creature at full Hit Points that obeys the caster's verbal commands. This spell cannot be cast on things that were never alive (robots), are a form of un-life (Modrons), are already undead (vampires), or have a Size of more than 10.

Focus: A large black semi-precious stone carved in the form of a brain and placed in the empty cranium of the dead creature.

## Transmutation

### Blood Wind

**Transmutation 1**
**Test:** Transmutation + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 15
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Material, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** One Scene

You may use your natural attacks at range. They gain the Reach property and can hit targets at a range up to 5m per level of the caster.

Material: A drop of the caster's blood

### Technological Transformation

**Transmutation 2**
**Test:** Transmutation + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 20
**Action:** Half Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Combo-OK, Touch
**Duration:** One Scene

After casting this spell the sorcerer may change an object that they touch, or that touches them, before the end of their next turn. If the object is a technological device, made from plastic, alloys, or advanced materials it is changed into a version made from wood, stone, or a natural material. This means that it stops working properly and is often easier to break. A high quality sculpture of some technological object made from a natural material can become a functioning device. A melee attack or Parry may be required to tag an object in the hands of an enemy. Technological armors are reduced in effectiveness to the equivalent form of primitive armor, and vice versa. ### Embody Spirit

**Transmutation 3**
**Test:** Transmutation + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 25
**Action:** Full Action
**Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal, Focus, Material
**Duration:** Concentration (Half or Reaction)

The Embody Spirit spell transforms the materials into a vessel for a local spirit to aid the caster. Such spirits are at least indifferent if they aren't friendly. The spirit takes the form indicated by the focus and materials, often an Elemental. The spirit speaks the universal spirit language and can communicate with the Caster. It can be engaged in social combat. It is normally helpful as long as the caster concentrates on the spell as a Reaction. If the spirit is engaging in combat the caster must use a Half Action to maintain the spell. If the caster releases concentration during combat the spirit may stick around and inflict violence on the caster and anything else it takes offense to. Fire spirits take offense to things that are not burning.

Focus: A representation of the spirit.
Materials: Sufficient material for the spirit to take physical form.

### Control Weather

**Transmutation 4**
**Test:** Transmutation + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 30
**Action:** Full Action/10 Minutes
**Keywords:** Non-Combat, Somatic, Verbal
**Duration:** One day

You change the weather in the local area, at least out to the horizon. It takes 10 minutes to cast the spell and an additional 10 minutes for the effects to manifest. You can call forth weather appropriate to the climate and season of the area you are in. The SM has final say over the particular weather condition. You control the general tendencies of the weather, such as the direction and intensity of the wind. You cannot control specific applications of the weather where lightning strikes, for example, or the exact path of a tornado.

### Animate Elemental

**Transmutation 5**
**Test:** Transmutation + Wisdom
**Target Number:** 35
**Action:** Full Action/One Hour
**Keywords:** Non-Combat, Verbal, Material
**Duration:** Permanent

You transform raw materials into a durable and loyal elemental servant and ally. Use an Elemental, adding +1 to any attribute per Raise on the Focus Power test. For every three raises add +1 to the elemental's Size, and for every five raises add +1 to its Level and +1 to all of its skills. A caster can have a number of loyal elementals in their service equal to their Fellowship + Composure. They speak the spirit language and social combat or other forms of persuasion can be used on them. If there are more than six of them they will form a union to agitate for better working conditions and compensation. It's an odd quirk of elemental spirits.

Material: Sufficient quantities of the materials needed to make up their bodies.
