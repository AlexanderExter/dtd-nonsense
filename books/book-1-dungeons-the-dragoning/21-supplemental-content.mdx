# Supplemental Content (Book 2)

The following additional content is found in _For a Few Subtitles More_ (Book 2) and supplements the rules in this book:

| Appendix | Topic                  | Description                                                             |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D        | Tracking               | Wilderness tracking rules and pursuit mechanics                         |
| F        | Vehicles               | Quick & dirty vehicle building rules and vehicle reference tables       |
| G        | Spelljamming           | Expanded rules for space travel, navigation, and spelljammer operations |
| H        | Zero-G Combat          | Specialized rules for combat in zero-gravity environments               |
| I        | Character Optimization | Tips and strategies for effective character builds                      |
| K        | Orbital Strikes        | Rules for calling in and resolving orbital bombardment                  |
| M        | Character Stubs        | Pre-built character templates for quick play                            |
| O        | Last Words             | Closing design remarks                                                  |

Additionally, Book 2 contains expanded content for: new races and exaltations (Chapters I-IV), additional classes (Chapter V), new feats and assets (Chapter VI), new spells across all schools (Chapter VII), new sword schools and gun katas (Chapters VIII-IX), additional equipment (Chapter X), expanded alignment pantheons (Chapter XI), vehicle building and ship construction (Chapters XII-XIII), and reference sheets (Chapter XIV).
