Steps

    Name your character and jot down a concept. No mechanics here — just who they are.

    Your race determines Size, characteristic bonuses, skill bonuses, and a racial power.

    Your Exaltation grants supernatural powers, a Power Stat, and a Resource pool. Mortals gain bonus dots instead.

    Assign priority groups (6 / 4 / 2 bonus dots) to Physical, Social, and Mental, then distribute within each group. All characteristics start at 1. No characteristic may exceed 4 in this step (racial bonuses can raise it further).

    Assign priority groups (8 / 6 / 4 dots) to Physical, Social, and Mental skill groups, then distribute dots. No skill may exceed 3 dots in this step.

    Distribute 7 free dots among backgrounds (max 3 per background). Dots 4 and 5 cost 100 XP each.

    Budget: 7 / 7 dots remaining

    Choose a deity or remain Unaligned. Your alignment determines moral code and grants certain powers.

    Choose your starting class (first is free, additional cost 100 XP each). Only Tier 1 classes are available at character creation.

    Select feats (100 XP each). Class mandatory feats are auto-included.

    Assets & Hindrances

    Assets cost 100 XP each. Hindrances grant bonus XP. These are commonly taken during character creation.

    Choose one of five starting equipment packages (Earth, Air, Fire, Water, Void), then resolve any choices within it.
