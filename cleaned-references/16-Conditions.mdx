:::note[Quick Reference]
Conditions are debilitating status effects applied during [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx), Critical Effects, environmental hazards, or [Social Combat](./15-Social-Combat.mdx) (Fear). Most conditions impose dice penalties or restrict actions. Multiple conditions can **compound** to worsen a character's wound state. Fear and Mental Trauma have their own track separate from physical injury.
:::

## Conditions Quick-Reference

| Condition | Effect | Duration | Removal |
| --------- | ------ | -------- | ------- |
| **Amputation** | Lose limb; treated as Heavily Wounded | Permanent | Cybernetics, healing magic, regeneration |
| **Blinded** | −`4k0` physical Tests; Athletics + Dex TN 15 to move | Until healed | Healing, spell, cybernetics |
| **Blood Loss** | Roll `1d10` each Round; on a 1, character dies | Until treated | Medicae TN 20 (TN 30 if active); magical healing |
| **Burning** | 1 HP + 1 Fatigue/Round (Energy damage) | Until extinguished | Dex Test TN 15 (Full Action, drop Prone) |
| **Crippled** | Limb unusable (arm = no attacks; leg = crawl only) | Until healed | Medical attention, magic |
| **Dazzled** | −`1k0` all Tests; −`2k0` sight-based Tests | Varies | Typically short-duration |
| **Deafened** | −`2k0` all Tests; auto-fail hearing Tests | Until healed | Healing, cybernetics |
| **Fatigue** | −`1k0` all Tests; unconscious if levels > Constitution | Until rested | 1 level/hour of rest |
| **Frightened** | Must flee; if unable: −`2k0`, no attacks/magic/stunts | End of encounter | Escape source of fear |
| **Helpless** | Auto-hit; damage rolled twice and added | While condition persists | Remove underlying cause |
| **Pinned** | Cannot move or act (only Full Defense); can speak | Until unpinned | Full Defense, suppression ends |
| **Prone** | −`2k0` ranged attacks vs. you; +`2k0` melee vs. you; −`2k0` to your attacks | Until you Stand | Stand action (Half Action) |
| **Stunned** | No Reactions; only action is Move at half rate | Varies (rounds) | Hero Point, duration expires |
| **Toxic** | Con Test (TN 15) or suffer 1 additional wound to Gizzards | After each hit | Antidote; immunity (Dryad, etc.) |

## Condition Descriptions

### Amputation

Character loses a body part and is treated as **Heavily Wounded** regardless of current wound state. All healing received must be applied to the amputation first. Only after stabilization can other wounds heal. Replaceable with cybernetics, healing magic, or regeneration — otherwise permanent.

### Blinded

Total inability to see. −`4k0` to physical Tests (no penalty to social Tests like Intimidation or Persuasion). Athletics + Dexterity Test (TN 15) required to move faster than half rate without tripping.

### Blood Loss

Result of Critical Damage. Each Round, roll `1d10`. On a roll of **1**, the character **dies**. A conscious character may attempt a **TN 20 Medicae Test** each Round to staunch the bleeding. If also engaging in strenuous activity (running, fighting), the TN increases to **30**. Another character may attempt the Test if the victim is unconscious or unable.

### Burning

A character on fire loses **1 Hit Point** and gains **1 level of Fatigue** each round. All fire damage counts as Energy damage for Critical Effects — if hit location is unspecified, use Body.

A burning character may try to extinguish the flames by dropping Prone and making a **TN 15 Dexterity Test** as a Full Action. The SM may adjust the TN based on environment and whether allies help.

### Combined Penalty

Multiple conditions compound. If you're already Lightly Wounded from one condition and receive another condition that would also make you Lightly Wounded, you become **Heavily Wounded**. Only applies to new/current conditions — pre-existing injuries don't combine with fresh ones.

### Crippled

A limb is damaged and unusable:

- **Arm** — cannot attack with it
- **Leg** — fall Prone, can only crawl (1m per Half Action)
- **Vital organ** — specific effects (may require tech/magic to survive)

### Dazzled

Dazed and confused. −`1k0` to all Tests, additional −`1k0` (total −`2k0`) to sight-based Tests.

### Deafened

Total inability to hear. −`2k0` to all Tests. Automatically fail any hearing-based Tests (SM discretion).

### Fatigue

Non-lethal exhaustion measured in levels. While at any Fatigue: −`1k0` to all Tests. If Fatigue levels exceed Constitution: collapse unconscious for 10 − Constitution hours, then Fatigue resets to Constitution. **Recovery:** 1 level per hour of rest.

### Frightened

Must flee the source of fear. If escape is impossible: cannot attack, use magic, or stunt; −`2k0` to all Tests. Lasts for the remainder of the encounter.

### Helpless

Cannot take any actions. Attacks against a Helpless target automatically hit. Damage is rolled **twice and added together**.

### Pinned

Cannot move or take any Actions/Reactions except **Full Defense** (which helps avoid further pinning). May still speak. Free actions limited to speech.

### Prone

- Melee attacks against you: +`2k0`
- Ranged attacks against you: −`2k0`
- Your attack rolls: −`2k0`
- Movement: crawl at half rate only
- **Stand up:** Half Action (Provokes)

### Stunned

Cannot take Reactions. Only action permitted: Move at half rate. Duration varies by source.

### Toxic

After taking damage from a Toxic weapon, the target must make a **Constitution Test** (TN 15 for Average quality; TN 10 for Poor, TN 20 for Good, TN 25 for Best) or suffer **one additional wound to the Gizzards**. Removed by antidotes or immunity (e.g., Dryad racial power).

## Fear and Insanity

### Fear Resistance

Characters with Fear resistance subtract **5 per point** from mental trauma damage and gain +`2k0` on mental-related Tests to resist horrors. Fear resistance comes from:

- Increasing Dark alignment
- Certain Feats
- Exposure to horrors (desensitization)
- Becoming a horror yourself (some undead)

### The Shock Table

When faced with terror, Test **Willpower + Composure** or suffer mental trauma:

| d10 | Trauma | Situation |
| --- | ------ | --------- |
| 1 | `1d10` | Surprised, unexpected encounter |
| 2 | `1d10` | Startled by something terrifying |
| 3 | `1d10`+1 | Witchery! |
| 4 | `1d10`+2 | Gore and viscera |
| 5 | `1d10`+3 | Severe injury to self or ally |
| 6 | `1d10`+4 | A minor warp entity manifests |
| 7 | `2d10` | A major warp entity manifests |
| 8 | `2d10`+2 | Witness a massacre or destroyed city |
| 9 | `2d10`+4 | Witness soul-destroying horror |
| 10+ | `3d10` | Unspeakable nightmare beyond mortal comprehension |

## Mental Trauma

The Trauma track starts at 0 with no upper limit. Passing thresholds triggers Mental Disorders:

| Threshold | Effect |
| --------- | ------ |
| 40 | Gain 1 **Minor** Disorder |
| 60 | Gain 1 **Severe** Disorder |
| 80 | Gain 1 **Acute** Disorder |
| 100 | Character must retire (too broken to function) |

### Disorder Severity

- **Minor** — manifests under stress. Example: −`1k0` to social Tests with certain people, brief catatonia in specific situations.
- **Severe** — significant life impact. Example: obsessive behavior, debilitating phobia, violent personality shifts.
- **Acute** — debilitating. Great difficulty functioning in society. Dangerous compulsions, complete loss of touch with reality.

## Therapy

A character reduces Mental Trauma through professional therapy:

1. **Treatment Period** — attend sessions (2×/week minimum) for `2d10` − Willpower months (minimum 2). Institutional care with medication: reduce months to weeks.
2. **Therapist Assessment** — Test Academic Lore + Intelligence (or Scrutiny + Wisdom) vs. TN 80 − patient's Trauma. On success, Test Persuasion + Fellowship vs. patient's Trauma as TN (+5 per Raise from first Test). Up to 2 assistants may aid.
3. **Patient Breakthrough** — If Step 2 succeeds and patient hasn't gained >10 Trauma since last success: patient Tests Wisdom + Composure − Forbidden Lore dots vs. Trauma as TN. Add +1 per Devotion dot, +5 per Raise from Step 2. Only a Spirit Mentor may aid.
4. **Recovery** — On success, reduce Trauma by `1d5` + 2 per Raise (capped at 1/10 of current Trauma).

> **Critical Failure:** If all dice on any Test show 1s, the patient gains 1 Trauma.

## Healing Mental Trauma

Beyond professional therapy, Trauma can be reduced through:

- **Rest and Recuperation** — extended downtime in safe environments
- **Magical Healing** — certain spells and abilities heal mental wounds
- **Fulfilling Alignment** — acting strongly in accordance with one's Alignment restores mental stability

The SM should work with the player to determine appropriate recovery methods and timeframes for their character's specific traumas and disorders.
