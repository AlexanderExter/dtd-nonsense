# SM Reference

This chapter provides guidance for Story Masters on running Dungeons the Dragoning 40,000 games effectively.

---

## System Assumptions

### Character Competency

Characters are action heroes. Normal everyday activities don't require rolls. Mr. Normal with 2 dots and no Drive skill can commute for ten years without accidents. Spider phobia requires Goliath Bird-Eating Tarantula on head or horse-sized spider approaching at 30 KPH to provoke TN 15 Fear(1).

Just because a character doesn't have `8k4` doesn't mean they shouldn't try. Common TNs are 10-20; over 20 is something fewer than 1 in 10 can do. Swinging across a chasm while shot at = TN 10-15. The `8k4` is for world champion level—flying a fighter through a collapsing skyscraper without damage is TN 35 (`8k4` pilot has 50% success).

If a character gets more than one try, the question is how long it takes and if they fail badly before succeeding.

### Player Input

Players have real input through Backgrounds, group feats/assets (Peer, Hunted), and stunting. The SM has final say, but player-chosen Backgrounds show interest in those setting aspects.

Only deny if it completely conflicts with established setting. Work out name/detail changes before refusing.

---

## Stunting

Two and three die stunts allow scene editing (not contradicting existing content). If there needs to be a chandelier or trash chute, it exists unless it contradicts the scene.

**Stunt rolls:** Ask for second roll only if really needed. TN never over 25, usually 10-20. Stunt dice apply to all stunt rolls.

**Failure narration:** The stunting player narrates their failure. It didn't work, but no prat-fall unless desired.

---

## Scenery

Blow it up. Reinforced concrete is AP 16 cover—a strong guy with power fist has 60% chance of 6+ damage past it. Running firefights through collapsing buildings are good action scenes.

Kicking over a brazier isn't a stunt. Picking up two braziers and banging them on someone's head like burning cymbals = stunt.

### "No Empty Rooms"

An empty hangar is a bad set. Add scaffolding, toolboxes, tire stacks, oil slicks, half an airplane, exploding barrels. Characters bodily throw enemies into wood chippers.

## Rulings & Improvisation

**Not everything is in the combat actions list.** SMs wing it. There's an "Other Actions" paragraph even if short on details.

If conflicts arise, check if it works the same for NPCs as PCs.

**Example (throwing):** Grapple, get to hurt phase, use Strength Test (TN = Size × 5, +1 per armor Resilience boost) to lift. Victim can escape next turn. Presuming failure, throw next turn (another Test for distance = Strength meters + 1m per raise). Accurate throw = Ballistics Test, damage = thrown person's Size as rolled dice, half as kept. Strength 7 werewolf is good at chucking halflings.

### Character Sheets

Not everything is written there. Players can swing on chandeliers, bribe guards, light fires with Energy Ray, spike vampires to ceilings, punch cars to a stop, ride rockets into space by chaining themselves outside.

## Collaborative Storytelling

### SM Input

SMs can speak up: "I have an idea for this NPC" or "I'd love today's session to explore this dungeon." SMs ask players questions: "Who did your character hate most in her home town?"

### Just In Case Optional Rule

Once per session, anyone can ask for Level + Power stat roll (TN based on Level: 2, 10, 20, 30, or 40). No failure penalty. Caller asks one question plus one per two raises:

- "What can I use to \_\_\_?"
- "What is the biggest threat?"
- "What is the greatest danger?"
- "Who here is most vulnerable and how?"
- "How can we best end this quickly?"

## Minions

Carving through minion armies with chain-axe and power sword is normal and fun.

> **Clarification Note:** Use Minion rules for unnamed mooks and henchmen. Named NPCs with narrative importance should have full stat blocks appropriate to their role.

### When to Use Minions

- **Minions (simplified stats):** Nameless soldiers, cultists, guards, and other expendable enemies
- **Full NPCs:** Villains, important allies, recurring characters, boss fights

### Minion Combat Flow

Minions use Threat rating as their dice pool and Damage rating for harm dealt. They don't track Hit Points individually—instead, successful attacks remove minions from the group.

## Combat Flow Guidance

> **Clarification Note:** Each game event is discrete and follows a priority order determined by the SM. There is no such thing as truly simultaneous actions. When timing matters, use Initiative order as the baseline, with the SM adjudicating edge cases.

### Common Timing Questions

**Q: Two characters attack each other at the same time?**
A: Higher Initiative acts first. The second character's action happens after resolving the first, even if that results in them being unable to act.

**Q: Can I interrupt someone's action?**
A: Only with Reactions specifically designed for interruption (Parry, Dodge, Refute, etc.). You cannot take a Half Action to interrupt someone else's turn.

**Q: Multiple effects triggering at once?**
A: Active player (whose turn it is) resolves their effects first. For effects during the same player's turn, the controlling player chooses order.

## Scale Interactions

> **Clarification Note:** Personal, Vehicle, and Ship scales do not interact directly. Actions within a scale use that scale's rules; crossing scales requires narrative justification and SM ruling.

### Personal vs. Vehicle

- Characters can board, sabotage, or attack vehicles from inside
- Personal weapons generally cannot harm vehicles except through called shots to vulnerable points
- Vehicle weapons are devastating to personnel

### Vehicle vs. Ship

- Similar relationship: vehicle weapons have minimal effect on spelljammers
- Boarding actions allow personal/vehicle scale combat inside ships

---

## Action Economy

> **Clarification Note:** The number of Free Actions per turn is at SM discretion. Generally allow 2-3 free actions without question; more than that should require narrative justification or be denied.

### Guidelines

- Speaking a sentence or two: Free
- Drawing a holstered weapon: Free
- Dropping something: Free
- Multiple weapon swaps per turn: Probably not
- Lengthy monologue: Might require a Half Action to complete

## Common Skill Applications

### Persuasion vs. Deceive vs. Intimidation

- **Persuasion:** Honest attempts to convince through logic or appeal
- **Deceive:** Lies, half-truths, and misdirection
- **Intimidation:** Coercion through fear or threat

### Perception vs. Scrutiny

- **Perception:** Noticing physical things (hidden door, lurking assassin)
- **Scrutiny:** Reading people (detecting lies, noticing nervousness)

### Academic Lore vs. Forbidden Lore vs. Common Lore

- **Common Lore:** General knowledge everyone might know
- **Academic Lore:** Scholarly or technical knowledge
- **Forbidden Lore:** Dangerous, secret, or heretical knowledge
