﻿# Ships

## Building a Ship

### Step One: Determine Build Points

The number of **Build Points (BP)** you have to spend is determined by your dots in Holdings. Build Points are used to purchase everything from the hull itself to systems, weapons, and crew.

| Holdings | BP  |
| -------- | --- |
| 0        | 0   |
| 1        | 2   |
| 2        | 3   |
| 3        | 4   |
| 4        | 5   |
| 5        | 5+  |

### Step Two: Purchase a Hull

Each ship hull has the following stats:

- **Hull Class** - Determines size (Escort, Destroyer, Cruiser, Battleship). Affects ramming damage.
- **Cost** - BP cost including basic necessities (spaceworthy but defenseless)
- **Crew** - Maximum Crew Size (abstract units: ~10 people per point for Escorts, ~20 for Destroyers, ~30 for Cruisers, ~50 for Battleships). Determines dice pool in combat.
- **Hull Strength** - Hit Points for the ship
- **Maneuverability** - Modifier to certain combat tests
- **Acceleration** - Modifier to speed change tests
- **Speed** - Combat speed in Void Units (VU 6Mm)
- **Sensors** - Modifier to perception-type tests
- **Consoles** - Equipment slots by type (Universal slots hold anything)
- **Weapons** - Hardpoints (Forward/Rear)

## Step Three: Outfitting the Hull

Every ship hull has basic components installed: sensor array, life support, living quarters, engines, small shuttlebay, main reactor, Geller Field generator, and spelljamming helm. Shields, weapons, and consoles must be purchased separately.

### Crew Quality

By default, a ship has **Crew Quality 2**. This can be improved by spending BP.

| Quality | Cost   |
| ------- | ------ |
| 1       | -5 BP  |
| 2       | 0 BP   |
| 3       | +10 BP |
| 4       | +20 BP |
| 5       | +30 BP |

### Shields

Shields have a **Capacity** (maximum damage absorbed) and **Regeneration** (strength regained each turn).

| Type         | Mk.I (Cost 5) | Mk.II (Cost 10) | Mk.III (Cost 15) | Mk.IV (Cost 20) |
| ------------ | ------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| Standard     | 80/15         | 105/15          | 125/20           | 150/20          |
| Covariant    | 100/5         | 135/5           | 165/10           | 200/10          |
| Regenerative | 60/25         | 75/30           | 85/35            | 100/40          |
| Resilient    | 80/7          | 115/9           | 140/11           | 170/13          |
| Multiphasic  | 20/10 (×3)    | 20/10 (×4)      | 20/10 (×5)       | 20/10 (×6)      |

**Shield Types:**

- **Standard** - Baseline shields, form a bubble around the ship
- **Covariant** - Higher capacity, slower regeneration. Good against burst damage
- **Regenerative** - Recover quickly, good against low-damage weapons
- **Resilient** - Cannot be disrupted (all weapons have Disruption 0 against them)
- **Multiphasic** - Multiple weak overlapping layers, each attacked separately

## Weapons

### Weapon Stats

- **Dam** - Damage dealt
- **Dis** - Disruption (reduces target shield regeneration)
- **Acc** - Accuracy modifier to attack rolls
- **Crit** - Critical rating (added to crit rolls when hitting hull)
- **Range** - Range in VU
- **Cost** - BP cost
- **Arc** - Firing arc: Narrow (fore/rear only), Normal (+port/starboard), Wide (all quadrants)
- **Type** - Lance (fired individually) or Array (can be linked together)

### Weapon Types

- **Las** - Default type, no special properties
- **Melta** - Short range, excellent damage, expensive
- **Plasma** - Good range and damage, slow projectiles (harder to hit)
- **Orgone** - Accurate, good disruption, low hull damage
- **Mass Driver** - Cheap, accurate, poor disruption, short range
- **Positron** - Long range, good disruption, inaccurate, expensive
- **Anti-Meson** - Cheap, excellent range, low stopping power

## Space Combat

### Combat Overview

1. **Surprise** - Surprised ship loses its turn in the first round
2. **Initiative** - Ship's Sensors + Acceleration + 1d10
3. **Taking Turns** - In initiative order, each ship takes a Maneuver action and additional actions
4. **Round Ends** - Continue until combat is over

### Actions

- **Maneuver Actions** - Required every turn (ship is too large to sit still)
- **Tactical Actions** - Attacks, boarding parties, called shots
- **Engineering Actions** - Repairs, power diversion
- **Command Actions** - Communication, crew motivation
- **Arcana Actions** - Sensors, shields, spelljamming helm

### Ship Tests

Ship tests use Crew Assigned (rolled dice, max 10) and Crew Quality (kept dice). Most ships have Crew Quality 2.

### Bridge Officers

**Bridge Officers** are skilled characters (usually PCs) in charge of a department. A Bridge Officer may use their own skill in place of Crew Quality for relevant tests. Positions:

- **Helmsman** - Maneuver Actions
- **Tactical Officer** - Tactical Actions
- **Chief Engineer** - Engineering Actions
- **Captain** - Command Actions
- **Chief Arcana Officer** - Arcana Actions

---

## Maneuver Actions

A ship must take a Maneuver Action every turn. Failure results in all attacks automatically hitting and potential crashes. If the ship is immobile, all Tactical Actions automatically succeed with an extra raise.

### Move

Move at half or full Speed, then turn up to 90 degrees. No test required, but at least 1 Crew must be assigned.

### Adjust Speed

Move at half or full Speed. Test Pilot (modified by Acceleration) vs TN 20. Success: Speed +/- 1. Every 2 raises: additional +/- 1. Cannot more than double Speed.

### Adjust Heading

Move at half Speed. Test vs TN:

- TN 15: Turn 90 degrees before moving
- TN 25: Turn 90 degrees before and after moving
- TN 35: Move 1/4 Speed, 180 degree turn, then complete half Speed move

### Evasive Maneuvers

Move at half Speed. Until next turn, TN to hit = Pilot Test result + ship's Maneuver (instead of 3x Crew Quality + Maneuver). May subtract 15 from TN to hit to add 90 degree turn at end.

---

## Tactical Actions

### Shoot Guns

Fire any number of weapons. Targets must be in range and arc. Attack with Ballistics vs Static Defense (3x Crew Quality + Maneuver).

**Lance weapons** must be fired individually. **Array weapons** can be linked (same test, same target, resolve damage separately).

### Boarding Party

Must be within 1 VU. Opposed Weaponry or Brawling Test. Success: Reduce target Crew by 2 + 1 per raise. Failure: Lose half the Crew used.

### Target Subsystem

Requires prior Active Augury. Hit at Ballistics -`1k0` or win Boarding with 2 raises: Target console disabled until Emergency Repair (TN = damage dealt or Boarding result).

### Ramming Speed!

End turn within 1 VU facing target, or pass through their space. Opposed Piloting (+ Maneuver). Success: Damage = Speed or kept dice (whichever higher)k[Hull Class]. Kept dice: Escort 2, Destroyer 3, Cruiser 4, Battleship 5, Station 6, Asteroid 8, Planet 10. Ignores shields. Ramming ship takes half damage (full vs non-ships). Both roll crits at +0.

---

## Engineering Actions

### Divert Power to Weapons

Shields don't regenerate next turn, can only move at half Speed. Tech-Use vs TN 15: Energy weapons +`1k0` damage, +`1k0` per 2 raises.

### Divert Power to Shields

Energy attacks at -`1k0` attack/damage next turn, can only move at half Speed. Tech-Use vs TN 15: Regenerate shields immediately, +1d10 per 2 raises.

### Divert Power to Engines

Shields don't regenerate, energy attacks at -`1k0` attack/damage next turn. Tech-Use vs TN 15: If moving full speed, move additional VU = half Speed, +1 VU per 2 raises. Cannot use with Adjust Speed.

### Divert Power to Deflector Dish

Shields don't regenerate, energy attacks at -`1k0` damage, can only move at half Speed next turn. Tech-Use vs TN 15: +`1k0` to Arcana tests next round, +`1k0` per 2 raises.

### Emergency Repair

Tech-Use vs TN 15: Repair `1k1` hull damage, +`1k0` per raise. Cannot repair more than half damage taken since last turn. For disabled consoles, TN listed in critical effect. May split crew among multiple Emergency Repairs.

---

## Command Actions

Command Actions don't require Crew assignment. Use Command skill.

### Brace for Impact!

Charisma + Command vs TN 30 (-5 per Crew Quality). Success: Reduce crew losses from crits by 1 (minimum 0) until next turn.

### Hail

Communicate with other ship. May initiate social combat.

### Picard Speech

Fellowship + Command vs TN 10 (+5 per Crew Quality). Success: Crew Quality +1 for all other tests this round. Once per scene (twice at Level 3, three times at Level 5).

### Micromanage

Must have at least 1 dot in the relevant skill. Composure + Command vs TN 15: Give one test +`1k0`.

---

## Arcana Actions

### Active Augury

Arcana vs TN 10. Success: Learn target's hull strength, crew remaining, shield strength, or one console/weapon. Additional choice per raise.

### Jam Communications

Arcana Test. Target must beat your result to send signals. Lasts until your next turn.

### Triage

Medicae vs TN 15. Success: 1 Crew lost since last turn returns to duty, +1 per raise. Cannot recover more than half lost crew.

---

## Fighter Craft

Ships with Fighter Bays can launch fighters as minion squads.

- Devote Crew to fighters: squad of 2x that crew, Quality = Crew Quality
- Static Defense = 5x Crew Quality
- Base damage = 5
- Attack range = 2x Crew Quality in VU

**On Attack:** Use minion rules, no shield disruption, no crit bonus.

**On Defense:**

- Prevent one Boarding Action
- Sacrifice one fighter to block one weapon hit
- Replace ship's Static Defense with opposed roll vs enemy fighters

## Attacks and Damage

### Step One: Fire Everything

Fire any number of weapons. Lance weapons fired individually; Array weapons can link if same target and within range/arc of all weapons.

### Step Two: Hitting Things

TN to hit = 3x Crew Quality + Maneuverability (modified by actions, consoles, feats).

### Step Three: Shields Up

Damage goes to shields first. Regeneration reduced by Disruption of all weapons that hit since last turn.

### Step Four: Cracking Bulkheads

If shields down, damage goes to hull. Roll on Critical Damage chart, adding weapon's Crit rating.

### Step Five: Results

- Shields at 0 or below: Collapsed (cannot regenerate during combat)
- Hull at 0: Ship explodes (loss of all hands except those in escape pods)

## Repairs and Resupply

### Wealth

- Each week in port: Repair 1d10 Hull or recover 1 Crew
- Cannot make requisition tests while funding repairs
- One week to refuel/resupply
- Torpedoes via Wealth tests

### Backing

- Friendly port: Approval + 1d10 Hull and 1 Crew per week; fuel/food in 1 week; torpedoes in weeks = cost
- Non-friendly port: 1d10 weeks for paperwork
- Not approved: 1d10 Hull OR 1 Crew per 2 weeks

- Per week: Hull = Chief Engineer's Craft skill; 1 Crew recruited locally
- 1d10 weeks to scavenge fuel/food
- Torpedoes via Acquisition tests

One test between ports (unless Extended Supply Vaults). Requirements:

1. Find anchorage (orbit, asteroid field, planet landing)
2. Assign Crew (unavailable for other duties)

- **Success:** Restore `2k1` Hull, repair all critical effects
- **Per Raise:** Restore 1 console OR 1d5 Hull

In port with shipyard: Wealth test vs TN 15 + BP cost. Installation: 1 day per BP. Resale value: Zero.

## Warp Travel

Requirements: Spelljamming helm, Geller Field, Navigator (at least 1 dot in Divination magic).

### Step Zero: Leave the Crystal Sphere

Find natural openings: Active Augury + Sensors vs TN 20 (+10 with map or previous entry). Syrneth Portal Stations auto-detected. Typically 500 VU away (1d100+50 × 5).

**Create Portal:** Arcana Action vs TN 25 (+5 per shield mark). Success: Portal open 1d5 hours (+/-1 hour per raise). Failure by 2+: Unstable portal; Maneuver Action (+ Acceleration) vs TN 20 to get through. Failure: `8k6` hull damage, 2d5 crit rolls at +3.

### Step One: Open Portal into Warp

Outside Crystal Sphere with Geller Field on: Navigator Arcana + Intelligence vs TN 15 (+`2k0` with Ancient Spelljamming Helm). Failure: 1d10 Fatigue to Navigator.

### Step Two: Chart the Course

Navigator Divination + Wisdom vs TN 20 (modified by ship's sensors).

- **Raises for:** Syrneth route map, starting from Warp Relay, going to Sigil, destination has Warp Relay
- **-`2k0`:** Going where nobody has gone before
- **Success by 2+ raises:** +`1k0` per 2 raises to Step 3 rolls
- **Failure:** -`3k0` to Step 3 rolls

### Step Three: Steer the Vessel

Piloting + Wisdom (+ Acceleration) vs Voyage TN.

| TN  | Duration      | Example                                 |
| --- | ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 5   | 1d10+5 days   | Nearby, less than half sector           |
| 15  | 1d10 weeks    | Other side of sector                    |
| 25  | 1d10+5 weeks  | Near side of next sector                |
| 30  | 2d5 months    | Somewhere in next sector                |
| 35  | 1d10+5 months | Skipping past next sector               |
| 40  | 1d5 years     | More than halfway across explored space |

**Bonuses:** +`1k0` for Syrneth route map, convoy
**Penalties:** -`1k0` for warp vortexes, reality rifts, Sargasso, daemons, nebula, no relief helmsman

- **Success:** Per 2 raises, reduce time by 1 step (minimum 1d10+5 days)
- **Failure:** Roll Warp Encounters at cumulative +1, make another Test at cumulative -`1k0`, time increases 1 step

At trip end, roll Warp Encounters with accumulated modifiers.

### Step Four: Leave the Warp

Navigator Arcana + Intelligence vs TN 15 (+`1k0` with Ancient Spelljamming Helm).

- **Failure:** Drop dangerously close to object, possibly flying at it
- **2+ Checks:** Trapped in Warp; Navigator takes warp damage (checks rolled, half kept) to head; 1d10 Fatigue. Try again. Roll Warp Encounters every 2d10 hours.

## Spelljamming Advice

### General Advice

1. **Don't fight in empty space.** It's boring. Put action at Crystal Sphere edges, asteroid fields, around disrupting gas giants, near Warp rifts, up star-beast noses.

2. **Go easy on torpedoes.** Four tubes with Extended Supply Vaults make every fight about who lands 40+ damage first. Shields top at 200, hulls at 100. That's two rounds with torpedoes.

3. **Ten crew per roll, maximum.** 40 crew modron death-sphere? `10k5` to shoot, `30k5` = `10k10`+25 to dodge. 85 Static Defense dodge-tank breaks the game.

4. **90° turns don't have to be exact.** Let them do 27° to barely miss an asteroid. Nothing breaks.

## Crashing Examples

1. **Sultana (S) clips Belle (B) head-on:** S traveled 6 VU, B traveled 4 VU. 6+4=10, so S takes `10k5` (~42), B takes `10k2` (~22). Both roll critical at +0.

2. **Belle crashes into planet (1g = 10m/s²):** 10-4=6, ship takes `6k10`→`6k6` (~35). Three critical rolls.

3. **Sultana crashes into moon base (1.7m/s²):** 12+2=14, so `14k10`→`10k10`+10 (~70). Three criticals. Base (if built as immobile ship) takes `14k2`→`10k4` (~37).

## Divert Power

**For next round limitations:** 1/2 speed basic move only, weapons attack at -`1k0` and deal -`1k0` damage (except torpedoes & fighters), shields don't regenerate, sensors at -`1k1`.

**On next round bonuses (choose one):**

1. Shields regenerate at 150% (+1d10/2 raises)
2. Engines move at 150% (+1 VU/2 raises)
3. Weapon damage +`1k0` (+`1k0`/2 raises)
4. Sensors/Arcana +`1k1` (+`1k0`/2 raises)
5. Other active systems: generally +50% range or +`1k1` dice (+10% or +`1k0`/2 raises)
