# Races

## Racial Traits Overview

- **Characteristic Bonus:** Your race offers a bonus to one characteristic. For most races, choose between one of two options (you only get one bonus unless specifically stated otherwise).
- **Skill Bonus:** Each race gives a bonus to a few skills representing natural inclinations and ability.
- **Power:** Every race has a special talent or power unique to them.
- **Size:** Used to determine Hit Point loss from attacks and how hard you are to hit. Larger characters lose fewer Hit Points but are hit more often; smaller characters are harder to hit but take more damage per hit.

---

## Aasimar

**Size:** 5 | **Languages:** Trade, Celestial

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Wisdom or Constitution

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Command, +1 Ballistics

**Racial Power:**
- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** Aasimar begin play with the feats Jaded and Fearless (Angry).

---

## Dark Eldarin

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Trade, Dark Eldarin

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Charisma or Dexterity

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Deceive, +1 Forbidden Lore

**Racial Power:**
- **Warp Miasma:** As a half action, create a 4m radius sphere of darkness. This blocks line of effect and anyone within the sphere is blind. The sphere lasts for one round per level. Can be used once per scene at Level 1, twice per scene at Level 3, and three times per scene at Level 5.

---

## Dragonborn

**Size:** 5 | **Languages:** Trade, Draconic

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Strength or Charisma

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Command, +1 Intimidation

**Racial Power:**
- **Dragon Breath:** Once per scene, make an attack with your breath weapon using the same weapon profile as a Flamer. At Level 3 and 5 you gain an additional use of this power per scene.

---

## Eldarin

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Trade, Eldarin

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Wisdom or Intelligence

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Academic Lore, +1 Arcana

**Racial Power:**
- **Warp Step:** Once per scene, teleport up to twice your speed as a half action. You must be able to see your destination. At Level 3 and 5 you gain an additional use of this power per scene.

---

## Elf

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Trade, Elven

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Wisdom or Dexterity

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Perception, +1 Charm

**Racial Power:**
- **Elven Accuracy:** Once per scene, reroll a failed Weaponry or Ballistics skill Test. At Level 3 and 5 you gain an additional use of this power per scene.

---

## Gnome

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Trade, Gnomish

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Intelligence or Fellowship

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Crafts, +1 Academic Lore

**Racial Power:**
- **Improvise:** Begin play with one free Weapon proficiency of any type and one free Armor proficiency of any type.

---

## Halfling

**Size:** 2 | **Languages:** Trade, Halfling

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Intelligence or Fellowship

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Larceny, +1 Deceive

**Racial Power:**
- **Shifty:** While most races use Wisdom and Dexterity to determine Static Defense, Halflings instead have a Static Defense equal to 10, plus 6 for every dot of Dexterity, minus twice their size.

---

## Human

**Size:** 4 | **Languages:** Trade, Human

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to any one Characteristic

**Skill Bonus:** +1 to any two skills

**Racial Power:**
- **Heroic Heritage:** A human begins play with one additional Hero Point.

---

## Ork

**Size:** 5 | **Languages:** Trade, Orkish

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Strength or Willpower

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Intimidation, +1 Scrutiny

**Racial Power:**
- **WAAAAAGH!:** At the beginning of each combat, heal Hit Points equal to your level.

---

## Squat

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Squat, Trade

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Constitution or Willpower

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Crafts, +1 Common Lore

**Racial Power:**
- **Squat Toughness:** A Squat's Resilience is treated as being one higher for the purpose of determining Hit Points lost due to damage.

---

## Tau

**Size:** 4 | **Languages:** Tau, Trade

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Intelligence or Composure

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Common Lore, +1 Persuasion

**Racial Power:**
- **Fall Back:** Whenever you successfully dodge a melee attack, you may make a free withdraw action, but you may only move half of the normal distance.

---

## Tiefling

**Size:** 5 | **Languages:** Abyssal, Trade

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Dexterity or Constitution

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Intimidate, +1 Weaponry

**Racial Power:**
- **Bloody Minded:** You may reroll damage dice that land on a 1.

---

## Thri-Kreen

**Size:** 4 | **Languages:** Trade, Thri-Kreen

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 Dexterity or +1 Wisdom

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Acrobatics, +1 Perception

**Racial Power:**
- **Multi-Armed:** A Thri-Kreen's smaller pair of arms allow them to take a Ready action as a free action once per round and reload their weapons in half the listed time.

---

## Kenku

**Size:** 3 | **Languages:** Trade, Kenku, any one extra

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 Intelligence or +1 Wisdom

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Performer, +1 Pilot

**Racial Power:**
- **Wing-Aided Movement:** As long as they have room to unfurl their wings, Kenku do not suffer from falling damage. Even when unconscious they instinctively slow their fall and glide, falling only two meters per round and being able to glide horizontally at their normal speed. Kenku get +`2k0` to all Acrobatics tests.

---

## Kobold

**Size:** 2 | **Languages:** Trade, Kobold

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 Willpower or +1 Dexterity

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Larceny, +1 Crafts

**Racial Power:**
- **The Blood:** Kobolds are spiteful and strong-willed enough to demand power they can't handle. Once per round as a Free Action, they may spend two Hit Points to gain a Resource Point which must be immediately spent. This does not count against the number of Resource Points they may spend in a round.

---

## Dryad

**Size:** 4 | **Languages:** Trade, Elven, Spirit-tongue

**Characteristic Modifiers:** +1 to Fellowship or Willpower

**Skill Bonus:** +1 Animal Ken, +1 Scrutiny

**Racial Power (choose one):**
- **Pheromone Pollen:** Dryads produce a type of pollen that subtly affects the minds of others. A Dryad may duplicate the effects of the Attraction spell using their Level + Fellowship in place of a Focus Power test.
- **Not An Animal:** As plants, dryads are not susceptible to certain effects that hinder or kill animals. Dryads are immune to the Toxic weapon attribute, the Shocking weapon attribute, and to the effects and addictions of drugs. They take an additional wound from being on fire and need to make a Fear(1) test every time someone tries to set them on fire. Every time a dryad would gain insanity points, they gain one less insanity than normal (minimum zero).
