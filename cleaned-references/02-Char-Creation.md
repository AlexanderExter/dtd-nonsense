﻿# Character Creation

## Step Zero: Character Concept

Before filling in a single dot on your character sheet, think of a concept. Decide on something you'll enjoy playing for the long haul. Your Story Master may restrict some options (e.g., disallowing some races or Exaltations), so make sure they approve your concept.

## Step One: Starting Scores

### Characteristics

All characters have nine Characteristics divided into three categories:

- **Physical:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution
- **Social:** Charisma, Fellowship, Composure
- **Mental:** Intelligence, Wisdom, Willpower

Prioritize your Characteristic groups as Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary.

All characters start with 1 dot in each Characteristic. Distribute additional dots based on priority:

| Priority  | Dots |
| --------- | ---- |
| Primary   | 6    |
| Secondary | 4    |
| Tertiary  | 2    |

**Restriction:** You may not bring any Characteristic to 5 in this step (racial bonuses can increase it later).

### Skills

Skills are also divided into Mental, Physical, and Social groups. All skills start at 0 dots.

| Priority  | Dots |
| --------- | ---- |
| Primary   | 8    |
| Secondary | 6    |
| Tertiary  | 4    |

**Restriction:** No skill can be raised above 3 dots during this step.

### Experience Point Buy Variant

As an alternative method, characteristics start at 1 and you spend XP instead of distributing dots. You do not receive starting XP during Step Seven, but may reserve up to 10% for feats, assets, backgrounds, etc.

| Power Level | Starting XP |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Low Power   | 4800 XP     |
| Mid Power   | 5200 XP     |
| High Power  | 5600 XP     |

## Step Four: Starting Class

Your Class represents your training and what you've learned to do. You can only use Experience Points to purchase Advances on your Class List.

Classes offer abilities and powers, typically in the form of Feats, Magic, and Fighting Styles.

## Step Five: Backgrounds

Backgrounds quantify a character's backstory—organizational ties, contacts, fame, etc.

**Starting dots:** 7

**Restriction:** No background may be raised above 3 dots without spending XP. XP may only be spent on Backgrounds during character creation.

| Background  | Description                                       |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Allies      | People willing to help you                        |
| Artifact    | A magical item (max 5 dots at character creation) |
| Backing     | Ranking member in an organization                 |
| Contacts    | Ability to find someone to help, at a price       |
| Fame        | How well people know of you                       |
| Followers   | Loyal assistants devoted to you                   |
| Holdings    | Large property like a business or ship            |
| Inheritance | Epic loot                                         |
| Mentor      | Someone guiding you on the Hero's path            |
| Status      | Other Heroes have heard of your deeds             |
| Wealth      | Liquid resources and expected lifestyle           |

## Step Seven: Experience

**Starting XP:** 600

### XP Costs

| Purchase                         | Cost                 |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Improve a Characteristic         | 100 × Current Rank   |
| Improve Devotion                 | 50 × Current Rank    |
| New Skill                        | 50                   |
| Improve a Skill                  | 50 × Current Rank    |
| New Sword School or Magic School | 100                  |
| Improve a Sword School           | 100 × Current Rank\* |
| Improve a Magic School           | 100 × Current Rank\* |
| Buy an Asset                     | 100\*\*              |
| Buy a Background                 | 50 per dot\*\*       |
| Improve Power Stat               | 200 × Current Rank\* |
| Buy a Feat                       | (varies)             |

**Notes:**

- \* Power Stat, Magic Schools, and Fighting Style ranks are capped at your current level.
- \*\* Assets and new Background dots can only be purchased at character creation. Background dots above 3 cost double.

You can only spend XP on skills, talents, and characteristics from your Class. Some feats can only be purchased during character creation.

## Step Nine: Finishing Touches

### Derived Statistics

| Stat           | Formula                                                   |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size           | 4 for average human; determined by race                   |
| Static Defense | 10 + (Dexterity + Wisdom) × 3 − (Size × 2)                    |
| Hit Points     | (Constitution + Willpower) × 2                              |
| Mental Defense | 5 + (5 × Composure)                                       |
| Resolve        | Willpower + Composure                                     |
| Speed          | Strength + Dexterity (meters per half action; ×6 for run) |
| Resilience     | Average of Size and Level (round up) + 1                  |
| Hero Points    | 2 (starting)                                              |

**Resilience Example:** A level 1 Human has Resilience 4. A level 5 Human has Resilience 6.

**Damage Calculation:** Divide incoming damage by Resilience to determine Hit Point loss and Critical Damage.

## Example Character Creation

Jane builds a Tiefling Werewolf Barbarian for melee combat.

**Step 1 - Characteristics:** Primary Physical, Secondary Social, Tertiary Mental. Distributes points accordingly.

**Step 1 - Skills:** Primary Physical (8 dots), Secondary Mental (6 dots), Tertiary Social (4 dots).

**Step 2 - Race:** Tiefling. Adds +1 Constitution (now 4), free dot in Intimidate and Weaponry. Notes Tiefling racial power.

**Step 3 - Exaltation:** Werewolf. Notes Shifting, Lycan Resistance, Spirit Tongue, Silver Bane. Starts with Feral Heart 1 (Fast Healing power). Max Rage: 5.

**Step 4 - Class:** Barbarian (meets prereqs: Weaponry 2, Athletics 1).

**Step 5 - Backgrounds:** Contacts 3, Status 2, Backing 2 (Society of Sensation).

**Step 6 - Alignment:** Malal. Starting Devotion: 6.

**Step 7 - XP (600 total):**

- Hindrances taken: Enemy, Impulsive (grants additional XP)
- Tiger Claw sword school Rank 1: 200 XP
- Black Spiral Dancer asset: 100 XP
- Appearance asset: 100 XP
- Frenzy feat: (cost)
- Power Attack feat: (cost)
- Dexterity 2→3: 200 XP

**Step 8 - Equipment:** Air package (Hand Weapon, Laspistol, Knife, Quilted Vest, Bodyglove, Charm, Deck of Cards).

**Step 9 - Finishing Touches:**

- Size: 5
- Hit Points: 12 ((Con + Wil) × 2)
- Resolve: 4 (Wil + Composure)
- Static Defense: 15 (10 + 9 Dex + 6 Wis − 10 Size)
- Speed: 7 (Str 4 + Dex 3)
- Hero Points: 2

**Character Name:** Traya Psine Kaos
