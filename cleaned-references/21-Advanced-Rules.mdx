## Daemonic Possession

Modifies: [Exaltations](./05-Exaltations.mdx), [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx)

### Standard Daemon Possession

Apply to the possessed victim:

1. **Increase** Strength, Constitution, and Willpower by +1 (Lesser) or +2 (Greater). May exceed 5.
2. **Add traits:** Daemonic (+Con to AP & HP), Dark Sight, Resource Stat
3. **Increase Level** by 1 (max: daemon's Level)
4. **Increase Resilience** by 1
5. **Recalculate** Hit Points, Speed, etc.
6. Greater Daemons also gain warp fire breath weapon

### Modified/Random Daemon Possession

1. **Increase** Strength, Constitution, and Resilience by +1. Level +1 if less than daemon's.
2. **Add** Dark Sight; make them a Daemonhost Exalt with Power stat = daemon's Level (magic school, bonus armor, Resource Points)
3. **Add** daemon's feats, special powers, magic schools, Gun Katas, or Sword Schools
4. **Use** better of daemon's or victim's skills
5. **Recalculate** Hit Points, Speed, etc.
6. Greater Daemons also gain warp fire breath weapon

### Example: Possessed Soldier

**Lesser Daemon:** Str/Dex/Con 3, Int 2, Wis/Wil 3, Cha 5, Fel 1, Cmp 2. Static Defense 20, Armor(all) 10, Resilience 4, HP 9, Init +7, Speed 6, Level 2, Size 4. Skills: Weaponry 2, Ballistics 2, Brawl 3. Feats: Cleave, Iron Jaw, Devastating Critical, Furious Assault. Abilities: Fear, Caster (Illusion).

**Soldier:** Physicals 3, else 2. Ballistics 2, Weaponry 2. Flak Armor (5 AP arms/body, 4 AP head). Lasgun, knife. Level 2.

**Combined Result:** Str 4, Dex 3, Con 4, Level 2, Resource 8. Speed 7, Size/Resilience 4/5, Static Defense 17, HP 8. AP: Flak + daemon +6 (all, not vs magic/silver). Illusion `4k2`, spells. Fearless (Angry). Enhanced melee attacks.

## Tracking

Modifies: [Core Rules — Tests](./01-Core-Rules.mdx)

### Following Tracks

**Default TN: 15.** Apply modifiers cumulatively:

**Numbers:**

- Subtract number of creatures after the first from TN
- Subtract 2× Size of largest creature if squeezing through spaces

**Terrain:**

| Mod | Condition |
| :---: | --- |
| −10 | Easily retains signs (sandy beach, light snow) |
| +10 | Doesn't retain signs (building interiors, running water) |

**Entity:**

| Mod | Condition |
| :---: | --- |
| −20 | Obvious marks (giant slug slime trails) |
| −10 | Bleeding/leaking |
| +10 | Minimal marks (small snake in grass) |
| +20 | Phasing creatures leaving minimal ectoplasm |

**Senses:**

| Mod | Condition |
| :---: | --- |
| −10 | Tracking with sense the creature doesn't know about |
| +10 | Creature knows and can counter tracking sense |
| +20 | Wrong sense for the job (smell in perfume shop) |

**Time:**

| Mod | Condition |
| :---: | --- |
| −20 | Just moved out of sight/hearing, fresh spoor |
| −10 | Nothing has obscured signs |
| +10 | Something has covered/removed some signs |
| +20 | Very few signs left (days old, beach after tide) |

**Identification:**

| Mod | Condition |
| :---: | --- |
| −20 | Easily identifiable (rabbit vs. cougar tracks) |
| −10 | Distinguishing features provide decent guess |
| +10 | Little information, maybe a clue or two |
| +20 | Difficult signs or no specific markings |

## Astropaths & Navigators

Modifies: Setting assumptions (see [Setting Lore](./23-Setting-Lore.mdx))

The setting is intentionally incomplete and contradictory, giving SMs freedom. Below are considerations for communication and navigation.

### Astropaths

Key design questions: speed/reliability, what they communicate, how many exist, who controls them.

**Speed options:**

- **Same as spelljammer:** Astropaths reserved for off-route and secure faction networks. Messages sent multiple times for accuracy.
- **Faster than spelljammers:** Massive demand for services, faction control implications.

**Communication models:**

- **Broadcast** — general cries for help, interception possible
- **Connection (Familiarity)** — like cellphones; Alice messages Bob directly
- **Connection (Location)** — like landlines; call a location

**Recommended settings:**

1. No Astropaths — communication by spelljammer only
2. Relatively rare (specialist doctor level), need special equipment, contact by meeting in Warp "locations," can only talk (no data transfer)

### Navigators

Every spelljammer requires a Navigator with Divination magic school dots. This implies either few spelljammers or many sorcerers with 1–2 Divination dots.

Possibilities: Navigators Guild controlling trade, national training camps, schools/unions. Many people can cast Augury or Detect Thoughts.

## Zero-G Combat

Modifies: [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx) — movement, melee, ranged

### Key Concept: Braced

**Braced** (secured against something stable) means: holding a strut, tied to equipment, magnetic boots on hull, clipped to tie-down. Taking a Half Action to Brace counts for heavy weapons.

- **Not braced** does **not** automatically mean Out of Control
- **Out of Control** condition: halve all rolled dice. Stop by grabbing something and bracing.

**Recovery Tests** (TN 15, free Raise with appropriate equipment): Athletics, Acrobatics, Brawling, Tech-Use, Weaponry (whips), Ballistics (grappling hooks), Piloting (jetpacks).

### Combat Penalties

| Situation | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| Melee (not Braced) | Cumulative −`1k0` per attack; carries between turns |
| Ranged (not Braced) | Cumulative −`1k0` per shot fired |
| Full Auto without Bracing | Lose +`2k1` bonus; penalty = half RoF |
| Half Action to Brace | Resets all penalties to zero |

### Melee Damage

- Effective Strength **halved** unless Braced
- **Unaffected:** Grappling damage, parrying weapons (short stabs), Power Field weapons

### Weapon Ranges

- Thrown/muscle-powered: ranges **tripled**
- Other weapons: unaffected

### Movement

Without flight: climb, jump (10× distance per round in straight line, free Raise), or artificial propulsion.

- **Good propulsion:** Flamers (lower but steadier thrust)
- **Useless:** Directed energy weapons, needlers, rocket launchers

**Direction changes:** Dexterity + Acrobatics, TN 15 modified by speed/precision. Near stop = TN 10.

### Explosions

- **If Braced:** Athletics + Strength vs damage to stay put
- **If not Braced:** Acrobatics + Dexterity vs damage to avoid Out of Control (still pushed)

### Zero-G Creatures

Natural zero-G creatures can fly or have bracing method; automatically get two free Raises on rolls. Immaterial/Phasing creatures don't roll — just move.

## Explosives

Modifies: [Combat — Ranged Attacks](./14-Combat.mdx)

### The Problem

When you miss with a blast weapon, where does it land? What's the TN to hit a group?

### Optional Blast Rules

**Scatter on miss:** Miss distance = 1m per range increment (1m short, 2m normal, 3m long, 4m extreme) + 1m per Check. Randomize direction.

**Targeting groups:**

1. Roll attack, **add 2× blast radius** to total
2. Check against all Static Defenses in area
3. Find placement hitting everyone hit and excluding everyone missed
4. If impossible, maximize intended targets; shooter chooses among equal options
5. On miss, minimum scatter = enough to miss whole group

**Cannot target people further apart than blast diameter.**

**Dodging:** Not a direct attack — cannot be dodged without a two-die stunt using environment for cover (still only provides cover AP).

### Missing Allies (Friendly Fire)

When enemies are near allies, calculate both:

- Attack + 2× blast (to hit enemies)
- Attack − 2× blast (to miss ally — ally's Static Defense is TN)

| Attack − 2× blast vs Ally | Attack + 2× blast vs Enemies | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under all | Under all | Miss All (scatter away) |
| Over Ally's defense | Over Enemies' defenses | Hit All (ally may be excluded if geometry allows) |
| Under Ally's defense | Over Enemies' defense | Miss Ally & Hit Enemies (ideal) |

### How to Survive

Don't provoke people with big space guns.

### Survival Math

## Animal Companions

Modifies: [Feats](./07-Feats.mdx) (Divine Bond, Obtain Familiar, Animal Companion)

### Base Companion

**Minion version:** One creature with Threat and Damage ratings of 1.

**Animalistic creature:** Befriended creatures don't take to bionics or combat drugs. Mistreatment = Druid's Oath violation ("respecting nature" clause).

### Improved Animal Companion

**Minion version:** Squad size = character Level (if not already higher). Each time gained: +1 Threat or +1 Damage (max 5 each).

**Creature version:** Level = higher of normal or (character Level − 1). Each time gained: choose one feat from list, increase one physical/mental attribute (except Intelligence) by 1. Can't increase same attribute twice in a row.

**Bonded Companion Feat List:** Armor Proficiency (Light/Medium/Heavy), Blind Fighting, Cat-Fall, Evasion, Defensive Mobility, Heightened Senses (Any), Light Sleeper, Danger Sense, Sound Constitution, Weapon Focus (Any), Combat Mastery

### Beastmaster

**Minion version:** +1 Threat and +1 Damage (max 5 each), squad size = character Level + 1.

**Creature version:** +1 physical characteristic, +1 mental characteristic, +1 Size, +1 Level. Plus one feat and one attribute:

- **Feats:** Devastating Critical, Fearless (Angry), Improved Weapon Focus, Iron Jaw, Swift Attack, True Grit
- **Attributes:** Armor(4), Aura(4), Dark Sight, Regeneration(1), Unnatural Toughness

### Familiars

Choose from: industrial servitor (no arc welder), animal/creature, zombie, spirit/ghost, or elemental. Reduce Size and Strength to 1–2, Level to character's Level (minimum 1).

Each character level increase: familiar gains 1 dot in any attribute or skill (can't increase same one twice in a row).

Replacement familiars don't have increased attributes/skills. Familiars can use appropriate weapons/armor and may be given bionics (if fleshy or constructed).

## The Umbra

Modifies: [Magic](./11-Magic.mdx), [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx), Setting

### Overview

The Umbra is the space between the material world and the Warp. Entry methods: powerful werewolf mirror-stepping, wraith shifting, ghost/daemon sliding, sorcerer portals/rituals.

The barrier ("**Veil**") thickness reflects how close the Warp is to the material world and how easy magic is to use.

### Perception

Experiences vary by individual — slightly out-of-focus, washed out, sepia-toned, thick fog, or dim shadows with lurking horrors. People together may disagree on visuals but agree on what exists.

### Topography

Roughly parallels material world:

- **Solid:** Permanent structures (ground, large plants, permanent buildings)
- **Translucent/invisible:** Non-permanent things (people, doors, shrubs, water)

**Warning:** Don't exit Umbra at ocean bottom unprepared.

Places of natural forces (volcanoes, vortexes, waterfalls) feel nearly normal with extra-large elementals.

### Magic in the Umbra

- Magic is **visible** as neon geometric forms, twisting ribbons, sparkles
- Magic is **tangible** — wards function as doors and walls
- **Breaking wards:** Barrier rating = Focus Power Test result. Damage enough to reduce below minimum
- **Ranged attacks** pass through without effect — must use bare hands, artifact melee weapons, or spells
- **Destructive failure:** Broken spells/wards explode. Radius = 1/5 original Focus Power in meters. Damage = remaining Focus Power result (magic damage, requires Aura)

### Deeper Umbra

Moving deeper requires 1d5+5 Level/Power stat Tests (TN 10 + 2 per step from material):

- Material world fades after third Test
- Landscape shifts faster near the Warp
- Two checks from Warp: Psychic Phenomena roll every few rounds
- One check from Warp: Perils of the Warp Test every `1d5` rounds
- Next Test: actual Warp (bad for most people)

Return requires same Tests in reverse.

### Other Destinations

Accessible from Umbra: Webways, Commoragh, Sigil, Abyss realms (including Ruinous Powers), possibly Celestia.

- **Finding locations:** TN 20 Forbidden Lore for known places, TN 25–30 for Webways
- **Raises** reduce travel time/difficulty
- **Failure** adds +`1d5` to TN; failure with Checks = wrong destination, Warp storm, or hostile encounter
- **Connection** to destination = free Raise

### Building Umbral Realms

Requires reaching middle area and performing difficult rituals (TN 35+) over time. Participants get free Raise finding that place.

Bringing building materials attracts wandering creatures.

**Technology:** Electronics and simple chemical reactions work. Internal combustion, rockets, anti-grav, nuclear power don't work (except small/simple, Syrneth magitech, and Super Solenoid Engines).

### Umbral Creatures

Spirits, elementals, ghosts, wraiths, daemons, powerful spellcasters and werewolves, occasional dragons or aboleths.

**No Modrons:** Their existence is antithetical. Modron areas look monochrome, geometric; movement becomes impossible.

**Native creature traits:** Dark Sight plus: Constitution + Willpower armor/aura, toxic/tearing attacks, pseudo-spells (Level + Wisdom), Amorphous, Stuff of Nightmares, Regeneration, Phasing. Immune to material world drugs/toxins except Null and Spook.

| 2 | Regeneration (Con) |
| 3 | Bonus AP & Aura (Con+Will) |
| 4 | Attacks gain Toxic & Tearing |
| 5 | Uses abilities like spells |

| 8 | Roll `1d5` twice (reroll duplicates) |
