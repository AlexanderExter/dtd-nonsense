﻿# Character Creation

:::note[Quick Reference]

- Character creation follows 10 steps (Steps 0–9)
- Starting scores: Characteristics 6/4/2 dots, Skills 8/6/4 dots, Backgrounds 7 dots
- Starting XP: 600 (purchases capped by class level)
- No Characteristic above 4, no Skill above 3, no Background above 3 without XP at creation
- Hero Points (narrative resource for rerolls and special actions): 2 at start
:::

## Step Zero: Character Concept

Think of a concept you'll enjoy long-term. Your Story Master (SM) may restrict some options — get approval before committing.

Talk to your SM about the kind of game they intend to run. Ask if they suggest minimum Characteristics or Skills, or if they want a character with a specific Background (e.g., Holdings 3 for a spelljammer). Discuss expectations with your group — what kind of story you want, topics anyone finds distasteful, and how your characters fit together.

Having `4k2` in something is perfectly normal. `6k3` indicates a seasoned professional. `8k4` is among the best in the world. You can get a long way with high Characteristics and a single dot in many Skills.

---

## Step One: Starting Scores

### Characteristics

Nine Characteristics in three categories — prioritize groups as Primary, Secondary, Tertiary:

- **Physical:** Strength, Dexterity, Constitution
- **Social:** Charisma, Fellowship, Composure
- **Mental:** Intelligence, Wisdom, Willpower

All start at 1 dot. Distribute additional dots by priority:

| Priority  | Dots |
| --------- | ---- |
| Primary   | 6    |
| Secondary | 4    |
| Tertiary  | 2    |

:::tip[Optional Rule]
Three alternative groupings exist — Power (Intelligence, Strength, Charisma), Finesse (Wisdom, Dexterity, Fellowship), and Resilience (Willpower, Constitution, Composure). These can be used instead of the Physical/Social/Mental split.
:::

**Restriction:** No Characteristic above 4 in this step (racial bonuses may raise it to 5 later).

### Skills

Skills also split into Mental, Physical, and Social. All start at 0. Prioritize groups separately from Characteristics:

| Priority  | Dots |
| --------- | ---- |
| Primary   | 8    |
| Secondary | 6    |
| Tertiary  | 4    |

**Restriction:** No Skill above 3 in this step.

### Experience Point Buy Variant

Alternative method — Characteristics start at 1, spend XP instead of distributing dots. No starting XP in Step Seven; may reserve up to 10% for feats/assets/backgrounds.

| Power Level | Starting XP |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Low Power   | 4800 XP     |
| Mid Power   | 5200 XP     |
| High Power  | 5600 XP     |

## Step Five: Backgrounds

Backgrounds quantify your character's external advantages — allies, wealth, rank, etc. See [Backgrounds](./08-Backgrounds.mdx) for full descriptions.

- **Starting dots:** 7
- **Restriction:** No background above 3 without spending XP
- **XP for Backgrounds:** Can only be spent during character creation; dots above 3 cost double

For Backgrounds that require definition (Allies, Contacts, Backing, etc.), write down 4–5 descriptive words and share them with your SM. Example: Contacts 3 = "street urchins in Sigil's Hive Ward, information brokers."

| Background  | Description                                       |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Allies      | People willing to help you                        |
| Artifact    | A magical item (max 5 dots at character creation) |
| Backing     | Ranking member in an organization                 |
| Contacts    | Ability to find someone to help, at a price       |
| Fame        | How well people know of you                       |
| Followers   | Loyal assistants devoted to you                   |
| Holdings    | Large property like a business or ship            |
| Inheritance | Epic loot                                         |
| Mentor      | Someone guiding you on the Hero's path            |
| Status      | Other Heroes have heard of your deeds             |
| Wealth      | Liquid resources and expected lifestyle           |

## Step Seven: Experience

**Starting XP:** 600 — spend only on items from your Class List.

| Purchase                         | Cost                 |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Improve a Characteristic         | 100 × Current Rank   |
| Improve Devotion                 | 50 × Current Rank    |
| New Skill                        | 100                  |
| Improve a Skill                  | 50 × Current Rank    |
| New Sword School or Magic School | 200                  |
| Improve a Sword School           | 100 × Current Rank\* |
| Improve a Magic School           | 100 × Current Rank\* |
| Buy an Asset                     | 100\*\*              |
| Buy a Background                 | 50 per dot\*\*       |
| Improve Power Stat               | 200 × Current Rank\* |
| Buy a Feat                       | (varies)             |

**Key restrictions:**

- \* Power Stat, Magic Schools, and Fighting Styles capped at your current level
- \*\* Assets and new Background dots can **only** be purchased at character creation; Backgrounds above 3 dots cost double
- Some feats are creation-only purchases — see [Feats](./07-Feats.mdx)

## Step Nine: Finishing Touches

Calculate **derived statistics** from your final Characteristics:

| Stat           | Formula                                                   |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size           | Determined by race (4 for average human)                  |
| Static Defense | `10 + (Dex + Wis) × 3 − (Size × 2)`                     |
| Hit Points     | `(Con + Wil) × 2`                                        |
| Mental Defense | `5 + (5 × Composure)`                                    |
| Resolve        | `Wil + Composure`                                        |
| Speed          | `Str + Dex` (meters per half action; ×6 for run)         |
| Resilience     | Average of Size and Level (round up) + 1                  |
| Hero Points    | 2 (starting)                                              |

**Damage Calculation:** Divide incoming damage by Resilience to determine Hit Point loss and Critical Damage.

## Example Character Creation

Jane builds a **Tiefling Werewolf Barbarian** for melee combat.

1. **Characteristics:** Primary Physical (6 extra dots), Secondary Social (4), Tertiary Mental (2)
2. **Skills:** Primary Physical (8 dots), Secondary Mental (6), Tertiary Social (4)
3. **Race — [Tiefling](./04-Races.mdx):** +1 Constitution (now 4), +1 Intimidation, +1 Weaponry. Power: reroll 1s on damage
4. **Exaltation — [Werewolf](./05-Exaltations.mdx):** Shifting, Lycan Resistance, Fast Healing, Silver Bane. Feral Heart 1. Max Rage: 5
5. **Class — [Barbarian](./06-Classes.mdx):** Prereqs met (Weaponry 2, Athletics 1)
6. **[Backgrounds](./08-Backgrounds.mdx):** Contacts 3, Status 2, Backing 2 (Society of Sensation)
7. **Alignment:** Malal. Devotion: 6
8. **XP (600 total):**
   - Hindrances: Enemy, Impulsive (grant bonus XP)
   - Tiger Claw [Sword School](./12-Sword-Schools.mdx) Rank 1: 200 XP
   - Black Spiral Dancer asset: 100 XP
   - Appearance asset: 100 XP
   - Frenzy feat + Power Attack feat
   - Dexterity 2→3: 200 XP
9. **Equipment:** Air package
10. **Derived Stats:** Size 5, HP 12, Resolve 4, Static Defense 15, Speed 7, Hero Points 2

**Character Name:** Traya Psine Kaos

## Bringing Your Character to Life

The dots on your sheet define capabilities — not your character's appearance, history, or personality. Consider these questions before play begins (share the answers with your SM):

- What was your family life like? Parents, siblings, spouse?
- Where are you from? Region shapes appearance, language, customs.
- How did you become a Hero? What was your Exaltation like? Training or self-taught?
- When did you meet your companions? How long have you known them? Rivalries?
- How has power changed you?
- What do you think of mortals now that you command great power?
- What motivates you — riches, glory, revenge, duty, justice?
- What will you sacrifice? How far will you go?

## Character Optimization

Character optimization allows characters to excel in specific areas. In D:TD, characters are action heroes — "balance" is less critical. Each Exaltation is designed to be good in its own way. Classes matter less for final capability than Exaltation + race + feat choices.

### Key Optimization Principles

- **Veteran O' The Wheel** (100 XP + semi-hindrance): If it raises an attribute to 5 dots = 400 XP saved. Adding skill to 5 saves another 200 XP. Completely worth it for long games.
- **Low attributes = weak points:** High attributes provide both rolled and kept dice
- **Varied saves matter:** This revision spread saves across Size, Intelligence, Fellowship, Composure, Strength, etc. A combat specialist with 1s in mental/social suffers against Mind Flayer Stun (`8k5` Focus Power, TN 26+ save needed)
- **Take up to four Hindrances** for 1000 starting XP
- **Choose from all six attribute groups** (vertical and horizontal)
