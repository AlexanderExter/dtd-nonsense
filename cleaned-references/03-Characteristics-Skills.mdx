- 9 Characteristics (Physical / Social / Mental) — ordinary folk have 1–3 dots; Heroes can reach 5 (some 6)
- 27 Skills (9 Mental, 9 Physical, 9 Social) — Basic Skills can be attempted untrained at −1; Advanced Skills require at least 1 dot
- Standard Test (a skill check): roll `Skill + Characteristic` dice, keep `Characteristic` dice — e.g., `5k3` (roll 5 dice, keep highest 3)

- Derived stats use Characteristics — see [Character Creation](./02-Char-Creation.mdx) for formulas

## Characteristics

**Characteristics** represent innate capacities. Ordinary folk have 1–3 dots; Heroes can reach 5 (sometimes 6). Every Characteristic and Skill gains a **Specialty** at 4 dots — reroll any 1s when it applies. The SM decides when a Specialty is relevant.

### Characteristics Reference

| Characteristic | Category | Used For | Example Specialties |
| -------------- | -------- | -------- | ------------------- |
| **Strength**   | Physical | Lifting, melee damage, jumping | Iron Grip, Reserves of Strength, Fists Like Anvils, Hurting People |
| **Dexterity**  | Physical | Agility, aim, reflexes, Static Defense | Fast Movement, Feline Grace, Lightning Reflexes, Perfect Aim |
| **Constitution** | Physical | Toughness, Hit Points, endurance | Tough as Nails, Tireless, Resolute, Stoic |
| **Charisma**   | Social | Force of personality, leadership | Eloquent Speaker, Catchphrases, Genteel, Cutting Wit |
| **Fellowship** | Social | Manipulation, persuasion, appearance | Persuasive, Seductive, Well-Reasoned, Charming Rogue |
| **Composure**  | Social | Emotional control, staying calm | Poise, Dignity, Unflappable, Fearless |
| **Intelligence** | Mental | Memory, reasoning, bonus languages (3+ dots = extra languages) | Trivia, Puzzle Solver, Book Knowledge, Creativity |
| **Wisdom**     | Mental | Alertness, perception, insight | Attentive, Insightful, Experienced, Discerning |
| **Willpower**  | Mental | Mental resistance, resolve | Mental Resolve, Ambushes |

### Willpower

**Willpower** describes how strongly your character can hold onto her own ideas and resist mental commands from others. This attribute is used to resist many supernatural effects and, as they say, willpower is nine-tenths of effort.

**Specialties:** Getting the Jump on Others, Mental Resolve, Meditation, Ambushes

| Dots  | Rating      | Description                                      |
| ----- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| ●     | Poor        | You're a follower, not a leader.                 |
| ●●    | Average     | You can hold out as long as the average person.  |
| ●●●   | Good        | You're seldom left surprised or speechless.      |
| ●●●●  | Exceptional | You can look death in the eye and make it blink. |
| ●●●●● | Outstanding | You are a bastion of mental power.               |

### Variant Rules & Guidance

> **Play Tip — Charisma vs. Fellowship:** Charisma is for short-term pushes and first impressions. Fellowship is for longer-term relationships. If you meet someone in a bar and they talk you into something, that's Charisma. If you still like them a year later and are willing to lend them your stuff, that's Fellowship.
>
> **Optional — Dodging:** By default, dodge attempts use Acrobatics + Dexterity, so untrained characters cannot dodge at all. Consider reserving the Advanced restriction for actual acrobatic stunts and treating basic dodging as a straight Dexterity roll.
>
> **Optional — Common Lore:** May be treated as a Basic Skill for general knowledge (local animals, basic cultural facts). Reserve the Advanced restriction for obscure or highly specific information.
>
> **Optional — Medicae:** May be treated as a Basic Skill for first aid and CPR. Reserve the Advanced restriction for surgery, cybernetics, and rare diseases.
>
> **Optional — Perception:** SM-initiated Perception Tests may ignore the untrained penalty. Player-initiated Tests remain penalized as Advanced.

## Skills

Skills represent training and education. Roll `Skill + Characteristic` dice, keep `Characteristic` dice.

- **Basic Skills** — can be attempted at 0 dots (−1 to Characteristic for the roll)
- **Advanced Skills** — require at least 1 dot to attempt

The SM can call for any Characteristic + Skill combination as the situation demands.

### Mental Skills

- **Academic Lore** *(Intelligence, Advanced)* — Scholarly subjects, research. **Specialties:** Archaic, Astromancy, Beasts, Cryptology, Heraldry, History, Law, Occult, Philosophy, Psychology
- **Arcana** *(Intelligence, Basic)* — Supernatural legends, distinguishing myth from fact. **Specialties:** Cultural Beliefs, Ghosts, Magic, Monsters, Superstitions
- **Common Lore** *(Intelligence, Advanced)* — Habits, institutions, traditions, public figures, and superstitions of a culture, organization, region, or race. **Specialties:** Sigil, Underworld, War, Tech, Animals, Specific Crystal Spheres, Specific Races
- **Crafts** *(Wisdom, Basic)* — Creating and repairing physical objects. Requires tools and workspace. **Specialties:** Forging, Painting, Sculpting, Sewing, Ship Repair, Jury-Rigging
- **Forbidden Lore** *(Intelligence, Advanced)* — Dangerous, often illegal knowledge. Possessing this skill can warrant death in some societies. **Specialties:** Cults, Daemonology, Warp, Heresy
- **Medicae** *(Wisdom, Advanced)* — Treating wounds and stabilizing allies. **Specialties:** First Aid, Pathology, Surgery, Pharmaceuticals
- **Perception** *(Wisdom, Basic)* — Noticing ambushes, traps, and hidden details across all senses. **Specialties:** Spot, Listen, Search, Taste, Touch
- **Politics** *(Wisdom, Advanced)* — Navigating bureaucracies, knowing who holds power. **Specialties:** Bribery, Elections, Specific Crystal Sphere, Local, Scandals
- **Tech-Use** *(Intelligence, Advanced)* — Operating, repairing, or reverse-engineering unfamiliar technology. **Specialties:** Computers, Repairing Machines, Reverse Engineering

### Physical Skills

- **Acrobatics** *(Dexterity, Advanced)* — Tumbling, flipping, gymnastic movement. **Specialties:** Jumping, Backflips, Totally Cool Awesome Gymnastics
- **Athletics** *(Constitution, Basic)* — Running, jumping, throwing, swimming, climbing, sports. **Specialties:** Swimming, Climbing, Endurance Running, Specific Sports
- **Ballistics** *(Special, Basic)* — Firearms and ranged weapons. Requires weapon proficiency [Feat](./07-Feats.mdx) for trained use. **Specialties:** Fast Draw, Pistols, Sniping, Shotguns
- **Brawl** *(Special, Basic)* — Unarmed attacks, grappling, martial arts. **Specialties:** Kung Fu, Karate, Jeet Kun Do, Solar Hero Style
- **Drive** *(Dexterity, Basic)* — Land and hover vehicles. Tests only for bad terrain, speed, or dangerous maneuvers. **Specialties:** Ground Vehicle, Hover Vehicle, Walker
- **Larceny** *(Dexterity, Basic)* — Palming, pickpocketing, lockpicking, bypassing security. **Specialties:** Lockpicking, Pickpocketing, Prestidigitation, Security Systems, Safecracking
- **Pilot** *(Dexterity, Advanced)* — Atmospheric craft to spelljamming ships. Tests for dangerous maneuvers or bad conditions. **Specialties:** Civilian Craft, Military Craft, Spelljamming Ship
- **Stealth** *(Dexterity, Basic)* — Hiding and sneaking. Requires cover or concealment. Opposed Test vs. Perception + Wisdom. **Specialties:** Concealment, Silent Movement, Shadowing, Crowds
- **Weaponry** *(Special, Basic)* — Melee weapons. Requires weapon proficiency [Feat](./07-Feats.mdx) for trained use. **Specialties:** Knives, Swords, Improvised Clubs, Martial Arts, Axes

### Social Skills

- **Animal Ken** *(Composure, Basic)* — Predicting animal behavior, training, riding, calming. **Specialties:** Riding, Animal Training, Big Cats, Horses, Farm Animals
- **Charm** *(Fellowship, Basic)* — Befriending, seducing, begging. Test when changing opinions or requesting favors. **Specialties:** Seduction, First Impressions, Begging
- **Command** *(Charisma, Basic)* — Ordering subordinates. Only works on those under your authority. **Specialties:** Oratory, Compelling, Open, Noble, Military
- **Deceive** *(Charisma, Basic)* — Lying, conning, misdirection. **Specialties:** White Lies, Con Jobs, Lying, Misdirection
- **Disguise** *(Fellowship, Basic)* — Altering appearance. Opposed by Perception Tests. **Specialties:** Mimicking Voices, Mannerisms, Mask of a Thousand Faces
- **Intimidation** *(Charisma, Basic)* — Scaring people. Can use any Characteristic depending on approach. **Specialties:** Bluster, Physical Threats, Stare-Downs, Torture
- **Performer** *(Fellowship, Basic)* — Entertaining and enthralling. **Specialties:** Dancer, Musician, Singer, Storyteller
- **Persuasion** *(Charisma, Basic)* — Getting your point across clearly. **Specialties:** Acting, Poetry, Fiction, Impromptu, Conversation
- **Scrutiny** *(Composure, Basic)* — Assessing others, spotting lies, sensing motives. Not telepathy. **Specialties:** Spotting Lies, Motives, Personalities, Emotion
