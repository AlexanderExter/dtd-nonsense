> **Key points:**
> 
> - Sorcerers channel the Warp through nine **Magical Schools**, each tied to a Characteristic
> - Three **Psychic Strength** levels control risk vs. power: Fettered (safe), Unfettered (moderate risk), Push (dangerous)
> - Casting uses a **Focus Power Test** — roll School + Characteristic against the spell's TN (Target Number)
> - Dice notation: `5k3` means roll 5 dice, keep the best 3. Exploding 10s (kept dice showing 10) trigger Psychic Phenomena unless Fettered
> - Spell effects **do not stack** — only the highest applies. See [Combat](14-Combat.mdx) for action economy

## Sorcerer Types

- **Astropaths** — transmit messages across space via the Warp
- **Sanctioned Sorcerers** — officially trained spellcasters serving militaries, merchant houses, or organizations
- **Navigators** — navigate Warp travel using magical abilities (often blind)
- **Apostates** — unsanctioned, untrained sorcerers operating outside official channels
- **Untouchables** — individuals with no Warp presence who dampen magical activity

**Sanctioned** sorcerers are officially trained; **Unsanctioned** sorcerers are self-taught with greater Warp risks.

## Casting Spells — Step by Step

1. **Choose a spell** you know from your Magical Schools
2. **Choose Psychic Strength** — Fettered, Unfettered, or Push (see table below)
3. **Make a Focus Power Test** — roll your Magic School rating + the spell's Key Characteristic
4. **Compare to the spell's TN** — meet or exceed to succeed. Each full 5 above TN is a **raise** (extra effect)
5. **Check for Psychic Phenomena** — if Unfettered or Pushing and you kept any exploding dice (10s), roll on the Psychic Phenomena table
6. **Apply effects** — even if Phenomena trigger, the spell succeeds if you beat the TN

### Psychic Strength Table

| Psychic Strength | Sanctioned Sorcerer | Unsanctioned Sorcerer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fettered** | Halve rolled dice. No Phenomena roll | Halve rolled dice. No Phenomena roll |
| **Unfettered** | Phenomena if keeping any exploded dice | Phenomena at +5 per spell level if keeping any exploded dice |
| **Push** | +1 to +3 to School rating. Phenomena at +5 per die added (max +15) | +1 to +4 to School rating. Phenomena at +10 per die added (max +40) |

- **Fettered** — halve rolled dice (may also reduce kept dice if rolled drops below starting kept). Completely safe
- **Push** — gain enhancement to effective Magic School rating. If multiple sources increase rating, only the highest applies. Can exceed rating 6

### Key Casting Rules

- **Focus Power Test** uses the spell's Magical School + a Characteristic (determines kept dice). Some spells use a **Power Stat** instead for kept dice
- **Cumulative effects** do not stack — modifiers, Characteristic Bonuses, and spell-generated effects use only the highest value
- **Range and Line of Sight** — unless noted, spells targeting individuals require line of sight or awareness of the target, plus the target must be within stated range
- **Detecting magic** — sorcerers can Test **Arcana + Wisdom (TN 20)** when spells are active in their presence to identify the source

## Spell Format Reference

Each spell entry contains:

- **Name** — the spell's common name
- **School (Level)** — which school and what spell level (1–5)
- **Focus Power Test** — School + Characteristic to roll
- **Target Number** — TN to activate; many spells gain strength with raises (each raise = 5 above TN)
- **Action** — Half Action, Full Action, or Reaction Action
- **Keywords** — special properties (see table below)
- **Duration** — how long the spell lasts

### Keywords

| Keyword | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Attack** | Counts as an attack against the target |
| **Combo-OK** | Can be used to build a Combo |
| **Focus** | Requires a material focus; if destroyed, spell ends instantly |
| **Language Dependent** | Caster and target must share a common language |
| **Material** | Requires material components consumed in casting |
| **Mind-Affecting** | Only works on targets with minds (animals and AIs: yes; robots and zombies: no) |
| **Ranged Touch** | Requires hitting target with ranged attack (Level + Ballistics) |
| **Saving Throw** | Target can Test to resist. TN equals Focus Power result unless specified |
| **Non-Combat** | Cannot be used in combat without becoming Helpless until start of next turn |
| **Somatic** | Cannot be used if bound, grappled, or unable to move freely |
| **Subtle** | Casting and effect not obvious. Others can Test Arcana + Wisdom (TN 20) to notice |
| **Touch** | Requires touching target; unwilling targets require unarmed attack (Brawl) |
| **Verbal** | Cannot be used if unable to speak; must speak clearly and loudly |

### Duration Types

- **Rounds/Scenes/Days** — real time units
- **Indefinite** — lasts until caster cancels
- **Instant** — happens immediately
- **Concentration** — requires action each round to maintain; **(E)** means can be expended for a one-time benefit

## Learning Spells

When gaining a dot in a spell school, learn **one spell** in that school of the appropriate level. Some Feats (see [Feats](07-Feats.mdx)) allow additional spells — notably **Spell Focus** and related magic Feats. Once chosen, spells cannot be changed.

**Classes that grant magic:** Many [Classes](06-Classes.mdx) provide dots in Magical Schools. Consult class progression tables for specifics.

## Spell Combos

Experienced sorcerers can combine two or more spells into a single action.

### Developing Combos

- Cannot be purchased during character creation
- **XP Cost:** 50 XP per level of each spell in the combo
- Story Master approval required

### Combo Rules

- **Test** — uses the **lowest** Characteristic and Magic School rating among all spells
- **TN** — highest TN spell + 5 per additional spell
- **Cannot be Fettered** — usually requires Pushing for success
- **Psychic Phenomena** — add +5 per spell in combo (plus Pushing/Unsanctioned modifiers)
- **Action** — equals the longest action among spells in combo
- **Duration/Range** — handled separately per spell
- **Components** — if any spell requires Focus/Verbal/Somatic/Material, all must be provided or combo fails
- **Spell-Specific Effects** — Language Dependency, Mind-Affecting, and Attack status are per-effect. Failed saves only negate that portion
- **No Duplicates** — same spell cannot appear twice in a combo

## Abjuration

*Protective spells — barriers, negation, wards, and counterspelling. Key Characteristic: **Willpower**.*

### Shield

- **Abjuration 1** · **TN 10** · Reaction Action · Somatic
- Use the Focus Power result as a Parry Test

### Armoring Aura

- **Abjuration 1** · **TN 10** · Half Action · Somatic, Touch, Combo-OK · One Scene
- Touched creature gains **Aura 1**. +1 Aura per raise (max 3× your Level)

### Voidskin

- **Abjuration 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Somatic · One Scene
- Become immune to vacuum, pressure, radiation. Functions as any extreme environment suit (diving, hazmat, lava walking, etc.)

### Knock & Lock

- **Abjuration 2** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Somatic, Touch · Instant
- **Knock:** Unlock a touched door; all non-magical locks open. Beat a magical lock's Focus Power by two raises
- **Lock:** Magically seal a touched door (adds Non-Combat keyword). Normal unlock methods fail. Can set a passphrase to unlock. Locked doors can still be kicked in or blown up

### Mage Armor

- **Abjuration 2** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Somatic, Touch, Combo-OK · One Day
- Touched creature gains **Armor Points equal to your Level**. +2 AP per raise (max 3× your Level). Does not stack with other armor but requires no proficiency

### Endure Elements

- **Abjuration 2** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Somatic, Verbal · 24 hours
- No weather penalties. Comfortable in extreme temperatures. **Halves damage** from energy sources (fire, lasers, plasma, electricity, cold — NOT acid, disintegration, warp vortexes, psychic pain). Applied after Armor/Aura reduction

### Glyph

- **Abjuration 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Focus (small gem), Somatic, Touch · One Day
- Touch an object/person to create a Glyph. Cast spells at the Glyph without line of effect (must be within range). Example: throw Glyph-rock over wall, cast Fireball targeting it

### Dispel

- **Abjuration 3** · **TN 15** · Half Action or Reaction · Somatic · Instant
- **Counter** (Reaction): counter a spell being cast. **Dispel** (Half Action): end an active spell. Range: 50m per Level. TN to stop = opponent's Focus Power result

### Globe of Invulnerability

- **Abjuration 3** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Somatic, Verbal · One Scene
- Become immune to Level 1–2 spells. Cannot be targeted by them and ignores their area effects. Does not block indirect effects (enemy's Shield can still parry your attacks, enemy's Boon still applies against you)

### Wall of Force

- **Abjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Somatic, Verbal · One Scene
- Create a 20m × 5m force plane within 50m. +5m length or +1m height per raise. Any orientation (flat, vertical, slanted). Effectively indestructible. Cannot cause damage — stops short of intersecting creatures/objects. Transparent or opaque (caster's choice). Blocks teleportation if fully enclosing an area

### Exploding Runes

- **Abjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Touch, Somatic, Non-Combat · Indefinite (E)
- Inscribe runes on an object. Anyone reading them (except caster and named exemptions) causes explosion: `4k4`+10 magical E damage within 3m. Inscribed object is unharmed

### Ruby Ray

- **Abjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Reaction or Half Action · Verbal, Touch · Instant
- More powerful, limited Dispel. **Shield mode** (Half Action): reflects the next single-target spell at the shielded target before your next turn. **Reaction mode**: protects the caster only. Reflected spell rebounds at original caster if within 200m

### Contingency

- **Abjuration 5** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Non-Combat, Somatic, Touch · Indefinite (E)
- Cast another spell immediately after — it doesn't activate until a chosen trigger occurs. Targets chosen at cast time (can be vague: "what I'm pointing at"). One Contingency active at a time. Can be placed on other people or objects up to large airplane size

### Disjunction

- **Abjuration 5** · **TN 35** · Reaction · Verbal · Instant
- As Dispel with 500m range. On success, target cannot cast spells until end of your next turn (+1 turn per raise). Five or more raises: target cannot cast for rest of scene

## Conjuration

*Moving objects, creatures, and energy through the Warp — teleportation, summoning, and portal creation. Key Characteristic: **Willpower**.*

### Invisible Servant

- **Conjuration 1** · **TN 15** · Full Action · Non-Combat, Verbal · One Day
- Summon invisible animating force. Performs tasks not requiring Tests. Rating 1 in each Characteristic. +5 TN per additional copy. TN 15 Perception to spot

### Call Item

- **Conjuration 1** · **TN 15** · Full Action (cast), Free Action (recall) · Touch, Non-Combat, Verbal · Indefinite
- Cast on a handheld item. Recall it from any distance as a Free Action. One item at a time. Cannot cross Crystal Sphere boundaries. Max ~1.5m length, 15 kg mass

### Web

- **Conjuration 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Attack, Combo-OK, Verbal, Somatic, Material (cobwebs/sticky candy), Saving Throw · Instant
- Choose point within 25m. All targets within 10m save Arcana + Dexterity or be **Immobilized** for rounds equal to Caster Level. Creatures entering the area get no save. Stuck creatures may Test Strength or Size vs. Focus Power to move at half speed

### Blink

- **Conjuration 2** · **TN 20** · Half Action · Verbal, Somatic · Instant
- Teleport to a visible spot within 40m

### Lesser Servant

- **Conjuration 2** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Material (candle), Somatic, Combo-OK · One minute per Caster Level
- Summon creature with 2 in all Characteristics and 2 ranks in relevant skills. Appears within arm's reach

### Obscuring Fog

- **Conjuration 2** · **TN 20** · Half Action · Verbal, Somatic, Combo-OK · Instant
- Create up to 3 cubes (5m×5m×5m) per Caster Level. Visibility reduced to 1m. Dissipates in ~5 minutes (faster with wind/sunlight)

### Porte

- **Conjuration 3** · **TN 20** · Half Action or Reaction · Somatic · One hour per Caster Level
- Create a portal (person-sized) on a visible flat surface within 100m. Must cast twice to create both ends. Entering one exits the other instantly. Momentum conserved. If surface moves, portal vanishes. Max 2 portals open at once

### Jaunt

- **Conjuration 3** · **TN 25** · Reaction · Verbal, Combo-OK · Instant
- Teleport up to 10m. If used as Reaction to an attack, counts as a Dodge attempt

### Cloudkill

- **Conjuration 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Verbal, Somatic · Instant
- Create poison gas cloud: 1 cube (5m³) per Caster Level. Visibility 2m. Deals magical E damage `(Level + Conjuration)k(Level)` when targets start or end turn inside. Breathing requires Constitution vs. TN 15 or take a Wound (crits go to gizzards). Lasts 3 minutes + 1 per raise

### Greater Servant

- **Conjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Material (candle), Somatic, Combo-OK · One minute per Caster Level
- As Lesser Servant: 4 in all Characteristics and 4 in all relevant skills

### Teleport

- **Conjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal · Instant
- As Blink with no max distance — teleport anywhere you've seen before. Unfamiliar destinations require Wisdom + Perception vs. TN 25 (SM may raise TN). Cannot cross Crystal Spheres or enter shielded ships. +1 Size worth of passengers per raise (must hold them; whole creature or nothing)

### Black Blade of Disaster

- **Conjuration 4** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Verbal, Somatic · One Scene
- Create a dimensional rift sword (Power Sword profile). +`1k0` damage per raise. Properties: Balanced, Toxic, Shocking. **Ignores Armor** (Aura still applies, reduced by penetration). Use Arcana in place of Level for sword Tests

### Gate

- **Conjuration 5** · **TN 35** · Full Action or 1 hour · Somatic, Combo-OK (combat only) · One hour per Caster Level
- **Combat** (Full Action, Combo-OK): as Porte but creates both portals at once, lasting at least until start of your next turn
- **Ritual** (1 hour, Non-Combat): create portal pair almost anywhere — even unseen locations (TN 25 Wisdom + Perception, TN 50 for known-only locations). Portals survive being moved. Cannot cross Crystal Spheres or enter shielded ships

### Incendiary Cloud

- **Conjuration 5** · **TN 35** · Full Action · Verbal, Somatic · Instant
- As Obscuring Fog but: 5 cubes per Conjuration dot, unaffected by wind/sunlight. Deals magical E damage `(Level + Conjuration)k(Conjuration)` on turn start/end and on entry. Sets everything On Fire (even fire-immune things). Lasts 1 minute + 1 per raise

## Divination

*Reading past, present, and future — fate manipulation, scrying, and telepathy. Key Characteristic: **Wisdom**.*

### Augury

- **Divination 1** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Material (dice/cards/runes), Somatic, Non-Combat · Instant
- Get a general sense of whether your current course will have positive or negative results. Failure = inconclusive. Once per hour; +10 TN per additional attempt within 12 hours

### Whisper

- **Divination 1** · **TN 15** · Free Action · Verbal, Combo-OK, Subtle · One Scene (E)
- Send and receive short messages (sentence-length) to a visible target. One message each way + 1 per raise. Only caster and target can hear

### Detect Thoughts

- **Divination 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Somatic, Subtle, Combo-OK, Mind-Affecting · One Round
- Detect surface thoughts and emotions. If Focus Power exceeds target's Mental Defense: more specific thoughts. Raises equal to target's Willpower: answer one specific question (if they know it)

### Luck

- **Divination 2** · **TN 20** · Half Action · Somatic, Combo-OK, Subtle · One Scene (E)
- Gain 1 extra floating rolled die to apply to any action. +1 floating die per raise. Use up to your Level on any one roll. Dice vanish when used

### Foresee

- **Divination 2** · **TN 15** · Reaction · Verbal · Instant
- Use this spell's result in place of a Dodge Test

### Arcane Eye

- **Divination 2** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Material (creature eyeball), Verbal, Non-Combat · One hour per Level + 1 hour per raise
- Create invisible flying sensor (Speed 8, unlimited range). Visual capacity matches source creature. Bionic eyes don't work. Eyeball destroyed when spell ends

### Scry

- **Divination 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Verbal, Somatic, Non-Combat, Saving Throw, Focus (mirror/crystal ball), Subtle · Concentration (Half)
- Requires object or body part from the target. Range: several light years. Target resists with Arcana + Willpower. See and hear target + surroundings out to 10m. Half Action each round to maintain

### Precognition

- **Divination 3** · **TN 25** · Half Action · Somatic, Combo-OK, Subtle · One Scene (E)
- Reroll one failed roll. +1 reroll per raise. Use up to your Level on any one Test. Stacks with other reroll abilities

### Legend Lore

- **Divination 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Non-Combat, Somatic, Touch · Instant
- Touch an item to learn its history, abilities, and famous owners. Three+ raises: see historical events surrounding the object as if present. Choose specific events, dates, or time periods

### Unluck

- **Divination 4** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Attack, Combo-OK, Saving Throw · One Scene
- Target within 10m loses 1 rolled die from all rolls (+1 per raise). Resist: Arcana + Willpower

### Mind Net

- **Divination 4** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Mind-Affecting, Subtle, Language Dependent, Saving Throw · One Scene
- Create telepathic network: caster + up to 5 people within 200m. Silent, instant communication. Unwilling targets save Arcana + Wisdom. All messages are intentional and go to entire network. Cannot read minds

### Foresight

- **Divination 4** · **TN 30** · Full Action · Somatic, Verbal, Non-Combat · Instant (E)
- Gain vague insight into the next significant threat. SM gives preparation hints. When the danger triggers: cannot be surprised, automatically win Initiative. One instance active at a time

### Commune

- **Divination 5** · **TN 35** · Full Action · Focus (scrying pool/crystal ball), Non-Combat, Language Dependent, Verbal, Material (incense) · One minute per Level
- Begin conversation with anyone you name, regardless of distance or barriers. Target may end it at any time

### True Knowledge

- **Divination 5** · **TN 35** · Full Action · Somatic, Verbal, Focus (encyclopedia) · One round per Level + 1 per raise
- All skill/attribute rolls gain additional rolled dice equal to Divination dots used. Non-skill rolls (Alignment Tests, Perils): roll twice, use better result. Count as having at least 1 dot in all attributes and skills

## Enchantment

*Affecting minds — influence, control, and manipulation. Key Characteristic: **Charisma**. See [Advanced Magic Rules](#general-mind-control-rules) for mind control save mechanics.*

### Charm Person

- **Enchantment 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Mind-Affecting, Non-Combat, Subtle, Saving Throw, Language Dependent · One Scene
- Target treats you as an old friend: +`2k0` to social rolls, +`0k1` per two raises. Resist: Arcana + Willpower. Good treatment may maintain friendship after spell ends

### Command

- **Enchantment 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Attack, Combo-OK, Language Dependent, Mind-Affecting, Saving Throw, Verbal · Instant
- One-word command; target obeys with next action if failing Arcana + Willpower save. Self-destructive commands auto-resisted. Failed by <2 checks: spend Hero Point to misunderstand, or Resource Point to obey literally

### Shock and Awe

- **Enchantment 1** · **TN 15** · Half Action · Attack, Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal · Instant
- Choose point within 20m. Everyone within 10m loses 5 Initiative. If already acted and this gives them an extra turn, they skip it

### Stun

- **Enchantment 2** · **TN 20** · Half Action · Attack, Somatic, Saving Throw, Combo-OK · Instant
- Target within 10m Tests Arcana + Composure or be **Stunned** until end of next turn. After un-Stunning: 1 check = lose next Half Action; 2 checks = lose both Half Actions

### Attraction

- **Enchantment 2** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Mind-Affecting, Saving Throw, Non-Combat, Subtle, Touch · One Hour
- Touched object/creature draws attention within 30m. On a person: +`1k1` to all social rolls; people interact eagerly. Resist: Arcana + Wisdom

### Confusion

- **Enchantment 2** · **TN 20** · Full Action · Attack, Verbal, Combo-OK, Mind-Affecting · One Scene
- Target saves Arcana + Intelligence. On failure, roll `1d10` each turn: 1–4 self-harm (1 Wound), 5–6 does nothing, 7–9 acts normally, 10 spell breaks. Cannot stack with other confusion effects

### Dominate

- **Enchantment 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Attack, Verbal, Saving Throw, Language Dependent, Mind-Affecting · Concentration (Half)
- Control target within 10m. Resist: Arcana + Willpower. Actions against nature: +1 free raise on save. Self-destructive/ally-harmful: +2 free raises. New saves triggered by more extreme commands

### Awe

- **Enchantment 3** · **TN 25** · Full Action · Attack, Somatic, Saving Throw, Combo-OK · Instant
- As Stun, but affects all creatures within 30m radius (not the caster)

### Instill Desire

- **Enchantment 3** · **TN 25** · Half Action · Subtle, Non-Combat, Verbal, Mind-Affecting, Language Dependent · Concentration (Half)
- Target within 30m saves Arcana + Composure or desperately wants what caster names. +1 raise on save if desire opposes personality/Alignment. Target acts immediately to fulfill desire through their own chosen methods

### Encore

- **Enchantment 4** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Mind-Affecting, Verbal, Saving Throw · One round per Level
- Target within 30m Tests Arcana + Wisdom each turn or repeats last turn's actions

### Designated Target

- **Enchantment 4** · **TN 30** · Half Action · Attack, Somatic, Saving Throw, Combo-OK · One round per Level
- Target within 40m becomes everyone's hated enemy. Resist: Arcana + Fellowship. Even allies treat them as an immediate lethal threat

### Enchant an Item

## Advanced Magic Rules

### General Mind Control Rules

Mind control saves use this framework:

- **Failed by 10+ points (2+ checks):** Must follow spirit and intent of command
- **Failed by <10 points:** May spend a Resource Point (obey the letter, cannot intentionally misunderstand) or Hero Point (may misunderstand the command)
- **Ongoing control:** Requires Resource/Hero Point expenditure every round to subvert

**Example:** Compelled to "Disarm" — failed by 10+, you divest all weapons. Failed by <10, spend a Resource Point to attempt disarming someone else. Spend a Hero Point to interpret "dis-arm" as removing the caster's arm.

### Do I Know I Saved?

Successfully saving against mind control feels strange unless the spell has the **Subtle** keyword. Identify the influence with:

- **Arcana + Intelligence** TN 15
- **Forbidden Lore + Wisdom** TN 20
- **Free raise** if previously targeted by mind control

Subtle spells do not allow identification attempts.

### General Illusion Rules

- Visual illusions include appropriate shadows, reflections, and footprints by default
- Audible illusions include appropriate echoes
- Quality fools casual observers unless the caster has nearly zero reference information
- A monster atlas entry + good visual reference = acceptable creature illusions

**Detection:** If spell lacks specific guidance, use Focus Power as TN for both:

- **Scrutiny + Wisdom** Test
- **Perception + Intelligence** Test

Making one but not both allows suspicion (could be inflatables, disguised robots, holograms, painted walls).

**Additional properties:**

- Illusions appear on recordings and audiovisual equipment
- They fool robots and zombies reliably
- Obvious reality breaks (walking through walls) give away the fakery but don't indicate magic
- Identified illusions don't become transparent — an illusionary wall still blocks vision

## Safe Casting Reference

### The Actual Risk of Warp Events

Many players overestimate the danger. Here's the real math:

**Fettered casting** is always safe — no Phenomena roll. Success rates:

- `2k2` Fettered: 60% at TN 10, 25% at TN 15
- `3k3` Fettered: 90% at TN 10, 66% at TN 15
- `4k4` Fettered: 95% at TN 10, 90% at TN 15
- `5k5` Fettered: 99% at TN 10, 95% at TN 15

**Unfettered casting** — only triggers Phenomena if you keep exploded dice (10s):

| Roll | Safe (no 10s kept) | Warp Event Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `7k7` | ~85% | ~15% |
| `8k6` | ~96% | ~4% |

### Warp Table Danger Levels

**Psychic Phenomena (non-Perils results):**

- ~15% chance of insanity, wounds, or fear effects
- ~(26+modifier)% chance of escalating to Perils table
- ~59% or less "cosmetic" results (smells, frost, echoes)

**Perils of the Warp:**

- **1%** instant death
- **30%** Insanity effects
- **25%** direct damage
- **27%** incapacitation (unconscious, catatonic, teleported)
- **12%** casting restrictions
- **5%** area effects
- Every Perils roll has (25+modifier)/100 chance of instant death

### Maximum Safety Build

**Dragon-blooded Eldarin Apprentice** with implement and Wraithbone bionic heart: roll `8k5`, reroll one die, roll four times on Warp tables at +0 and pick preferred result. **~0.09% Warp chance** (~1 in 1,000 casts).

## Spell Revision Design Notes

Design commentary from the revised edition explaining why spells changed. Not rules — included for SM context when adjudicating edge cases.

- **Baseline spells:** 5th-level Evocation **Meteor Storm** and 5th-level Healing **Resurrection** define what a 5th-level spell is worth
- **Non-combat spells are dangerous during combat:** Caster is helpless until next turn; casting is a Full Action
- **Diversified saving throws:** Added Int, Dex, Com, Fel, Wis, Size, Str, Devotion saves (reduces Willpower's dominance)
- **Reduced frustration:** Illusion and mind control spells have clearer resolution
- **Attack touch spells last through caster's next turn:** Fixes timing with opportunity attacks
- **Non-attack touch spells provoke opportunity attacks when used offensively:** Buffs like Transformation that can also harm
