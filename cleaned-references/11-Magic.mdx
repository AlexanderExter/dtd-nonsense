# Magic

## Overview

Sorcerers draw power from the Warp and shape it into magical effects. They may be **Sanctioned** (officially trained) or **Unsanctioned** (self-taught, with greater warp risks).

**Sorcerer Types:**

- **Astropaths:** Transmit messages across space via the Warp
- **Sanctioned Sorcerers:** Officially trained spellcasters serving militaries, merchant houses, or organizations
- **Navigators:** Navigate Warp travel using magical abilities (often blind)
- **Apostates:** Unsanctioned, untrained sorcerers operating outside official channels
- **Untouchables:** Individuals with no warp presence who dampen magical activity

## Casting Spells

When casting a spell, choose one of three **Psychic Strength** levels:

- **Fettered:** Roll fewer dice but no risk of psychic phenomena
- **Unfettered:** Roll full dice but risk psychic phenomena
- **Push:** Greater effect but increased danger

Warp Phenomena and Perils of the Warp don't necessarily mean the spell failed - as long as you beat the spell's TN, it still goes off.

### Fettered Spells

Halve the number of rolled dice. This may also reduce kept dice if rolled dice drop below starting kept dice.

### Pushing Spells

Gain an enhancement to effective Magic School rating:

- **Sanctioned Sorcerers:** Add up to +3 to Magic School rating
- **Unsanctioned Sorcerers:** Add up to +4 to Magic School rating (greater risk)

Any push can result in a school rating greater than 6. If multiple sources increase the rating, only the highest applies.

### Psychic Strength Table

| Psychic Strength | Sanctioned Sorcerer                                                | Unsanctioned Sorcerer                                                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fettered         | No roll for psychic phenomena                                      | No roll for psychic phenomena                                                       |
| Unfettered       | If keeping any exploded dice, roll on Psychic Phenomena table      | If keeping any exploded dice, roll on Psychic Phenomena table at +5 per spell level |
| Push             | Roll on Psychic Phenomena table at +5 per die desired (max +3/+15) | Roll on Psychic Phenomena table at +10 per die desired (max +4/+40)                 |

### Focus Power

Focus Power is a Test using the spell's Magical School and a Characteristic. The characteristic determines the number of kept dice. Some spells require a Magic School and a Power Stat - in these cases, the Power Stat determines kept dice.

### Cumulative Effects

Modifiers, Characteristic Bonuses, and other effects generated by spells do not stack; only the highest applies.

### Range and Line of Sight

Unless noted, spells targeted against individuals require line of sight or awareness of the target, plus the target must be within the spell's stated range.

### Detecting Magical Effects

Sorcerers can Test **Arcana + Wisdom (TN 20)** when spells are in effect in their presence to determine their source.

## Magical Schools

There are nine Magical Schools:

- **Abjuration (Willpower):** Protective spells creating barriers, negating abilities, harming trespassers, or creating trigger points
- **Conjuration (Willpower):** Moving objects, creatures, or energy through the warp. Summoned things must arrive in an open location on a supporting surface
- **Divination (Wisdom):** Reading past, present, and future; influencing fate and destiny
- **Enchantment (Charisma):** Affecting minds, influencing or controlling behavior. Can be resisted with willpower
- **Evocation (Charisma):** Manipulating energy to produce spectacular effects; deals large amounts of damage
- **Healing (Wisdom):** Enhancing abilities, granting superhuman strengths, curing deadly wounds
- **Illusion (Intelligence):** Deceiving senses or minds
- **Necromancy (Intelligence):** Manipulating death, unlife, and life force. Heavily used by Vampires
- **Transmutation (Wisdom):** Spells of change, drawing on the caster's mutable core

## Learning Spells

When gaining a dot in a spell school, learn one spell in that school of the appropriate level. Some feats allow additional spells. Once chosen, spells cannot be changed.

## Spell Format

Each spell entry contains:

- **Name:** The spell's common name
- **School (Level):** The school and spell level
- **Focus Power Test:** The Test required (school + characteristic). Keeping 10s invokes Psychic Phenomena unless Fettered
- **Target Number:** The TN to activate. Many spells gain strength with raises
- **Action:** Half Action, Full Action, or Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Special properties (see below)
- **Duration:** How long the spell lasts

## Keywords

| Keyword                | Effect                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack**             | Counts as an attack against the target                                            |
| **Combo-OK**           | Can be used to build a Combo                                                      |
| **Focus**              | Requires a material focus; if destroyed, spell ends instantly                     |
| **Language Dependent** | Caster and target must share a common language                                    |
| **Material**           | Requires material components consumed in casting                                  |
| **Mind-Affecting**     | Only works on targets with minds (animals and AIs: yes; robots and zombies: no)   |
| **Ranged Touch**       | Requires hitting target with ranged attack (Level + Ballistics)                   |
| **Saving Throw**       | Target can Test to resist. TN equals Focus Power result unless specified          |
| **Non-Combat**         | Cannot be used in combat without becoming Helpless until start of next turn       |
| **Somatic**            | Cannot be used if bound, grappled, or unable to move freely                       |
| **Subtle**             | Casting and effect not obvious. Others can Test Arcana + Wisdom (TN 20) to notice |
| **Touch**              | Requires touching target; unwilling targets require unarmed attack (Brawl)        |
| **Verbal**             | Cannot be used if unable to speak; must speak clearly and loudly                  |

### Duration Types

- **Rounds/Scenes/Days:** Real time units
- **Indefinite:** Lasts until caster cancels
- **Instant:** Happens immediately
- **Concentration:** Requires action each round to maintain; (E) means can be expended

## Spell Combos

With practice, sorcerers can combine two or more spells into a single action.

### Developing Combos

- Cannot be purchased during character creation
- **XP Cost:** 50 xp per level of each spell in the combo
- Story Master approval required

### Combo Rules

- **Test:** Uses the lowest characteristic and magic school rating among all spells
- **TN:** Highest TN spell + 5 per additional spell
- **Cannot be Fettered:** Usually requires Pushing for success
- **Psychic Phenomena:** Add +5 to roll per spell in combo (plus Pushing/Unsanctioned modifiers)
- **Action:** Equals the longest action among spells in combo
- **Duration/Range:** Handled separately per spell
- **Components:** If any spell requires Focus/Verbal/Somatic/Material, all must be provided or combo fails
- **Spell-Specific Effects:** Language Dependency, Mind-Affecting, and Attack status are per-effect. Failed saves only negate that portion
- **No Duplicates:** Same spell cannot be used twice in a combo

## Abjuration

### Shield

**Abjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 10
- **Action:** Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic
- **Duration:** Instant

You may use the result of this spell as a Parry Test.

### Armoring Aura

**Abjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 10
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Touch, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** One Scene

The touched creature gains Aura 1. For every raise you get on the Focus Power Test, the value of the granted Aura goes up by 1, to a maximum Aura of three times your Level.

### Voidskin

**Abjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic
- **Duration:** One Scene

You become immune to the ravages of space. While the spell is active it provides you with all the air you need to breathe and protects you against pressure and radiation. Voidskin also works as a diving suit, Dune still-suit, lava walking suit, hazmat suit, or any other sort of extreme environment survival suit.

### Knock & Lock

**Abjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 20
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Touch, see text
- **Duration:** Instant

You unlock a touched door, opening all non-magical locks on it. You can unlock a magically locked door by beating the other caster's Focus Power Test to lock it by two raises.

You may magically lock the touched door, making all normal methods of unlocking it automatically fail, but this applies the Non-Combat keyword to the spell. When locking a door in this manner you have the option of designating some word, phrase, or action that will cause it to unlock and end the spell. Magically locked doors are still vulnerable to being kicked in or blown up.

### Mage Armor

**Abjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Touch, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** One Day

The touched creature gains Armor Points equal to your Level. For every raise you get on the Focus Power Test, the value of the granted Armor Points goes up by 2, to a maximum AP of three times your Level. The armor provided by this spell does not stack with any other armors, but it requires no proficiency to use.

### Endure Elements

**Abjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 25
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
- **Duration:** 24 hours

You may comfortably exist in most natural environments. You don't suffer penalties to your actions from weather conditions, and you're comfortable even in very hot or cold weather. The spell also halves damage from the appropriate energy sources (fire, lasers, plasma, electricity, cold, etc., but NOT acid, disintegration, warp vortexes, psychic pain, etc.) even if they are magical or spell effects. Apply this halving after the damage is reduced by Armor or Aura.

### Glyph

**Abjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 25
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Focus, Somatic, Touch
- **Duration:** One day
- **Focus:** A small gem

You touch an object or person and create a Glyph. You may cast spells at the glyph without having line of effect, though it must still be within range of the spell or have a range of Touch. For example, you could put a glyph on a rock, throw it over a wall, then cast Fireball targeting the Glyph, exploding anything behind the wall without poking your head over to look.

### Dispel

**Abjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Half Action or Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic
- **Duration:** Instant

You may use this spell as a Reaction to counter a spell being cast, or as a Half Action to dispel a spell, that is within 50m per Level and that you are aware of. The TN to stop the opposing spell is the Focus Power result of the sorcerer who cast it.

### Globe of Invulnerability

**Abjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
- **Duration:** One Scene

The caster of the spell becomes immune to spells of Level 1 and 2. The caster cannot be targeted by such spells, and if caught in the area of effect of such a spell simply ignores its effect. However, they are not immune to spells that don't directly affect them - their attacks can still be parried with Shield, enemies under the effect of Boon still get their bonus against the caster, and so forth.

### Wall of Force

**Abjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Verbal
- **Duration:** One scene

This spell creates a powerful magical force field. A stationary plane of force 20m long and 5m high is created within 50m of the sorcerer. Every raise on the Focus Power Test adds 5m of length or 1m of height. The plane can be oriented in any manner, flat, vertical, slanted, etc. It is effectively indestructible for as long as it lasts. This spell cannot cause damage; if the Wall's area would intersect something (like a person or a tree) it instead stops just short of touching them. The wall is not 2-dimensional and cannot cut people with the edges. The sorcerer chooses if the wall is mostly transparent or completely opaque at the time of casting. While it may be difficult to see in thick fog or smoke it is in no way invisible or otherwise undetectable. This spell blocks teleportation if you completely enclose an area.

### Exploding Runes

**Abjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Touch, Somatic, Non-Combat
- **Duration:** Indefinite (E)

You inscribe a series of runes on an object. Anyone except you, and those you name as safe during the casting of the spell, who reads the runes causes them to explode. Everyone within 3m of the enscrolled object takes `4k4`+10 magical E type damage. The object that had the runes inscribed on it is unharmed by the explosion.

### Ruby Ray

**Abjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Reaction Action or Half Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Touch
- **Duration:** Instant

A Ruby Ray is effectively a more powerful and limited form of Dispel. It can be used in one of two ways - as a Reaction to a spell, or to set up a shield in advance. When used as Half Action to shield someone it reflects the next spell cast at the shielded target before the end of the Ruby Ray caster's next turn. It only reflects spells that target a single target, not those that effect everyone in an area or a burst effect like Energy Ball. If it is used as a Reaction Action then it can only protect the spell caster. The reflected spell rebounds to strike the original spell caster if they are within 200m, otherwise the spell dissipates harmlessly.

### Contingency

**Abjuration 5**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Non-Combat, Somatic, Touch
- **Duration:** Indefinite (E)

On the completion of this spell, you must immediately cast another spell. That spell does not take immediate effect. Under a condition chosen at the time of casting, that second spell takes effect instantly. Targets must be chosen when the spell is cast, though they may be left vague (for example, you might cast Fireball and set the target at "what I am pointing at", and casting Wall of Force "between myself and the incoming attack" is pretty popular too). A character may only have one Contingency active at a time. This spell can be cast on other people or on objects up to the size of a large airplane, and that does count as your one active Contingency.

### Disjunction

**Abjuration 5**

- **Focus Power Test:** Abjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 35
- **Action:** Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal
- **Duration:** Instant

This spell functions as Dispel with a 500m range, but if you succeed on the counter-spelling attempt, the target cannot cast spells until the end of your next turn, plus an additional turn per raise on the Focus Power Test. If you get five or more raises on the Focus Power Test and successfully dispel the opponent's spell then they may not cast any more spells until the end of the scene.

## Conjuration

### Invisible Servant

**Conjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Non-Combat, Verbal
- **Duration:** One day

You summon an invisible roaming animating force. It can perform any task that doesn't require a Test, and has effective rating of 1 in each characteristic. It lasts for one day, and is often used for cleaning and simple tasks. It is possible to summon multiple copies of the servant, the TN increases by +5 for each additional copy. Their invisibility isn't great, it's about a TN 15 Perception Test to spot one.

### Call Item

**Conjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Full Action (see text)
- **Keywords:** Touch, Non-Combat, Verbal
- **Duration:** Indefinite

You cast this spell on an item you can easily hold in your hands. You may teleport that item back into your hands at any time, from any distance, by casting this spell again as a free action. You may have only one item prepared in this way at a time. This effect cannot cross the boundary of a Crystal Sphere and is erratic in the Astral Sea. It is a bad idea to cast this in the Warp. The effect often fails to cross force-fields and cannot cross barriers designed to prevent teleportation. As a rule of thumb items up to about a meter and a half in length, and 15 kg in mass are safe to work with.

### Web

**Conjuration 1**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 15
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Attack, Combo-OK, Verbal, Somatic, Material, Saving Throw
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Material:** Cobwebs or sticky candy

When you cast Web, choose a point within 25m. All targets within 10m of the point save Arcana + Dexterity or they are Immobilized for a number of rounds equal to your Caster Level. Creatures entering the area do not get a saving throw. Stuck creatures may Test Strength or Size against the Focus Power Test to move through at half speed.

### Blink

**Conjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 20
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
- **Duration:** Instant

You teleport to a spot you can see within 40m.

### Lesser Servant

**Conjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 20
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Material, Somatic, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** One minute per caster level
- **Material:** A candle

You summon a weak creature to aid you. It appears within arm's reach, has a rating of 2 in all characteristics plus 2 ranks in any relevant skills.

### Obscuring Fog

**Conjuration 2**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 20
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** Instant

You create a bank of fog, up to three 5m x 5m x 5m cubes per Caster Level. Within this bank of fog, visibility is reduced to 1m. The fog dissipates at a normal rate, vanishing in about five minutes in normal conditions, less if there's wind, bright sunlight, and so forth.

### Porte

**Conjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 20
- **Action:** Half Action or Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Somatic
- **Duration:** One hour per Caster level

You create a portal large enough for a person to walk through in a place you can see within 100m. The portal must be placed on a relatively flat surface, but can otherwise be set at any angle. If the surface moves relative to its surroundings it vanishes instantly. You must cast this spell twice for it to have much effect. If the portals move relative to each other one of them will vanish (which one seems random). The portals are like a doorway - there's no real space between them, entering one immediately puts you out the other. You can hang halfway through a portal, though if one is closed you're expelled through the open side. Momentum is conserved through the portals. You may only have two portals open at once. If you attempt to open a third, you must choose which of the existing portals closes.

### Jaunt

**Conjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 25
- **Action:** Reaction Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** Instant

You instantly teleport up to 10m away. If used in Reaction to an attack, you can use this as a Dodge attempt.

### Cloudkill

**Conjuration 3**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 25
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
- **Duration:** Instant

You create a cloud of acidic poison gas, up to one 5m x 5m x 5m cube per Caster Level. Within this cloud of gas visibility is reduced to 2m. The cloud deals magical E type damage with rolled dice equal to the caster's Level plus their dots in Conjuration when they cast the spell, and kept dice equal to the caster's Level (Level + Conjuration, keep Level). The damage is dealt every time someone starts or ends their turn in the cloud. Breathing inside the cloud requires a Constitution vs. 15 Test or take a wound as though from a Toxic weapon (crits go to the gizzards). The cloud lasts 3 minutes plus another minute per raise on the Focus Power Test.

### Greater Servant

**Conjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Material, Somatic, Combo-OK
- **Duration:** One minute per caster level
- **Material:** A candle

As Lesser Servant, except that the summoned creature has 4 in all characteristics and 4 in all relevant skills.

### Teleport

**Conjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Combo-OK, Somatic, Verbal
- **Duration:** Instant

As Blink, but with no maximum distance, and you may teleport anywhere you have seen before. Unless you are extremely familiar with the destination, you must make a Wisdom + Perception Test against TN 25 (The SM may raise the TN for locations you've only seen once or have seen only from a distance or through divination spells). Teleport cannot be used to teleport through Crystal Spheres or into shielded ships. You can bring along 1 size worth of another creature per raise on the Focus Power Test. You need to be holding on to them and it has to be the whole creature or nothing, cybernetics or equipment that increases resilience counts as an increase to size (this is mostly just Power Armor).

### Black Blade of Disaster

**Conjuration 4**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 30
- **Action:** Half Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
- **Duration:** One Scene

This spell creates a dimensional rift to a zero space of utter destruction, a realm of void and emptiness so hungry that it has a kind of terrible anti-life. The rift takes the form of a large sword that functions as a Power Sword. It does an additional +`1k0` damage per raise on the Focus Power Test and has the following additional properties: Balanced, Toxic, and Shocking. The sword ignores Armor, its damage is reduced by Aura, but that Aura is reduced by the sword's penetration rating. The caster is automatically proficient with the blade and may choose to use their dots in Arcana in place of their Level for any Tests made with the sword (attacks, parries, etc.). If the blade leaves the hands of the caster it dissipates at the start or end of the caster's turn, or as soon as the caster wants it to, whichever comes first.

### Gate

**Conjuration 5**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 35
- **Action:** Full Action or 1 hour (see text)
- **Keywords:** Somatic, Combo-OK (see text)
- **Duration:** One hour per Caster Level

When cast in combat as a Full Action this spell is Combo-OK and functions as Porte, with the exceptions that it creates both portals at once and that those portals last until at least the start of the Caster's next turn. When cast as a one hour Non-Combat ritual it targets a doorway, archway, corridor, or entrance. It then creates a portal in that space and another portal of the same size and orientation almost anywhere else, even places that you haven't seen before. As with Porte these portals are fixed to their surroundings and have no distance between them. Unlike Porte they don't vanish if they get moved. Unless you are extremely familiar with the destination, you must make a Wisdom + Perception Test against TN 25 (the SM may raise the TN for locations you've only seen once or have seen only through divination spells), or TN 50 for places you only know exist. Gate cannot be used to pass through Crystal Spheres or into shielded ships.

### Incendiary Cloud

**Conjuration 5**

- **Focus Power Test:** Conjuration + Willpower
- **Target Number:** 35
- **Action:** Full Action
- **Keywords:** Verbal, Somatic
- **Duration:** Instant

## Advanced Magic Rules

### General Mind Control Rules

Mind control is a perennial issue with magic. These mechanics cut a middle path between the SM undermining PCs and players exploiting vague commands.

- **Failing a saving throw by 2 checks (10+ points):** You must follow the spirit and intent of the command.
- **Failing by less than 10 points:** You may spend a Resolve Point or Hero Point to subvert the command:
    - **Resolve Point:** Obey the letter of the command, but cannot intentionally misunderstand it.
    - **Hero Point:** May misunderstand the command.
- **Ongoing control spells:** Require Resolve Point or Hero Point expenditure every round to subvert.

**Example:** If compelled to "Disarm" and the save fails by 10+, you divest yourself of all weapons. If failed by less than 10, spending a Resolve Point lets you attempt to disarm someone else. Spending a Hero Point lets you interpret "dis-arm" as removing the caster's arm if you can achieve that level of critical damage.

### Do I Know I Saved?

Successfully saving against a mind control spell feels strange unless the spell has the Subtle keyword. The subject can identify a magical influence attempt with:

- **Arcana + Intelligence Test:** TN 15
- **Forbidden Lore + Wisdom Test:** TN 20
- **Free raise** if subjected to mind control spells before

Mind control spells with the Subtle keyword do not allow identification attempts.

### General Illusion Rules

Visual illusions look like real things and include appropriate shadows, water ripples, and dusty footprints by default. Audible illusions sound real with appropriate echoes. The illusion quality fools casual observers unless the caster has nearly zero information about the subject.

A D&D-style monster atlas entry combined with good CGI renderings provides enough reference to produce acceptable illusions of creatures like beholders, dragons, or world leaders. Illusionary mirrors and windows work fine.

**Detection Tests:** If questionable and the spell lacks specific guidance, use the Focus Power as the Target Number for both:

- **Scrutiny + Wisdom Test**
- **Perception + Intelligence Test**

Perception or distraction penalties apply to both rolls; bonuses only apply to appropriate rolls. Making one but not both allows suspicion that the illusion may be fake (inflatable decoys, disguised robots, holograms, doppelgangers, painted walls).

**Additional Properties:**

- Illusions appear on recordings and audiovisual equipment
- They fool robots and zombies reliably
- Obvious reality breaks (walking through walls, heavy vehicles leaving no tracks) give away the fakery but don't necessarily indicate magic
- Illusions don't become transparent when identified—an illusionary wall still blocks vision even when known to be fake

## Safe Casting Reference

### Safe, Boring, Predictable Magic

People are afraid of the magic system because they read the worst Warp chart results and assume they happen regularly. They see spell TNs and think they can't reliably cast without invoking the Warp. Here's the actual math.

**Worst Case Scenarios:** Vampire necromancers, werewolf transmuters, and dragon-blooded evokers.

A vampire with Tremere asset and 5 Intelligence rolls `7k6` to cast a TN 15 first rank spell. 90% of results are 28 or higher—massive overkill for first level. You can reliably cast third level spells before using re-rolls.

| Roll           | TN 15 Success Rate           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `5k4`            | 97%                          |
| `3k3` (Fettered) | 66%                          |
| `4k4` (Fettered) | 90% for TN 15, 69% for TN 20 |

Casting Fettered is always perfectly safe with 2/3 success rate. At 2nd level with 2 dots of Necromancy, you have `4k4` Fettered giving 90% chance for TN 15.

### Warp Table Danger Levels

**Psychic Phenomena:**

- 3% — 1 insanity (if modifier < +10)
- 3% — Fear(1)
- 3% — Fall 1d10m
- 3% — 1d5 wounds & Fear(2)
- 3% — Tech jamming & bionics save vs. 1d5 wounds
- (26+mod)% — Roll on Perils table

15% chance of insanity/wounds/fear, plus 26+% chance of Perils, leaving 59% or less "safe" result.

- 1% — Instant death
- 30% — Insanity (13% 1d5, 9% 1d10, 8% 3d10)

- 10% — Out 1 round
- 8% — Out 1d10 rounds
- 9% — Out 1d10 weeks
- 25% — Damage (1d5 wounds to 3d10 damage)

- 8% — Future rolls at +10 (adds +10% to instant death)
