> **Key points:**
> 
> - Nine disciplines of the **Sublime Way**, each teaching unique Advantages and Restrictions for melee combat
> - **Martial Adept Level** = highest Sword School dots (applies regardless of which school's technique you're using)
> - Build custom **Special Attacks** by combining Advantages (costing Style Points) with Restrictions (granting Style Points)
> - Cost: **50 XP per Style Point** to buy or improve a Special Attack
> - Cross-ref: [Feats](07-Feats.mdx) for martial Feats, [Equipment](10-Equipment.mdx) for weapon types, [Combat](14-Combat.mdx) for action economy

## Martial Adept Level

A character's **Martial Adept Level** equals their highest Sword School dots. Unlike Magic Schools (each rolled separately), Sword Schools combine — your full Martial Adept Level always applies regardless of which school a particular advantage came from.

---

## Building Special Attacks

Special Attacks are the core of the Sublime Way. Unlike spells, martial maneuvers flow into one another — Martial Adepts adapt elements of each school into a personal fighting style.

### Step by Step

1. **Choose a base action** — restricted by which schools you know (Standard Attack is universal at Level 1)
2. **Choose a weapon type** — restricted by your known schools
3. **Add Advantages** — each costs Style Points. Mix Advantages from different schools
4. **Add Restrictions** — each grants Style Points. Must cover any Advantage cost exceeding your Martial Adept Level
5. **Pay XP** — 50 XP per Style Point of Advantages
6. **Name it** — every Special Attack gets a name

**Style Point budget:** You have free Style Points equal to your Martial Adept Level. Beyond that, add Restrictions to fund more Advantages.

**Improving attacks:** 50 XP per Style Point added to an existing attack.

**Free Style Points:** When learning a new school level, gain free Style Points equal to the new level (for new or improved attacks).

**Restrictions:**

- Weapons with Blast or Flame qualities, explosives, and grenades **cannot** be used with Special Attacks
- Some weapon types (Ordinary, Syrneth) cover both ranged and melee — Special Attacks only work in melee
- Cannot add Advantages/Restrictions that would have no effect (e.g., reduced damage to a non-damaging action)

## School Progression

All nine schools follow this progression:

| Level | Title | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Apprentice | Learn school's weapon type and combat action |
| 2 | Initiate | Learn the school's Restriction and first Advantage |
| 3 | Journeyman | Learn to use school's favored skill as a Restriction; learn second Advantage |
| 4 | Master | Learn a Mastery ability (usable without Special Attacks); learn third Advantage |
| 5 | Grandmaster | Gain the school's ultimate secret technique (most powerful Advantage) |

## Example: Building a Special Attack

> **Boot To The Head** — Rocky has Setting Sun 3 and Iron Heart 2 (Martial Adept Level 3).
>
> - **Base:** Unarmed Standard Attack
> - **Advantages (7 SP):** Steel Wind from Iron Heart (1), Distraction Method from Setting Sun (2), +`1k1` damage from Universal (4)
> - **Restrictions (4 SP):** Opening the Path (2), Difficult Strike (1), Inaccurate (1)
> - **Net cost:** 7 SP of Advantages × 50 XP = **350 XP**
> - **Effect:** Every other turn, make an unarmed attack at -`1k0` to hit. Deals +`1k1` damage with Tearing. Damaged targets take -`1k1` to all rolls. Your Static Defense takes -10 until next turn.
