:::note[Quick Reference]

- Vehicle stats: **Size** (base HP, Resilience, Slots), **Speed**, **Acceleration** (Acc), **Maneuver** (Man), **Momentum** (Mom 0–10)
- Movement per turn = `Speed x Drive Rating x Momentum`
- Static Defense = `10 + Man - (2 x Size) + Speed Bonus` (based on Momentum tier)
- Control Test = `Skill + Attribute + Maneuver - Momentum`
- Losing 2+ HP in one hit triggers a **Critical Damage** roll
- See [Ships](./18-Ships.mdx) for space vessel rules; [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx) for general combat; [Equipment](./10-Equipment.mdx) for personal weapons
:::

## Vehicle Stats

### Size

The most important stat. Base **HP**, **Equipment Slots**, and **Resilience** all equal Size.

| Size Category | Range | Scale per Point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | 1–10 | ~0.5 meters |
| Large Vehicle | 11–20 | ~1 meter |
| Enormous | 21–30 | ~2 meters |
| Colossus | 31–40 | ~5 meters |
| Titanic | 41–50 | ~10 meters |
| Spelljammer | 51+ | Use ship rules |

### Speed

Base speed value. Combined with Drive Rating and Momentum to determine actual movement.

### Acceleration (Acc)

How quickly a vehicle can change Momentum per Maintain Control action. Acc 0 vehicles cannot change speed without Punch It tests.

### Maneuver (Man)

Modifier to Control Tests and Static Defense. **Penalized by current Momentum.** Vehicles with base Man < 0 require Punch It tests to turn.

### Momentum (Mom)

Dynamic value (0–10) representing current speed:

| Momentum | Speed Bonus to SD | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | Dead stop; SD = 0 |
| 1–5 | 1x Speed | Normal operation |
| 6–9 | 2x Speed | High speed |
| 10+ | 3x Speed | Maximum |

### Static Defense

`Static Defense = 10 + Man - (2 x Size) + Speed Bonus`

### Hit Points

When a vehicle reaches 0 HP, it is **destroyed**. Crew must make Acrobatics + Dexterity vs TN of fatal-blow damage to escape; failure = take the vehicle's death-blow damage (armor applies).

## Vehicles in Combat

A vehicle moves automatically on its controller's turn:

`Movement = Speed x Drive Rating x Momentum`

*Example:* A car (Speed 10, Wheeled x5) at Mom 10 moves 500m/round (~150 KPH).

### Control Tests

Made when the pilot must keep control (obstacles, combat, damage):

`Control Test = Skill + Attribute + Maneuver - Momentum`

Most ground vehicles use **Drive**; flying vehicles use **Pilot**.

### Out of Control

| d10 | Result | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | **Straight Edge** | Continues in a straight line |
| 5–7 | **Swerve** | Turns randomly |
| 8–9 | **Wild Stallion** | Random turn + random Momentum change by Acc |
| 10 | **Turn Over** | Flips; loses HP = Momentum; Momentum drops to 0 |

**Crashing** = automatic result of 10 on this table.

## Vehicle Actions

### Maintain Control (Half Action)

**Required every turn** to keep the vehicle under control. No roll needed unless conditions are adverse. Options:

- **Accelerate** — Change Momentum by up to Acc (max 10). Wheeled/tracked/walker may reverse at half speed
- **Turn** — Up to 90° turn (requires Man >= 0; otherwise use Punch It)

If nobody takes Maintain Control and Mom > 0, the vehicle goes Out of Control.

### Punch It (Half Action)

Push past safe limits. Make a Control Test:

- **Speed** — TN = 3x Momentum. Pass: +1 Momentum or Acc (bonus lost next turn if over 10)
- **Turning** — TN = 3x Momentum. Pass: change speed AND turn 90° in same action
- **Hard Turn** — TN = 10 + 2x Momentum. Pass: 180° turn without losing Momentum

### Fire Mounted Weapon (Half Action)

Fire one mounted weapon using Ballistics (ranged) or Weaponry (melee). Personal feats/bonuses do **not** apply. Full auto allowed but prevents Evasive Maneuvers until next turn.

### Evasive Maneuvers (Reaction)

Requires Mom >= 1. Control Test; half the result adds to SD. Momentum reduced by 1.

### Ramming (Half Action or Unintentional)

- **Intentional:** Control Test + Momentum (instead of -Momentum) vs target SD
- **Unintentional:** Automatic hit; target dodges vs TN = Speed + Momentum + Drive Rating

**Ram damage** (to both vehicle and target): `XkY+Z` where X = Size, Y = Momentum, Z = Speed + Drive Rating. If Momentum > Size, each excess die adds +5 flat damage.

After ramming: Control Test vs damage dealt. Pass: Mom -2, maintain control. Fail: Out of Control, Mom -3.

## Chase Action (Half Action, Optional Rule)

Replaces Maintain Control in racing/dogfighting. Includes all Maintain Control effects plus a **Trick** (pilot sets TN, makes Control Test). Opponents spend a Reaction to counter.

**Trick successes:**

- **Drift** — Move any direction without changing facing
- **Handbrake Turn** — Change facing to any direction at end of turn
- **Vault the Curb** — Ignore difficult/impassible terrain one turn
- **Slip By** — Move through occupied spaces and narrow gaps
- **Barrel Roll** — +5 SD until next turn
- **Puchilev's Cobra** — Reduce Momentum by up to 5 (Aerospace won't stall)

## Terrain

- **Difficult Terrain:** Speed halved. At Mom 6+, requires Control Test (TN 5 + 2x Momentum) or Out of Control
- **Impassible Terrain:** Cannot cross without special drive/accessory. Entering = automatic crash (result 10)

---

## Flying Vehicles

Any vehicle with VTOL, Aerospace, or Scramjet drive (or Jump Jets) can fly in 3D.

- **Falling:** If Out of Control or loses 2+ HP, begins falling. Falls 400m (turn 1), 1200m (turn 2), 2000m (turn 3), 2700m (turn 4). Terminal velocity = Size x 100m/turn. Hitting ground = Ram at Mom 10
- **Recovery:** After Maintain Control, Control Test vs TN 10 + Size to pull out of fall
- **Runways:** Aerospace needs Size x 50m flat ground. Scramjet needs Size x 150m. Wrong terrain = crash

---

## Vehicle Critical Damage

Triggered when losing **2+ HP** in a single hit (a **Penetrating Blow**). Roll +1 per HP lost beyond the second.

| d10 | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Paint ruined — no mechanical effect |
| 2 | Staggering blow — Momentum -1 |
| 3 | Pilot shaken — only half action next turn |
| 4 | Out of Control |
| 5 | Motive system hit — Mom -3, max Momentum -1 until repaired |
| 6 | Pilot hit — pilot loses `1k1` HP |
| 7 | Random system disabled until repaired (not controls) |
| 8 | Vehicle Crashes |
| 9 | Cockpit breach — lose environmental seals; pilot loses `3k1` HP |
| 10 | Roll twice (ignore further 10s) |

---

## Repairing Vehicles

- Time per HP = Size in hours
- Double manpower = reduce effective Size by 1 (min half actual Size)
- Disabled systems: 1 HP equivalent per unmodified slot occupied

---

## Vehicles On Their Backs

Righting requires Strength Test vs TN = 2x Size. Multiple people combine efforts. Flying vehicles that flip may attempt Control Test (Half Action, TN = Size) to right before crashing.

---

## Untrained Piloting

No ranks in Control Skill: Maintain Control becomes a **Full Action** using the worse of Dexterity or Intelligence (no skill added).

## Quick & Dirty Vehicle Rules

### Simplified Building

**Size =** 1/5 passengers + 1/5 m3 cargo + 1/5 armor + weapon slots (1/light, 3/heavy, 6/huge) + 1/feature + 3/module + 1/4 speed

### Simplified Stats

| Stat | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| HP = Resilience | Size |
| Move/turn | Speed x Drive x Momentum |
| KPH | Move / 3 |
| Defense | Man + Speed + Momentum - Size (0 at Mom 0) |
| Cost | 5 + Man + Size + Speed + 5/feature + 10/module-or-weapon |

### Quick Rolling

`Drive/Pilot + Int/Dex + Maneuver - Momentum`

- **No test:** Change Momentum by 1, 90 degree turn, non-threatened activity
- **TN 15:** Change Momentum by 2, 180 degree turn, avoid Out of Control
- **TN 25:** Change Momentum by 3, bootlegger reverse, recover from Out of Control
- **Opposed:** Racing, chasing, gaining advantage

## Extended Rules

### Ram/Crash (Quick Version)

**Damage:** Rolled dice = Size, kept dice = Momentum, +Speed +Drive Rating as flat bonus. Excess Momentum over Size = +5/die.

- **Vehicle vs Vehicle (head-on):** Add both Momentums
- **Vehicle vs Immovable Object:** Take same damage dealt
- **Vehicle vs Person:** Take 1/4 damage dealt (1/2 vs power armor)

### Called Shots

State desired effect. Damage determines result:

- Tearing-only = not achieved
- 1 HP = d10, on 10 roll crit chart
- 2+ HP = roll crit chart; modify by HP lost to reach appropriate result

### Pilot Swapping

If pilot changes mid-combat: vehicle gets its own initiative (set to original pilot's). Change only at Mom 0 at round end.

### Reaching Orbit

Vehicle with Speed 20+, Scramjet, Mom 10+ at high altitude: 2 afterburner charges for standard gravity (10m/s2). Each additional 10m/s2 = +1 charge.

### Limited Ammo

- Personal weapons in mounts: regular ammo; reload from inside or 3x clip size external
- Vehicle ballistics: (HP - Slots) x 3 shots. Weapons over +10 damage require downtime reload
