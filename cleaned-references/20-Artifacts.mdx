﻿# Artifacts

## Script Immunity

Story Masters are free to arrange theft of a character's assets, but many players feel protective about their artifacts. Figure out which artifacts mean something to their players before messing with them. Make sure anything you do with an artifact is reversible - create a story to hunt down the thief or find a workshop to effect repairs.

This applies more to artifacts purchased at character generation than to artifacts found during play. Weapons taken from unimportant enemies are easy come, easy go, but after an elaborate heist to acquire a legendary hero's weapon, a player may be less willing to lose it.

## Common Traits of Artifacts

Unless noted otherwise, all artifacts share these general traits:

- **Magical Detection:** Artifacts register as magical and may be detected by warp-energy flow. A character with Arcana can make a Test (TN 20) to identify an object as an artifact.
- **Eternal:** Artifacts do not wear down, wear out, or break accidentally. The power of an artifact is bound into it forever and does not need maintenance.
- **Difficult to Break:** Creation of a magical wonder solidifies magical flows. Most require a very specific ritual or extraordinary environment (such as the heart of a star) to break.
- **Cannot Easily Be Bought/Sold:** Artifacts are far beyond the reach of most. Even the most common artifact would take Wealth 4 or 5 just to consider as a purchase.

## Magical Weapons and Armor

Artifact weapons are extremely powerful, typically ancient, and made of one of the five magical metals. The artifact level depends on the base rarity of the weapon itself.

**Key Rules:**
- Bonuses listed for magical weapons/armor replace normal quality bonuses - each is already considered Best quality
- Only armor primarily made of metal can benefit from magical materials
- Only weapons using solid ammunition can benefit from special ammunition
- Special ammunition artifact rating is one less than the weapon it's for (minimum Artifact 1)
- Pistol ammunition grants two clips; other weapons grant one clip
- All magical weapons and armor have one hearthstone slot
- Damage counts as magical for purposes that care about magical damage

## Magical Bionics

Bionics can be made from magical materials following the same pricing as weapons and armor. Common types: bionic arms, locomotion, and (rarely) hearts.

**Key Rules:**
- Provide +2 AP to the replaced location
- Damage to these locations counts as damage to the character
- Critical damage effects work as normal
- Effects causing bleeding render the limb useless instead
- Artifact bionics are not easy to destroy - severed limbs can be made functional later
- Fast healing does not help attach bionics faster; normal attachment process required

## Magical Materials

### Orichalcum

A golden magical material with a sheen that bears the warmth and light of the sun. Weapons and armor of Orichalcum are generally better at everything they do.

- **Melee Weapon:** +`1k0` to all Weaponry tests, +2 damage, +2 Penetration
- **Ranged Weapon:** +`1k0` to all Ballistics tests, never jams or overheats
- **Special Ammunition:** Weapon never jams, +2 damage, +2 Penetration
- **Armor:** +2 AP, reduces Static Defense penalty by half
- **Bionic Arm:** +`1k1` to Dexterity Tests for delicate manipulation, +`1k1` to Strength Tests with that arm
- **Bionic Locomotion:** +2 Speed, +`1k1` to Athletics tests
- **Bionic Heart:** +2 Hit Points, can take two more Fatigue before falling unconscious

### Mithril

Forms only in wild and undeveloped spaces, mostly on moons and low gravity areas. Weapons and armor are exceptionally light, emphasizing movement and fluidity.

- **Melee Weapon:** +2 Static Defense, +`1k0` to parry, feint, and disarm
- **Ranged Weapon:** No penalties for firing heavy weapons without bracing or using Basic weapon in one hand. Pistols may be readied as a Free Action and used in grapples
- **Special Ammunition:** +2 Penetration, double the weapon's range
- **Armor:** +2 Static Defense, +`1k0` to dodge, +2 Maximum Dexterity
- **Bionic Arm:** One Ready action as free action each round, +`1k1` to parry and throw tests
- **Bionic Locomotion:** +2 Speed, +`2k0` to Acrobatics tests
- **Bionic Heart:** Gain Fleet of Foot feat, +3 on initiative tests

### Darksteel

A magical dark gray or black metal. All items made of darksteel are absolutely indestructible.

- **Melee Weapon:** +`0k1` damage, +2 Penetration
- **Ranged Weapon:** +`1k0` damage, when striking cover double Penetration and halve AP of cover, cannot overheat or jam
- **Special Ammunition:** +4 Penetration, when striking cover double Penetration and halve AP of cover
- **Armor:** +2 AP, halve Penetration of all weapons except Darksteel
- **Bionic Arm:** Cannot be destroyed by critical damage (severed instead if effect would kill/destroy), +`1k0` to Strength Tests with that arm
- **Bionic Locomotion:** Legs cannot be destroyed by critical damage, +`2k0` to Tests to stay on feet. May make a test to stay on feet even when not normally allowed (without bonus)
- **Bionic Heart:** Ignore critical damage effects to Gizzards, +3 armor (total +5 AP) to Gizzards

### Wraithbone

Crystallized magical energy from the Warp, shaped by sorcery. Stronger than steel and quickly repairs itself. Natural conductor of magical energy.

- **Melee Weapon:** Deliver melee-range spells using Weaponry + Level (instead of Brawl + Level). If spell has half action+ casting time, may add weapon attack to the spell
- **Ranged Weapon:** Once per scene as Free Action, generate one clip (or three arrows/bolts) of normal ammunition
- **Special Ammunition:** Counts as magical attack, ignores Armor and damage reduced by Aura instead
- **Armor:** +4 Aura
- **Bionic Arm:** May Parry any spell with Touch or Ranged Touch targeting the bearer. Successful parry completely absorbs the spell
- **Bionic Locomotion:** Once per scene, walk on air for one round per Level, moving at full speed in all three dimensions
- **Bionic Heart:** When suffering Perils of the Warp roll, roll twice and choose between results

### Necrodermis

A dull matte grey metal that wounds souls. The metal lives.

- **Melee Weapon:** Opponents take -`2k0` to parry or dodge
- **Ranged Weapon:** Opponents take -`2k0` to parry or dodge
- **Special Ammunition:** +`1k0` to hit, +2 damage, gains Toxic & Tearing properties
- **Armor:** Opponents take -`2k0` to hit
- **Bionic Arm:** At beginning of turn, all critical damage removed as metal heals. +`1k1` to all Tests in a grapple
- **Bionic Locomotion:** At beginning of turn, all critical damage to legs removed. Stand from prone as free action without provoking Opportunity Attacks
- **Bionic Heart:** Can take two more Fatigue before falling unconscious. When burning a Hero Point to survive, roll 1d10: on 5 or less, Hero Point not burned but lose 1 Wisdom as the artifact forces body back to life. At zero Wisdom, gain 50 insanity points instead

## Wonders

### Aetheroscope (Artifact 1)
Allows the user to see magical auras as a glow in the air out to 10m. If the user does nothing in a round but steady it and study, they can determine relative strength and source of auras.

### Cloak of Invisibility (Artifact 3-4)
- **Artifact 3 (Cloak):** Once per scene, turn invisible for five minutes. Items under the cloak are also invisible.
- **Artifact 4 (Body-glove):** Near perfect concealment from thermal cameras, x-ray, life scanners, pressure plates, motion detectors, and guard animals. Skin-tight, won't fit over armor, can hold only a pistol or knife. Once per scene for ten minutes.

### Rod of Might (Artifact 2-4)
Small rod with 2-4 functions. Common functions: extend to several meters/form ladder, grow a blade (Best quality fencing sword), fix in space, or transform into other weapons. Cost depends on number of functions.

### Silvery Box (Artifact 1)
Small box that can only be opened by its owner. First to pick it up becomes owner; owner can willingly abandon it. All attempts to open or destroy automatically fail.

### Hearthstone Amulet (Artifact 1)
Holds a hearthstone to enable use of its abilities. Keeps wearer clean - dirt and muck are instantly cleaned off.

### Dragon Tear Tiara (Artifact 2)
Has hearthstone setting on forehead. Wearer gains +`1k0` to all Perception tests. +`3k0` on Arcana tests to detect spells or determine magical effects.

### Hearthstone Bracers (Artifact 2)
Contains slot for single hearthstone. Provides +2 Armor Points that stack with other armor.

### Hat of Disguise (Artifact 2)
Change appearance at will. Free raise on all disguise tests, no makeup needed. Hat changes form to any head-wear. Does not change clothing.

### Perfected Boots (Artifact 1)
Size to fit anyone, always warm and dry. +2 Speed, can move overland at 20km/hour.

### Folding Vehicles (Artifact 1-4)
Vehicles that fold into small boxes (max 40cm, 5-20kg). Can be anything from two-person fly cycle to eight-crew submersible. Otherwise ordinary vehicles.

### Resplendent Personal Assistant (Artifact 2)
Bracelet combining functions of Chrono, Combi-Tool, Data Slate, Multikey, Pict Recorder, and Torch. Never runs out of power, auto-adjusts time, includes compass and altimeter that work in any environment.

### Bag of Holding (Artifact 2)
Larger on inside than outside. Extradimensional space means items don't add weight. Appears to hold 0.25 cubic meters but actually holds 2 cubic meters.

### Essence Union Dart (Artifact 1)
Finger-sized tracking dart with matching ring. Ring pulses and indicates direction to dart within 1-2km. If dart hits target (half action to remove), ring wearer ignores penalties for not seeing target (darkness, smoke).

### Jump Pack (Artifact 3)
Twice per scene, take Run action as half action instead of full action. While running, gain flight to move in all three dimensions.

### Psychic Paper (Artifact 2)
Assumes form of any document the user has seen or can imagine, with correct stamps and signatures. Passes cursory inspection. Change lasts one scene only.

## Hearthstones

Hearthstones are talismans of power, solid accumulations of pure magical power about the size of a large chicken egg. Many magical items have sockets for mounting hearthstones. When placed in a socket, the hearthstone resonates with the enchantment and provides beneficial effects determined by stone type.

### Gemstone of Aura Detection (Artifact 2)
Read someone's aura within 20m. Roll Wisdom + Perception (TN 10). Success gives vague impression of strongest emotion. Two+ raises reveal emotions and whether target is Exalted. Target may resist with Willpower + Arcana, replacing the TN.

### Windhands Gemstone (Artifact 3)
May attempt to parry ranged attacks (arrows, thrown weapons) without using a stunt.

### Gem of Adamant Skin (Artifact 4)
Reduce Hit Points lost by 1 (minimum 0), but gain a level of Fatigue. Characters immune to fatigue become Dazzled for 1d2 rounds instead.

### Gem of Immortality (Artifact 4)
Does not age or suffer infirmities/illnesses of old age. Cannot die of old age or illness. If stone is lost, aging resumes where it left off. Can spend Hero Point to survive certain death instead of burning it - works three times, then shatters.

### Gem of the Calm Heart (Artifact 1)
Gain the Common Sense feat.

### Jewel of the Hungry Fire (Artifact 2)
Comfortable in high temperatures. Reduce all fire damage by 5.

### The Freedom Stone (Artifact 3)
Cannot be physically restrained - shackles/ropes fall away. All grapple moves automatically parried/dodged. Does not counter locked doors or barred gates.

### Seacalm Gemstone (Artifact 3)
Breathe water as air. Protected from sea pressure and cold (except truly stygian depths).

### Stone of Healing (Artifact 1)
+`2k0` to all Medicae rolls.

### The Monkey Stone (Artifact 2)
Reduce difficulty of Athletics and Acrobatics rolls for balance, jumping, or climbing by 5 (minimum difficulty 5).

### The Speech Gemstone (Artifact 1)
Speak and understand one specific language. Found for every language except Syrneth.

### Gem of Sending (Artifact 2 for pair)
Always found in pairs. Can pass messages through any distance, but only to the paired gem, both must be socketed, and messages limited to a sentence or two.

### Dream-Stone (Artifact 4)
Dreams are always prophetic. Once per session on waking, make Wisdom + Scrutiny Test (TN 15). Success gives a dream indicating events to come. More raises = clearer dream.

### Golden Marble (Artifact 1)
Memorize a location by concentrating and tapping. Afterward, gem leads bearer back along the path traveled.

### Wilderness Gem (Artifact 2)
Instinctive knowledge of safe shelter, campsites, edible plants/animals, and dangerous weather signs.

### The Gem of Day's Light (Artifact 4)
Concentrate to illuminate everything within a kilometer with daylight. Effect lasts as long as needed.

### Spacer's Voidstone (Artifact 3)
No voidsuit needed in vacuum. Safe from micro-meteorites and hard radiation. +1 dot to skills for flying, navigating, repairing, and operating spelljammers/spacecraft. Protects during unprotected atmospheric reentry (not the landing).
