:::note[Quick Reference]

- Artifacts are **eternal**, **nearly indestructible** magical items that register as magical (Arcana Test TN 20 to identify)
- Rated 1–5 by rarity (Very Common → Very Rare); most require Wealth 4–5 to purchase
- **Magical Materials** (Orichalcum, Mithril, Darksteel, Wraithbone, Necrodermis) define weapon/armor artifact properties
- **Hearthstones** are socketed gems providing additional powers
- **Wonders** are unique items with special abilities
:::

See [Equipment](./10-Equipment.mdx) for base weapon/armor types.

## Common Traits

All artifacts share these properties unless noted otherwise:

- **Magical Detection:** Register as magical; Arcana Test (TN 20) to identify
- **Eternal:** Never wear down, break accidentally, or need maintenance
- **Difficult to Break:** Require specific rituals or extraordinary environments (e.g., heart of a star) to destroy
- **Cannot Easily Be Bought/Sold:** Minimum Wealth 4–5 to even consider purchasing

## Script Immunity

SMs may arrange theft of artifacts, but should:

- Figure out which artifacts matter to players before interfering
- Ensure any loss is reversible (create a recovery story)
- Treat character-generation artifacts with more care than loot found in play

## Magical Weapons and Armor

Artifact weapons are powerful, typically ancient, and made of one of the five **magical metals**. Artifact level depends on base rarity of the weapon.

**Key Rules:**

- Bonuses **replace** normal quality bonuses — each is already Best quality
- Only metal armor benefits from magical materials
- Only solid-ammo weapons benefit from special ammunition
- Special ammo artifact rating = weapon rating − 1 (minimum 1)
- Pistol ammo grants **two clips**; other weapons grant **one clip**
- All magical weapons/armor have **one hearthstone slot**
- Damage counts as **magical** for purposes that care

## Magical Bionics

Bionics can use magical materials following weapon/armor pricing.

- Provide +2 AP to the replaced location
- Critical damage effects work normally; bleeding renders limb useless instead
- Artifact bionics aren't easily destroyed — severed limbs can be repaired later
- Fast healing doesn't speed attachment; normal process required

## Magical Materials

### Orichalcum

Golden metal bearing the warmth and light of the sun. Generally better at everything.

| Application | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Melee Weapon** | +`1k0` Weaponry tests, +2 damage, +2 Penetration |
| **Ranged Weapon** | +`1k0` Ballistics tests, never jams or overheats |
| **Special Ammo** | Never jams, +2 damage, +2 Penetration |
| **Armor** | +2 AP, Static Defense penalty halved |
| **Bionic Arm** | +`1k1` Dexterity (delicate), +`1k1` Strength (that arm) |
| **Bionic Locomotion** | +2 Speed, +`1k1` Athletics tests |
| **Bionic Heart** | +2 Hit Points, +2 Fatigue tolerance |

### Mithril

Forms in wild spaces, mostly on moons and low-gravity areas. Emphasizes movement and fluidity.

| Application | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Melee Weapon** | +2 Static Defense, +`1k0` to parry/feint/disarm |
| **Ranged Weapon** | No heavy-weapon bracing penalty; Basic in one hand; Pistols ready as Free Action and work in grapples |
| **Special Ammo** | +2 Penetration, double range |
| **Armor** | +2 Static Defense, +`1k0` dodge, +2 Max Dexterity |
| **Bionic Arm** | One Ready as free action/round, +`1k1` parry/throw |
| **Bionic Locomotion** | +2 Speed, +`2k0` Acrobatics |
| **Bionic Heart** | Fleet of Foot feat, +3 initiative |

### Darksteel

Dark gray/black metal. Absolutely indestructible.

| Application | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Melee Weapon** | +`0k1` damage, +2 Penetration |
| **Ranged Weapon** | +`1k0` damage, double Pen vs cover (halve cover AP); no jam/overheat |
| **Special Ammo** | +4 Penetration, double Pen vs cover (halve cover AP) |
| **Armor** | +2 AP, halve Penetration of all non-Darksteel weapons |
| **Bionic Arm** | Indestructible (severed if crit would destroy), +`1k0` Strength |
| **Bionic Locomotion** | Legs indestructible, +`2k0` stay on feet (+ test even when normally disallowed) |
| **Bionic Heart** | Ignore Gizzards crits, +3 armor (+5 AP total) to Gizzards |

### Wraithbone

Crystallized Warp energy shaped by sorcery. Stronger than steel, self-repairing. Natural magical conductor.

| Application | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Melee Weapon** | Deliver melee spells via Weaponry + Level; combine weapon attack with half-action+ spells |
| **Ranged Weapon** | Once/scene free action: generate one clip (or 3 arrows/bolts) |
| **Special Ammo** | Counts as magical; ignores Armor (reduced by Aura instead) |
| **Armor** | +4 Aura |
| **Bionic Arm** | Parry any Touch/Ranged Touch spell; successful parry absorbs it completely |
| **Bionic Locomotion** | Once/scene: walk on air for rounds = Level, full speed in 3D |
| **Bionic Heart** | Roll Perils of the Warp twice, choose result |

### Necrodermis

Dull matte grey living metal that wounds souls.

| Application | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Melee Weapon** | Opponents −`2k0` to parry/dodge |
| **Ranged Weapon** | Opponents −`2k0` to parry/dodge |
| **Special Ammo** | +`1k0` to hit, +2 damage, gains Toxic & Tearing |
| **Armor** | Opponents −`2k0` to hit |
| **Bionic Arm** | All crit damage removed at turn start; +`1k1` grapple |
| **Bionic Locomotion** | Leg crit damage removed at turn start; stand from prone as free action (no Opportunity Attacks) |
| **Bionic Heart** | +2 Fatigue tolerance; when burning Hero Point to survive, roll 1d10: on 5 or less, Hero Point not burned but lose 1 Wisdom. At 0 Wisdom: gain 50 insanity instead |

## Wonders

### Utility Items

**Aetheroscope** (Artifact 1) — See magical auras as a glow within 10m. Spending a full round studying reveals relative strength and source.

**Silvery Box** (Artifact 1) — Small box openable only by its owner (first to pick it up). All attempts to open or destroy it automatically fail. Owner can willingly abandon it.

**Resplendent Personal Assistant** (Artifact 2) — Bracelet combining: Chrono, Combi-Tool, Data Slate, Multikey, Pict Recorder, Torch. Never runs out of power. Auto-adjusts time; includes compass and altimeter for any environment.

**Bag of Holding** (Artifact 2) — Extradimensional space: appears to hold 0.25m³ but actually holds 2m³. Items don't add weight.

**Essence Union Dart** (Artifact 1) — Finger-sized tracking dart with matching ring. Ring pulses direction to dart within 1–2km. If dart hits target (half action to remove), ring wearer ignores penalties for not seeing target.

**Psychic Paper** (Artifact 2) — Assumes form of any document the user has seen or can imagine, with correct stamps and signatures. Passes cursory inspection. Lasts one scene.

### Mobility Items

**Perfected Boots** (Artifact 1) — Size to fit anyone; always warm and dry. +2 Speed, 20km/hour overland.

**Jump Pack** (Artifact 3) — Twice per scene: Run action as half action + flight in 3D.

**Folding Vehicles** (Artifact 1–4) — Vehicles that fold into boxes (max 40cm, 5–20kg). Anything from two-person fly cycle to eight-crew submersible. Otherwise ordinary vehicles.

### Disguise & Stealth

**Hat of Disguise** (Artifact 2) — Change appearance at will. Free raise on all disguise tests, no makeup needed. Hat changes to any headwear. Does not change clothing.

**Cloak of Invisibility** (Artifact 3) — Once per scene: invisible for 5 minutes. Items under cloak also invisible.

**Cloak of Invisibility** (Artifact 4) — Body-glove version. Near-perfect concealment from thermal, x-ray, life scanners, pressure plates, motion detectors, guard animals. Skin-tight (no armor); holds only pistol or knife. Once per scene for 10 minutes.

### Weapons & Combat

**Rod of Might** (Artifact 2–4) — Small rod with 2–4 functions. Common: extend to several meters/ladder, grow a blade (Best fencing sword), fix in space, transform to other weapons. Cost scales with functions.

### Socketed Items

**Hearthstone Amulet** (Artifact 1) — Holds a hearthstone. Keeps wearer clean — dirt is instantly removed.

**Dragon Tear Tiara** (Artifact 2) — Hearthstone setting on forehead. +`1k0` Perception tests, +`3k0` Arcana tests to detect spells/magical effects.

**Hearthstone Bracers** (Artifact 2) — Single hearthstone slot. +2 Armor Points (stacks with other armor).

## Hearthstones

Hearthstones are solid accumulations of pure magical power (about the size of a large egg). When placed in a socket, they resonate with the enchantment and provide beneficial effects.

| Hearthstone | Rating | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gem of the Calm Heart** | 1 | Gain the Common Sense feat |
| **Stone of Healing** | 1 | +`2k0` to all Medicae rolls |
| **The Speech Gemstone** | 1 | Speak/understand one specific language (every language except Syrneth) |
| **Golden Marble** | 1 | Memorize a location; gem leads bearer back along path traveled |
| **Gemstone of Aura Detection** | 2 | Read auras within 20m (Wisdom + Perception TN 10); raises reveal emotions/Exalted status |
| **Jewel of the Hungry Fire** | 2 | Comfortable in high temperatures; reduce fire damage by 5 |

| **Gem of Sending** | 2 (pair) | Pass messages to paired gem at any distance (both must be socketed; sentence limit) |
| **The Monkey Stone** | 2 | Reduce Athletics/Acrobatics difficulty for balance/jumping/climbing by 5 (minimum 5) |
| **Windhands Gemstone** | 3 | May parry ranged attacks (arrows, thrown weapons) without a stunt |
| **Seacalm Gemstone** | 3 | Breathe water; protected from sea pressure and cold |
| **The Freedom Stone** | 3 | Cannot be physically restrained; all grapples auto-parried/dodged. Does not counter locked doors |
| **Spacer's Voidstone** | 3 | No voidsuit needed in vacuum; safe from micro-meteorites/radiation; +1 dot to spacefaring skills; survives unprotected reentry (not landing) |
