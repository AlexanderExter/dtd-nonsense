## Combat Actions

Action types: **H** = Half Action, **F** = Full Action, **R** = Reaction, **Fr** = Free Action, **V** = Variable. See [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx) for full rules.

| Action | Type | Tags | Effect |
| --- | :---: | --- | --- |
| Aid Another | H | Misc | Give another character +`1k0` on their next Test. Must be adjacent; skill-gated. |
| Aim | H/F | Melee, Ranged | Half: +`1k0` next attack. Full: +`2k0` next attack. Lost if you React before attacking. |
| All-Out Attack | F | Attack, Melee | +`2k0` to hit, +`1k0` melee damage. Cannot Dodge or Parry until next turn. |
| Brace | H | Misc | Brace a heavy weapon to fire normally. -`3k0` without bracing. |
| Bull Rush | H | Attack, Melee, Movement | Opposed Size + Strength. Push target 2m + 2m per Raise. |
| Called Shot | F | Attack, Melee, Ranged | Choose hit location. -`2k0` to hit. |
| Charge | F | Attack, Melee, Movement | Move up to 2× Speed (min 4m), +`1k0` melee attack. Must be straight line. |
| Delay | H | Misc | Save a Half Action for later this round. Interrupts before triggering action. |
| Disarm | H | Attack, Melee | Opposed Level + Weaponry. Two Raises = opponent drops weapon. |
| Dodge | R | Defense, Movement | Dexterity + Acrobatics Test. Half result added to SD vs this attack. |
| Feint | H | Attack, Melee | Opposed Level + Weaponry. Win = next melee attack can't be dodged/parried. |
| Fight Defensively | F | Attack, Melee, Ranged | -`2k0` to hit, +`2k0` to Parry and Dodge until next turn. |
| Focus Power | V | Varies | Cast a spell. School + Characteristic Test vs spell TN. |
| Full Auto Burst | F | Attack, Ranged | +`2k1` to hit. Each Raise = +`1k0` damage (up to RoF). Storm: +`2k0` per Raise. |
| Full Defense | F | Defense, Melee | +10 SD, gain one extra Reaction until next turn. |
| Grapple | H/F | Attack, Melee | H: Initiate grapple (Brawl to hit). F: Maintain, then opposed Size + Str for controller options. |
| Healing Surge | H | Misc | Spend RP up to Level to heal that many HP. +5 SD until next turn. |
| Knock Down | H | Attack, Melee | Opposed Size + Strength. Win = target prone. Lose by 3+ Raises = you're prone. |
| Move | H/F | Movement | Half: move Speed meters. Full: move 2× Speed. Provokes when leaving melee. |
| Multiple Attacks | F | Attack, Melee, Ranged | Extra attacks spending Reactions. Requires two weapons or feat (Swift/Lightning Attack). |
| Opportunity Attack | Fr | Attack, Melee | Free melee attack when enemy uses a Provokes action while engaged. Once per turn. |
| Overwatch | F | Attack, Ranged | Establish 45° kill zone. Fire Full Auto or Suppressing Fire when conditions met. Ends if you React. |
| Parry | R | Defense, Melee | Weaponry Test (+ Level `k0` if proficient). Half result added to SD vs melee attack. |
| Ready | H | Misc | Draw/stow weapon or item. Also: apply bandage, coat blade, etc. Provokes. |
| Reload | V | Misc | Reload per weapon's listed reload time. Provokes. |
| Run | F | Movement | Move 6× Speed. Ranged attacks at -`2k0` vs you; melee attacks at +`2k0` vs you. |
| Shift | H | Movement | Move Dexterity in meters. Does not provoke. |
| Stand | H | Movement | Stand up from prone. Provokes. |
| Standard Attack | H | Attack, Melee, Ranged | Make one melee or ranged attack. |
| Suppressing Fire | F | Attack, Ranged | Full auto into 45° kill zone. Targets must pass Pinning Test. Ballistics TN 20 for random hits. |
| Tactical Advance | F | Movement, Defense | Move up to 2× Speed between cover. Retain old cover's benefits during move. |
| Use a Skill | V | Misc | Use any skill. Time/Test varies by skill. |
| Withdraw | F | Movement | Disengage from melee. Move up to Speed without provoking. |

## Conditions

See [Conditions](./16-Conditions.mdx) for full rules.

| Condition | Effect | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amputation | Lose a body part. Treated as Heavily Wounded. Must heal limb damage before other healing. | Permanent (cybernetics/magic to restore) |
| Blinded | -`4k0` to physical Tests. Athletics + Dexterity TN 15 to move at Half Rate. | Until cured |
| Blood Loss | Constitution Test each Round (TN 10 × Blood Loss). Fail = 1d10 damage per Blood Loss, ignoring Armor/Aura. | Until treated (First Aid / magic) |
| Burning | 1d10 Energy damage per Round, ignoring Armor. No actions except trying to extinguish (TN 15 Intelligence). | Until extinguished |
| Crippled | Limb unusable. Arm: can't attack with it. Leg: fall prone, crawl 1m per Half Action. | Until healed |
| Dazzled | -`1k0` to all Tests. -`2k0` total to sight-based Tests. | Varies |
| Deafened | -`2k0` to all Tests. Auto-fail hearing-based Tests. | Until cured |
| Fatigue | -`1k0` to all Tests per level (applied once). Levels > Constitution = unconscious for 10 − Con hours. | 1 level per hour rest |
| Frightened | Must flee source. If unable: -`2k0` all Tests, no Attacks/Magic/Stunts. | End of encounter |
| Helpless | No actions. Attacks auto-hit. Damage rolled twice, results added. | Until condition causing it ends |
| Pinned | Cannot move or act (including Reactions) except Full Defense. Can still speak. | Until unpinned |
| Prone | +`2k0` melee attacks against you. -`2k0` ranged attacks against you. -`2k0` to your attacks. Crawl at Half Rate. | Stand up (Half Action) |
| Stunned | No Reactions. Only action: move at Half Rate. | Varies (usually 1 Round) |
| Toxic | Rating 1–2: that much damage/Round. Rating 3+: that many d10 damage/Round. Toughness TN 15 + Rating negates. | Until treated or purged |

| --- | --- |
| Static Defense | 10 + (Dexterity + Wisdom) × 3 − (Size × 2) |

| Mental Defense | 5 + (Composure × 5) |

| Speed | Strength + Dexterity |
