## Magic Revision Notes

The reason behind rewriting the spells in this revised edition is simple: some were ineffective, some were overpowered, and others were unclear. The methodology for rewriting spells focused on making spell choices meaningful and better describing spell effects.

The 5th level Evocation spell **Meteor Storm** and the 5th level Healing spell **Resurrection** serve as baselines for 5th level spells. All other 5th level spells were compared to them—would you consider taking another spell over Meteor Storm or Resurrection?

### Magic Guidelines

- **Non-combat spells are dangerous during combat:** They make you helpless until your next turn, and the casting action is a Full Action.
- **Enable different saving throws:** Added: Int, Dex, Com, Fel, Wis, Size, Str, Devotion, etc.
- **Reduce frustration with illusions and mind control spells:** See Magic chapter for detailed rules.
- **Attack touch spells last through the caster's next turn:** Several Evocation touch attack spells had timing issues with opportunity attacks.
- **Non-attack touch spells provoke opportunity attacks when used offensively:** For spells like Transformation that can be buffs but may also have negative consequences.
- **Spells requiring subtlety must be Subtle:** Consider Charm Person if the target saw you cast and knew they rolled against mind control—they'd know you're hostile regardless of the save result.
- **All spells are useful and choosing is difficult.**
- **Effects should be easy to use and rule on:** Added missing ranges, durations, and saves to some spells.
- **Effects should be worth the price:** Characters paid for these effects in XP and risk.

## Character Optimization

Character optimization allows some characters to excel in specific areas. The issue arises when one character dominates all areas or games focus exclusively on one area.

In DUNGEONS THE DRAGONING, characters are action heroes—"balance" is less critical. Effort went into making each exaltation good in its own way. Classes were adjusted to matter less for final capability.

### Assumptions

- Take up to four hindrances for 1000 starting XP
- XP applied in any order, everything applies before next chunk
- Choose from all six attribute groups (vertical and horizontal)

### Veteran O' The Wheel

Costs 100 XP and a semi-hindrance. If it raises an attribute to 5 dots, that's 400 XP saved. Adding skill to 5 saves another 200 XP. Completely worth it for long games.

### Final Advice

Low attributes are weak points; high attributes are excellent (rolled and kept dice). This revision varied saves to reduce Willpower's importance—spells now call for Size, Intelligence, Fellowship, Composure, Strength, etc. Fear and insanity use Composure and Wisdom.

A combat specialist with 1s in mental/social attributes will suffer against Mind Flayer Stun spells (`8k5` Focus Power, TN 26+ save needed to avoid Stun).

## Sample Optimized Characters

### 6 Weaponry Skill

- **Attributes:** Strength 4
- **Skills:** Weaponry 3, Athletics 2
- **Species:** Tiefling, +1 Weaponry → 4
- **Exaltation:** Atlantean (Weaponry can now go to 6 dots, magic: Conjuration)
- **Class:** Swordsman
- **Backgrounds:** Artifact 3 (mithril bionic arm), Inheritance 3 (best quality short spear)
- **XP Spending:** 200 XP +1 Weaponry → 5; 100 XP Veteran O' The Wheel +1 Str → 5, +1 Weaponry → 6; 200 XP +1 White Raven; 100 XP Weapon Prof (Melee 1)

**Result:** 8k6r1 (~45) attack, 7k2r1+2 (~23) R pen3 damage

### Mega-Evoker

- **Attributes:** Charisma 4
- **Skills:** Academic Lore 3, Forbidden Lore 3, social skills
- **Species:** Any (Gnome for proficiencies or Aasimar for racial asset)
- **Exaltation:** Atlantean (Evocation: Magic Missile)
- **Class:** Initiate → Priest
- **XP Spending:** 400 XP for Divine Ministration, Hatred, Peer, Minor Magic; 200 XP Atlantean Power +1; 100 XP Veteran O' The Wheel +1 Cha → 5

**Result:** Spend Resource Point to roll `9k5` (~41) on Magic Missile at level 5 = six missiles averaging 10 damage each. 16% chance of Fatigue KO on lesser incarnate daemon for single Half Action + Resource Point. Safe casting (need five 10s for Warp), rolls twice on Warp effects.

### Hard to Kill

- **Attributes:** Str 1, Dex 2, Con 4, Int 1, Wis 4, Will 4, Cha 1, Fel 1, Com 3
- **Skills:** Weaponry 3, Acrobatics 3
- **Species:** Squat (+1 Con → 5, +1 Resilience vs damage)
- **Exaltation:** Daemonhost (reduce damage by Con + Power stat, Healing magic)
- **Class:** Rat Catcher/Outcast/Guardsman
- **Backgrounds:** Artifact 3, Inheritance 3
- **XP Spending:** Various assets (Sturdy, No One Tougher, Power Armor, Mithril Shield)

**Result:** 20 HP, 7 Resilience, 29 Static Defense, -6 damage from non-silver/magic, AP 14(all), `8k4` (~35/2 = +17) parry

### Everything

- **Attributes:** All end at 3 dots
- **Skills:** All end at 1 dot
- **Species:** Human (+1 Hero Point)

- **Backgrounds:** Inheritance 3 (best quality cortex implant)

**Result:** Roll `4k3` for everything except combat skills. 4 Hero Points for rerolls + 3 pressure points. Can do anything—thievery, magic, fighting, engineering, socializing, captaining starships.

### Buddy!

- **Backgrounds:** Allies 5, Backing 5, Followers 5, Contacts 5

**Result:** Serious social power through Courtier class track, plus Allies/Backing/Contacts/Fame/Followers/Status each level. Get SM permission first.

### Ultra-Magic

- **Attributes:** Str 1, Dex 2, Con 2, Int 2, Wis 3, Will 4, Cha 4
- **Skills:** Arcana 1, Academic Lore 3, Forbidden Lore 3
- **Species:** Eldarin (+1 Int → 3, +1 Arcana → 2)

- **XP Spending:** Foresight, Implement Focus, Spell Might, Eidetic Memory; Abjuration 1; Ancestral Recall racial asset

**Result:** `7k4` casting with one die rerolled, 90+% success on TN 20. Warp event ~1/2400 chance. Access to all magic schools except Enchantment.

### Crusher

- **Attributes:** Strength 5
- **Skills:** Weaponry 3, Athletics 1
- **Species:** Tiefling
- **Exaltation:** Chosen
- **Class:** Feral
