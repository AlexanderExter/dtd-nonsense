:::note[Quick Reference]

- The **Great Wheel** is the galaxy — thousands of Crystal Spheres in an Astral Sea with **Sigil** at the hub
- **Crystal Spheres** are natural barriers enclosing planetary systems; physics can vary between them
- **The Warp** enables fast travel but causes insanity/death without protection (Geller Field + Navigator)
- **12 Factions** govern Sigil under the absolute rule of the **Lady of Pain**
- Three cosmic forces: Chaos (Ruinous Powers), Law (Blessed Pantheon), Neutral (Gray Council)
:::

## History of the Great Wheel

| Era | When | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The War in Heaven** | 40,000 years ago | The Syrne (first great civilization) warred against the C'tan and their enslaved Modrons. The Syrne uplifted the Eldarin, Orks, and Dragons as weapons. The war destroyed the Syrne and shut down the Portal Network. |
| **The Draconic Ascendency** | 10,000 years ago | Dragons forged an empire under Bahamut. The Tiamat Heresy shattered it, scattering Dragons across the Wheel. |
| **The War of the Monster's Fall** | 7,000 years ago | Eldarin reopened a sealed Portal Relay to Pandemonium, unleashing the Aboleth. First Aasimar and Tieflings created as supersoldiers. |
| **The Fall of the Eldarin** | 5,000 years ago | Eldarin decadence birthed Slaanesh, destroying their homeworlds and creating the Abyss. Survivors split into Eldarin (Craftworlds), Elves (settled), and Dark Eldarin (raiders under Lolth). |
| **The Council Era** | 1,000 years ago | Elves, Squats, Dragonborn, and Eldarin formed the Council in Sigil. Factions organized along philosophical lines. |
| **The Age of the Imperium** | Current day | Humanity emerged using Syrneth ruins to build spelljamming ships. After a brief war with the Eldarin, humans joined the Council. |

**Current threats:**

- **Ork warbands** and **Eldarin pirates** — routine hazards
- **Modrons** may be awakening for another Great March
- Crystal Spheres found stripped bare — whispers blame the **Reapers** who destroyed the Syrne
- The eternal **Blood War** between Law and Chaos

*In the grim darkness of the Great Wheel, there is only war.*

## Cosmology

### The Great Wheel

The galaxy itself — thousands of Crystal Spheres floating in the Astral Sea, arranged in a spiraling wheel with Sigil at its hub. Most of the Wheel lies unexplored.

### The Astral Sea

The void between Crystal Spheres. Empty, cold, and safe, but distances are so vast that crossing between spheres takes years without Portal Relays.

### Crystal Spheres

Natural barriers enclosing planetary systems. Physical laws can differ between spheres — most changes are subtle, but some have radically different physics (fluidic space, reversed time, matter dissolution).

### The Portal Network

Giant constructs built by the Syrne, connecting Crystal Spheres via the Warp. Reduces travel from years to weeks. Sigil serves as a fixed navigation beacon.

### The Warp

A co-existing universe of churning elemental energy shaped by the thoughts and emotions of living beings.

- **Source of all magic**
- Essential for fast travel but extremely dangerous
- Unprotected exposure causes insanity, possession, or death
- A **Navigator** (at least 1 dot in Divination magic) is required for long-distance jumps
- Without a Navigator, ships can only make short hops of ~5 light years

See [Ships](./18-Ships.mdx) for Warp travel mechanics.

### The Umbra

A shallow layer between the real world and the Warp — a dark mirror of reality.

- Geography echoes the material world but is warped by emotion and history
- Supernatural beings (ghosts, daemons, spirits) can cross between Umbra and reality
- Additional depth leads toward the Warp — deeper = more alien and dangerous

## Sigil — The City of Doors

A free-floating torus-shaped space station at the hub of the Great Wheel, outside all Crystal Spheres. Political, cultural, and financial capital of the Wheel. Its light serves as a Warp navigation beacon visible everywhere.

**The Lady of Pain** rules absolutely through her servants, the dabus:

- Rarely decrees, but her word is law
- No god may manifest in Sigil — the last one who tried was destroyed along with all worshippers
- Tolerates the Factions managing daily governance but will annihilate any that overstep

**Anything** can be found in Sigil. Even direst enemies share tavern space under enforced neutrality.

### Faction Ranks

| Rank | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Namers** | Lowest rank; part-time auxiliaries with outside jobs |
| **Factotums** | Full-time faction workers handling important tasks |
| **Factors** | Senior leadership, representing factions publicly |
| **Factol** | Faction leader, chosen from Factors by group decision |

## The Factions of Sigil

| Faction | Philosophy | Role in Sigil |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Believers of the Source** | All things can ascend to godhood through tests across lifetimes | Dispute mediation |
| **Bleak Cabal** | The universe has no inherent meaning — people have only each other | Charities, orphanages |
| **Doomguard** | Everything dies. Entropy is the natural goal of the multiverse | Armory |
| **Dustmen** | Everyone is already dead. Explore "life" to understand Death | Mortuary |
| **Fated** | The universe belongs to those who can take it. Nothing is free | Hall of Records, taxes |
| **Fraternity of Order** | The universe has laws. Learn them all for power beyond sorcery | City courts, lawyers |
| **Harmonium** | Peace through unity of views. The Harmonium is always right | City watch |
| **Mercykillers** | Justice is everything. Every crime must be punished, no mercy | Criminal justice |
| **Sign of One** | Each person is the center of their universe, dreaming reality | Hall of Speakers |
| **Society of Sensation** | The universe is known only through experience | Civic Festhall |
| **Transcendent Order** | Act without thought — train until the right action comes automatically | Diplomacy, advising |
| **Verdant Guild** | Wilderness is the foundation of all life; preserve it | Marketplace, customs |

## Notable Crystal Spheres

| Sphere | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **The Abyss** | Largest sphere — roiling blight visible everywhere. Home to the Ruinous Powers of Chaos. Fortress-world Cadia guards the only Portal Relay out. |
| **Arborea** | Tropical paradise with terraformed weather. Tourist destination. Eldarin-settled, alive with nature spirits and faeries. |
| **Arcadia** | Orderly worlds of geometric landscapes and precise seasons. Strong Modron presence. Aasimar homeland. |
| **Acheron** | War-torn worlds locked in perpetual conflict. Floating iron cubes crash together endlessly. Ork paradise. |
| **Baator** | Nine frozen, hellish layers ruled by Tiefling lords in rigid hierarchy. Aasimar and Tieflings clash constantly. |
| **Beastlands** | Untamed wilderness with massive predators. Entire sphere is one ecosystem. Werewolf homeland. |
| **Bytopia** | Twin worlds facing each other, connected by shared atmosphere. Honest work and small communities. |
| **Carceri** | Prison sphere — six nested layers trapping different prisoners. Escape nearly impossible. |
| **Commorragh** | Hidden Dark Eldarin city-realm in the Webway. Raiders, slavers, and sadistic spectacle. |
| **Elysium** | Peaceful agricultural worlds. Healing springs and places of rest. |
| **Gehenna** | Volcanic hellscape of scheming fiends. Four layers of ever-steeper terrain. |
| **The Grey Waste** | Bleak, colorless expanse draining hope and emotion. Everything decays toward apathy. |
| **Mechanus** | Clockwork world of the Modrons — vast gears and geometric structures. Great Marches launch from here. |
| **Mount Celestia** | Shining mountain of seven layers ascending toward ultimate good. Blessed Pantheon headquarters. |
| **Pandemonium** | Sealed Crystal Sphere — prison of the Aboleth. Howling winds through infinite tunnels. Forbidden to enter. |
