## History of the Great Wheel

The Great Wheel's history spans 40,000 years of conflict, rise, and ruin.

| Era                           | When            | Summary                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The War in Heaven**         | 40,000 years ago | The Syrne, first great civilization, warred against the C'tan and their enslaved Modrons. The Syrne uplifted the Eldarin, Orks, and Dragons as weapons. The war's end destroyed the Syrne and shut down the Portal Network. |
| **The Draconic Ascendency**   | 10,000 years ago | Dragons forged an empire under Bahamut. The Tiamat Heresy shattered it, scattering Dragons across the Wheel.                            |
| **The War of the Monster's Fall** | 7,000 years ago | Eldarin reopened a sealed Portal Relay to Pandemonium, unleashing the Aboleth. The first Aasimar and Tieflings were created as supersoldiers to push them back. |
| **The Fall of the Eldarin**   | 5,000 years ago  | Eldarin decadence birthed the Chaos God Slaanesh, destroying their homeworlds and creating the Abyss. Survivors split into Eldarin (Craftworlds), Elves (settled), and Dark Eldarin (raiders under Lolth). |
| **The Council Era**           | 1,000 years ago  | Elves, Squats, Dragonborn, and Eldarin formed the Council in Sigil. Factions organized along philosophical lines became the ruling forces.  |
| **The Age of the Imperium**   | Current day      | Humanity emerged from their Crystal Sphere using Syrneth ruins to build spelljamming ships. After a brief war with the Eldarin, humans joined the Council and now hold surprising political influence. |

**Current threats:** Ork warbands and Eldarin pirates are routine hazards. The Modrons may be awakening for another Great March. Crystal Spheres have been found stripped bare — whispers blame the Reapers who destroyed the Syrne. The forces of Law and Chaos wage an eternal Blood War.

_In the grim darkness of the Great Wheel, there is only war._

## Cosmology

### The Great Wheel

The galaxy itself — thousands of Crystal Spheres floating in the Astral Sea, arranged in a spiralling wheel with Sigil at its hub. Most of the Wheel lies unexplored.

### The Astral Sea

The void between Crystal Spheres — empty, cold, and safe, but distances are so vast that crossing between spheres takes years without Portal Relays.

### Crystal Spheres

Natural barriers enclosing planetary systems. Physical laws can differ between spheres — most changes are subtle, but some spheres have radically different physics (fluidic space, reversed time, matter dissolution).

### The Portal Network

Giant constructs built by the Syrne, connecting Crystal Spheres via the Warp. Reduces travel from years to weeks. Sigil serves as a fixed navigation beacon.

### The Warp

A co-existing universe of churning elemental energy, shaped by the thoughts and emotions of living beings. Source of all magic. Essential for fast travel but extremely dangerous — unprotected exposure causes insanity, possession, or death. A Navigator is needed for long-distance Warp jumps; without one, ships can only make short hops of ~5 light years.

### The Umbra

A shallow layer between the real world and the Warp — a dark mirror of reality where geography echoes the material world but is warped by emotion and history. Supernatural beings (ghosts, daemons, spirits) can cross between the Umbra and reality. Has additional depth leading toward the Warp — the deeper you go, the more alien and dangerous the landscape becomes.

## Sigil — The City of Doors

A free-floating torus-shaped space station at the hub of the Great Wheel, outside all Crystal Spheres. Political, cultural, and financial capital of the Wheel. Its light serves as a Warp navigation beacon visible everywhere.

**The Lady of Pain** rules Sigil absolutely through her servants, the dabus. She rarely decrees, but her word is law. No god may manifest in Sigil — the last one who tried was destroyed along with all worshippers. The Lady tolerates the Factions managing daily governance but will annihilate any that overstep.

**Anything** can be found in Sigil. Even direst enemies share tavern space under enforced neutrality. The city is a study in barely controlled anarchy — too many ideologies and peoples for true order.

### Faction Ranks

- **Namers** — Lowest rank; part-time auxiliaries with outside jobs
- **Factotums** — Full-time faction workers handling important tasks
- **Factors** — Senior leadership, representing factions publicly
- **Factol** — Faction leader, chosen from Factors by group decision

## Notable Crystal Spheres

| Sphere            | Description                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Abyss**     | Largest sphere — a roiling blight visible everywhere. Home to the Ruinous Powers of Chaos. Fortress-world Cadia guards the only Portal Relay out. |
| **Arborea**       | Tropical paradise with terraformed weather. Tourist destination. Eldarin-settled, alive with nature spirits and faeries. |
| **Arcadia**       | Orderly worlds of geometric landscapes and precise seasons. Strong Modron presence. Aasimar homeland.                |
| **Acheron**       | War-torn worlds locked in perpetual conflict. Floating iron cubes crash together endlessly. Ork paradise.            |
| **Baator**        | Nine frozen, hellish layers ruled by Tiefling lords in a rigid hierarchy. Aasimar and Tieflings clash constantly here. |
| **Beastlands**    | Untamed wilderness with massive predators. The entire sphere is one continuous ecosystem. Werewolf homeland.         |
| **Bytopia**       | Twin worlds facing each other, connected by a shared atmosphere. A place of honest work and small communities.       |
| **Carceri**       | Prison sphere — six nested layers, each trapping different kinds of prisoners. Escape is nearly impossible.           |
| **Commorragh**    | Hidden Dark Eldarin city-realm in the Webway. A nightmare of raiders, slavers, and sadistic spectacle.               |
| **Elysium**       | Peaceful agricultural worlds. Known for healing springs and places of rest. A place of slow recovery.                |
| **Gehenna**       | Volcanic hellscape of scheming fiends. Four layers of ever-steeper terrain where nothing is given freely.            |
| **The Grey Waste** | Bleak, colorless expanse that drains hope and emotion. Everything here decays toward apathy.                        |
| **Mechanus**      | Clockwork world of the Modrons — vast gears and geometric structures. The Modrons' Great Marches launch from here.  |
| **Mount Celestia** | Shining mountain of seven layers ascending toward ultimate good. Headquarters of the Blessed Pantheon.              |
| **Pandemonium**   | Sealed Crystal Sphere — prison of the Aboleth. Howling winds through infinite tunnels. Forbidden to enter.           |
