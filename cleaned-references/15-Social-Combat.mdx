> **Key concepts:** Social combat uses the same structured time as physical [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx) — Initiative, Rounds, Turns, Actions — but attacks target **Resolve** instead of Hit Points. The TN for social attacks is the target's **Mental Defense** (`5 + Composure × 5`). Targets can spend Resolve to resist effects, but after 2 points of Resolve drain in a scene, further attempts auto-fail unless a new approach (Stunt) is tried.

---

## Social Combat Procedure — Step by Step

1. **Determine Surprise** — a character can be socially ambushed (loses first Turn).
2. **Roll Initiative** — `1d10` + **Wisdom** + Composure (not Dexterity).
3. **Take Turns** — each character acts in Initiative order, using social combat actions.
4. **Resolve Effects** — targets either spend Resolve to resist or consent to the attack's effect.
5. **Repeat** until the situation resolves (agreement, withdrawal, or broken will).

Social combat rounds are longer than physical rounds — a minute or more, as needed for the dialogue.

---

## Key Stats

| Stat | Formula | Purpose |
| ---- | ------- | ------- |
| **Resolve** | Willpower + Composure | Social "Hit Points" — depleted by social attacks |
| **Mental Defense** | `5 + (Composure × 5)` | TN for social attacks (analogous to Static Defense) |

**Resolve Recovery:** Each morning, Test Composure (TN 10). Recover 1 point + 1 per Raise. Also gain 1 point for performing a Stunt that furthers your Alignment (SM discretion).

## Social Combat Actions

| Action | Type | Effect |
| ------ | ---- | ------ |
| **Social Attack** | Half | Attack target's Resolve using a social skill |
| **Monologue/Study** | Half/Full | +`1k0` (Half) or +`2k0` (Full) on next social attack |
| **Wordplay** | Half | If you win opposed Test, next social attack can't be Refuted |
| **Support** | Half | Give ally +`1k0` on their next social Test |
| **Speak Carefully** | Full | Social attack at −`1k0`, but +`1k0` to Refute until next turn |
| **Poker Face** | Full | No attacks; +10 Mental Defense, gain extra Reaction |
| **Refute** | Reaction | Negate a social attack (Scrutiny or Lore) |
| **Move** | Free | Movement (flavor only unless fleeing) |

**Action Type Key:** Half = Half Action, Full = Full Action, Reaction = Reaction, Free = Free Action

## Action Descriptions

### Social Attack

**Half Action**

Choose your approach:

- **Characteristic:** Charisma (honest persuasion) or Fellowship (guile/seduction)
- **Skill:** Charm, Command, Deceive, Intimidation, Performer, or Persuasion

Roll Characteristic + Skill against the target's Mental Defense. See [Characteristics & Skills](./03-Characteristics-Skills.mdx) for skill details.

### Monologue/Study

**Half or Full Action**

Prepare your next social attack. Half Action: +`1k0`. Full Action: +`2k0`. Bonus lost if your next action isn't a social attack, or if you use a Reaction before attacking.

### Wordplay

**Half Action**

Opposed Test: your Deceive + Fellowship vs. target's Scrutiny + Wisdom. If you win, your next social attack against that target **cannot be Refuted**. Advantage lost if next action isn't a social attack.

### Support

**Half Action**

Give an ally +`1k0` on their next social Test (act as a wingman, add facts, lend your voice). Maximum two characters may assist on one Test. SM may deem some uses inappropriate.

### Speak Carefully

**Full Action**

Make a social attack at −`1k0`, but gain +`1k0` to all Refute Tests until start of your next turn.

### Poker Face

**Full Action**

Make no social attacks. Gain +10 to Mental Defense and one extra Reaction until your next turn.

### Refute

**Reaction**

Negate a social attack using one of:

- **Wisdom + Scrutiny** — see through the argument. Works against any social attack.
- **Intelligence + Lore** — counter with facts. Applicable when a relevant Lore applies. Gains a **free Raise**.

### Move

**Free Action**

Movement during social combat is flavor unless actively fleeing (which uses a Withdraw action).

## Effects of Social Attacks

Social attacks target **Resolve**, not Hit Points. The TN is the target's Mental Defense. On a successful hit, the target has two choices:

1. **Spend 1 Resolve** to resist the effect entirely.
2. **Consent** — perform the behavior declared by the attacker.

Additional Raises on the social attack have no further effect.

**Scene Limit:** A character can lose at most **2 Resolve** from social attacks per scene. After that, she becomes jaded — further persuasion auto-fails unless the attacker tries a **new approach** (requires a Stunt). Social attacks that would force a character to act against their Alignment always cost a Resolve point to resist.

### Social Attack Effects

| Effect | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Build/Erode Disposition** | Shift target's attitude one step (see Disposition table). Temporary unless repeated Willpower times. |
| **Compel Behavior** | Target spends the rest of the scene doing one task (cannot violate Alignment). |
| **Break Alignment** | Only possible if target is at 0 Resolve and prevented from recovering for days ≥ Devotion. Successful attacks can force Alignment-betraying actions. **SM caution advised for protagonists.** |

## Disposition

A character's disposition determines base difficulty for social influence:

| Attitude | TN Modifier | Meaning |
| -------- | ----------- | ------- |
| Kismesissitude | +10 | Mortal enemy — will sacrifice to harm you |
| Hostile | +5 | Will take risks to hurt you |
| Unfriendly | 0 | Wishes you ill |
| Indifferent | 0 | Doesn't care |
| Friendly | 0 | Wishes you well |
| Helpful | +5 | Will take risks to help you |
| Fanatical | +10 | Absolute devotion |

> **Note:** Helpful and Fanatical targets are easier to influence toward actions they'd want to do, but the +5/+10 modifier represents their conviction — harder to push off their chosen course.
