:::note[Quick Reference]

- Ships are built with **Build Points (BP)** from the Holdings background
- Four hull classes: **Escort**, **Destroyer**, **Cruiser**, **Battleship** (increasing size/power)
- Ship tests use **Crew Assigned** (rolled, max 10) and **Crew Quality** (kept)
- **Bridge Officers** (usually PCs) replace Crew Quality with their own skill for their department
- Combat phases: Surprise → Initiative → Maneuver Action + Department Actions → Round End
- Warp travel requires: Spelljamming helm, Geller Field, Navigator (1+ dot Divination)
:::

See [Vehicles](./17-Vehicles.mdx) for ground/air vehicle rules (parallel system at smaller scale).

## Building a Ship

### Step 1: Determine Build Points

BP budget comes from **Holdings** background dots:

| Holdings | BP |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 5 |
| 5 | 5+ |

### Step 2: Purchase a Hull

Each hull has:

- **Hull Class** — Size tier (affects ramming damage kept dice)
- **Cost** — BP including basic necessities (spaceworthy but defenseless)
- **Crew** — Maximum Crew Size (abstract units: ~10/point Escort, ~20 Destroyer, ~30 Cruiser, ~50 Battleship)
- **Hull Strength** — Hit Points
- **Maneuverability** — Modifier to combat tests
- **Acceleration** — Speed change modifier
- **Speed** — Combat speed in **VU** (Void Units = 6 Mm)
- **Sensors** — Perception-type test modifier
- **Consoles** — Equipment slots by type (Universal fits anything)
- **Weapons** — Hardpoints (Forward/Rear arcs)

## Step 3: Outfitting

Every hull includes basic systems (sensors, life support, quarters, engines, shuttlebay, reactor, Geller Field, spelljamming helm). Shields, weapons, and consoles are purchased separately.

### Crew Quality

| 2 (default) | 0 BP |

| 4 | +20 BP |

## Space Combat

### Combat Flow

1. **Surprise** — Surprised ship loses turn 1
2. **Initiative** — Sensors + Acceleration + `1d10`
3. **Turns** — Each ship takes a Maneuver Action + department actions
4. **Round End** — Repeat until resolved

### Ship Tests

Roll = Crew Assigned (max 10 dice) keeping Crew Quality. **Bridge Officers** substitute their own skill for Crew Quality in their department:

| Position | Department |
| --- | --- |
| **Helmsman** | Maneuver Actions |
| **Tactical Officer** | Tactical Actions |
| **Chief Engineer** | Engineering Actions |
| **Captain** | Command Actions |
| **Chief Arcana Officer** | Arcana Actions |

### Static Defense

`3 x Crew Quality + Maneuverability` (modified by actions/consoles)

---

## Maneuver Actions

**Required every turn.** Failure = all attacks auto-hit + potential crash.

| Action | Movement | Test | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Move** | Half or full Speed + 90 degree turn | None (1 Crew required) | Basic movement |
| **Adjust Speed** | Half or full Speed | Pilot + Acc vs TN 20 | Speed +/-1 per success (+/-1 per 2 raises) |
| **Adjust Heading** | Half Speed | TN 15/25/35 | 90 degree before / before+after / 180 degree turn |
| **Evasive Maneuvers** | Half Speed | Pilot Test | TN to hit = result + Maneuver (until next turn) |

---

## Tactical Actions

### Shoot Guns

Fire any weapons in range/arc. TN to hit = Static Defense.

- **Lance:** Fired individually
- **Array:** Can be linked (same test, same target, resolve damage separately)

### Boarding Party

Within 1 VU. Opposed Weaponry/Brawling. Success: target loses 2 Crew + 1/raise. Failure: lose half Crew used.

### Target Subsystem

Requires prior Active Augury. Ballistics -`1k0` or win Boarding with 2 raises. Disables console until Emergency Repair.

### Ramming Speed

End within 1 VU facing target. Opposed Piloting + Maneuver. Damage = Speed or kept dice (whichever higher) k [Hull Class kept dice: Escort 2, Destroyer 3, Cruiser 4, Battleship 5]. Ignores shields. Ramming ship takes half.

## Attacks and Damage

1. **Fire Everything** — Declare all weapons firing (Lance individually, Array can link)
2. **Hit** — TN = 3x Crew Quality + Maneuverability
3. **Shields** — Damage hits shields first. Regen reduced by total Disruption of all weapons that hit
4. **Hull** — If shields down, damage goes to hull → roll Critical Damage + weapon's Crit rating
5. **Results** — Shields at 0: collapsed (no regen during combat). Hull at 0: ship explodes

## Fighter Craft

Ships with Fighter Bays launch fighters as minion squads:

- Devote Crew → squad of 2x that crew, Quality = Crew Quality
- SD = 5x Crew Quality
- Base damage = 5; attack range = 2x Crew Quality in VU
- No shield disruption or crit bonus
- Can: prevent one Boarding, sacrifice one fighter to block one weapon hit, replace ship's SD with opposed roll

## Silent Running

Shut down non-essential systems to hide:

- Speed halved; all tests +10 difficulty
- Failed Maneuver reveals position
- Only Arcana Action: Maintain Silent Running (result = TN to detect)
- Firing weapons immediately reveals position

## Surface Bombardment

Ships in low orbit can bombard the surface.

**Procedure:**

1. Enter low orbit: Maneuver + Acc vs TN 25 (failure by 2+ = second test or crash)
2. Fire: TN 25 to hit (min target = city block). Miss scatters `1k2` km (+`1k0`/Check)

**Lance orbital damage:** Everything in crater annihilated; blast wave radiates outward, halving per increment.

| Weapon | Crater | Blast | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light Las | 150m | 400m | `10k10` |
| Heavy Las | 200m | 600m | `10k10`+30 |
| Heavy Melta | 250m | 700m | `10k10`+55 |

**Torpedo ground burst:** Photon/Cruise = 300m crater, 600m blast, `10k10`+60. Rift = 800m crater, 1600m blast, `10k10`+100.

## Warp Travel

**Requirements:** Spelljamming helm, Geller Field, Navigator (1+ dot Divination magic).

### Step 0: Leave the Crystal Sphere

Active Augury + Sensors vs TN 20 (+10 with map/prior entry). Syrneth Portal Stations auto-detected. Typically 500 VU away.

**Create Portal:** Arcana vs TN 25 (+5 per shield mark). Success: open 1d5 hours. Failure by 2+: unstable portal → Maneuver + Acc vs TN 20 or `8k6` hull damage.

### Step 1: Open Warp Portal

Navigator Arcana + Intelligence vs TN 15 (+`2k0` with Ancient Helm). Failure: 1d10 Fatigue to Navigator.

### Step 2: Chart the Course

Navigator Divination + Wisdom vs TN 20 (+ Sensors modifier).

- Success by 2+ raises: +`1k0` per 2 raises to Step 3
- Failure: -`3k0` to Step 3

### Step 3: Steer the Vessel

Piloting + Wisdom + Acceleration vs Voyage TN:

| TN | Duration | Distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 1d10+5 days | Less than half sector |
| 15 | 1d10 weeks | Other side of sector |
| 25 | 1d10+5 weeks | Near side of next sector |
| 30 | 2d5 months | Somewhere in next sector |
| 35 | 1d10+5 months | Skipping past next sector |
| 40 | 1d5 years | More than halfway across explored space |

- **Success per 2 raises:** Reduce time by 1 step (min 1d10+5 days)
- **Failure:** Warp Encounters at cumulative +1; another test at -`1k0`; time +1 step

### Step 4: Leave the Warp

Navigator Arcana + Intelligence vs TN 15. Failure: dangerously close to object. 2+ Checks: trapped in Warp; Navigator takes warp damage.

## Repairs and Resupply

| Method | Hull Repair | Crew Recovery | Resupply | Torpedoes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wealth** | 1d10/week in port | 1/week | 1 week | Wealth tests |
| **Backing** (approved) | 1d10+1/week | 1/week | 1 week | Weeks = cost |
| **Backing** (not approved) | 1d10/2 weeks | 1/2 weeks | 1d10 weeks paperwork | None |
| **Followers** | Chief Engineer's Craft/week | 1/week local | 1d10 weeks scavenging | Acquisition tests |

### Extended Repairs

Between ports (once, twice with Extended Supply Vaults):

1. Find anchorage (orbit, asteroid, planet)
2. Assign Crew (unavailable for other duties)
3. After 1–2 months: Tech-Use vs TN 25

- **Failure:** Damage too severe
- **Success:** Restore `2k1` Hull, repair all critical effects
- **Per Raise:** Restore 1 console OR 1d5 Hull

### Acquiring Equipment

In port with shipyard: Wealth test vs TN 15 + BP cost. Installation: 1 day/BP. Resale value: zero.

## SM Advice for Spelljamming

1. **Don't fight in empty space** — use asteroid fields, Crystal Sphere edges, gas giants, Warp rifts
2. **Go easy on torpedoes** — four tubes with Extended Supply Vaults can end fights in 2 rounds
3. **Cap at 10 crew per roll** — prevents dodge-tanks from breaking the game
4. **90 degree turns aren't exact** — let players do 27 degrees to barely miss an asteroid
