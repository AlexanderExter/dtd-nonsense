:::note[Quick Reference]

- Six disciplines of ranged combat for warriors known as **Gunslingers**
- **Gunslinger Level** = highest Gun Kata dots (applies across all Kata, like Martial Adept Level)
- Build custom **Trick Shots** by combining Advantages (costing Style Points) with Restrictions (granting Style Points)
- Cost: **50 XP per Style Point** to buy or improve a Trick Shot
- Weapons with **Blast**, **Flame**, or explosives/grenades **cannot** be used with Trick Shots
- Cross-ref: [Feats](./07-Feats.mdx) for ranged Feats, [Equipment](./10-Equipment.mdx) for weapon types, [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx) for action economy
:::

## Gunslinger Level

A character's **Gunslinger Level** equals their highest Gun Kata dots. Like Sword Schools, different Gun Kata build on each other — the highest level always applies, even for level-related effects of other Kata.

---

## Building Trick Shots

Trick Shots work identically to Special Attacks (see [Sword Schools](./12-Sword-Schools.mdx)), using the same Style Point economy:

1. **Choose a base action** — restricted by which Kata you know
2. **Choose a weapon type** — restricted by your known Kata
3. **Add Advantages** — each costs Style Points. Mix from different Kata
4. **Add Restrictions** — each grants Style Points to fund Advantages beyond your Gunslinger Level
5. **Pay XP** — 50 XP per Style Point of Advantages
6. **Name it** — every Trick Shot gets a name

**Style Point budget:** Free Style Points = Gunslinger Level. Beyond that, add Restrictions.

**Free Style Points on level-up:** When learning a new mastery level, gain free Style Points equal to the new level.

---

Available to all Gunslingers. Stackable modifiers can be taken multiple times.

- **Key Skill:** Performer
- **Weapons:** Ordinary (see [Equipment](./10-Equipment.mdx) for Ordinary ranged weapons)
- **Action:** Called Shot

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Apprentice | Weapon (Ordinary) | — | Use Trick Shots with Ordinary ranged weapons |
| 1: Apprentice | Action (Called Shot) | — | Use Called Shot with Trick Shots. Attack-related advantages apply to the attack benefiting from the called shot bonus |
| 2: Initiate | Safe Shot | (-2) | This attack does no damage |
| 2: Initiate | Speed Loader | 1 | After this Trick Shot, reload weapon as a Reaction Action |
