> **Quick Reference:**
>
> - **Traits** define innate creature abilities (Daemonic, Regeneration, Undead, etc.)
> - **Stat blocks** use a standardized format: Characteristics → Speed/Size/Resilience → Skills → Feats → Armor → Attacks → Abilities → Traits → Gear
> - **Minion Squads** abstract groups of weak enemies into Threat/Damage ratings — see [Minion Rules](#minions) below
> - **NPC threat ranges** from Level 1 (noncombatants) to Level 5 (dragons)
> - For combat mechanics, see [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx); for conditions and fear, see [Conditions](./16-Conditions.mdx)

## Traits

Traits are innate abilities gained by birth and racial circumstance. They may rarely be gained later in life. Racial abilities are one example of traits.

### Amphibious

Never needs Tests to move through difficult water conditions. **Doubles** swimming speed.

### Amorphous

All hits go to body. **Doubles** Hit Points.

### Armor Plating (X)

Natural armor rating (tough skin, scales, bone ridges). Protects all locations unless noted otherwise. Unlike worn armor, Armor Plating does not penalize Static Defense or apply a maximum Dexterity limit.

### Aura (X)

Reduces magical damage as Armor reduces physical. Protects all locations. Indicates great magical power or wards.

### Auto-Stabilized

Always counts as braced for heavy weapons. May fire full-auto as a Half Action.

### Caster

Can cast spells with listed magic school ranks. Follows normal casting rules. Always counts as sanctioned.

### Crawler

Base speed halved, but ignores terrain penalties.

### Daemonic

Warp-manifested creature. Gains extra Hit Points and Armor equal to Constitution score. Stacks with all other armor.

### Dark Sight

Sees normally in total darkness. Never penalized by lighting.

### Fear

Inspires terror — targets must roll against the listed Fear rating. See [Conditions](./16-Conditions.mdx) for Fear rules.

### Flyer

Natural flight via wings, warp energy, or gas sacs. Speed while flying listed after trait (default: double ground speed).

### Machine

Inorganic construction. Immune to vacuum, cold extremes, mind-influencing effects. Gains listed armor to all locations. Does not breathe.

### Mindless

No Charisma, Fellowship, Composure, or Intelligence. Auto-fails social Tests. Immune to social attacks and mind control.

### Phasing

Can become incorporeal (Half Action to shift). Gains two extra Raises on Stealth when hiding inside objects. Immune to normal weapons — only magic weapons and Power Field weapons affect them.

### Quadruped

Ground speed doubled (uses all four legs).

### Regeneration

Regains HP equal to Regeneration score at the start of each turn.

### Resource Stat

Has a resource pool (like an Exalt). Type and amount listed with trait. Follows normal resource rules.

### Stuff of Nightmares

Immune to: poisons, diseases, breathing requirements, most environmental hazards, bleeding, stunning. Ignores critical results unless they destroy outright (unless caused by Spell, Power Weapon, or similar).

### Undead

Not alive — animated by sorcery and warp energy. No need to breathe, eat, or drink. Immune to blood loss, fatigue, and stunning.

### Unnatural Toughness

Hit Points doubled (including from Sound Constitution).

## Daemonic Possession

What happens when someone gets possessed by a daemon? It depends on the daemon. There are two procedures depending on whether you're using the stat-block daemons from the Antagonists chapter or custom/generated daemons.

### Method 1: Stat-Block Daemons

For daemons from the Antagonists chapter, take the selected victim and:

1. Increase their Strength, Constitution, and Willpower by 1 each if it's a Lesser Daemon, and by 2 each if it's a Greater Daemon. This can take stats to 6 or higher.
2. Add the traits **Daemonic** (+Con to AP & HP), **Dark Sight**, and **Resource**.
3. Increase the victim's Level by 1 to a maximum of the possessing daemon's level.
4. Increase their Resilience by 1.
5. Recalculate Hit Points, Speed, and other derived stats.
6. If it's a Greater Daemon, they also get the warp fire breath weapon.

### Method 2: Generated/Custom Daemons

For generated or randomly created daemons, take the victim and:

1. Increase their Strength, Constitution, and Resilience by 1. Increase Level by +1 if it's less than the daemon's Level.
2. Add the **Darksight** trait and make them a **Daemonhost** exalt with a Power stat equal to the daemon's Level. This means a magic school, bonus armor, Resource Points, etc.
3. If the daemon has any feats, special powers, extra magic schools, gun katas, or sword schools, add those too.
4. Use the better of the daemon's or victim's skills.
5. Recalculate Hit Points, Speed, and other derived stats.
6. If it's a Greater Daemon, they also get the warp fire breath weapon.

## Minions

For heroes who are more than human, some enemies simply aren't individual threats. Minion Squads abstract groups of weak enemies into simple mechanics.

> For full combat rules, see [Combat](./14-Combat.mdx). Minions interact with all standard combat rules but use simplified stat blocks.

### Minion Squad Rules

Squads contain **up to six** minions (the most that can engage one target at once).

**Threat Rating (1–5)** — general competence:

- **1** — Peasants, hobos, untrained civilians
- **3** — Pirates, military soldiers
- **5** — Ninja assassins, elite supernatural enforcers

**Combat Rolls:** Keep dice = Threat Rating. Roll dice = number of minions acting. Cannot keep more than rolled.

> *Example:* Five Space Pirates (Threat 3) attack → roll `5k3`. Only two attacking → `2k2`.

**Static Defense:** Threat Rating × 5. A hit removes one minion; each Raise removes one additional. Blast weapons remove minions equal to Blast rating.

> *Example:* To hit Pirates (Threat 3), need TN 15. Taking out three at once = two Raises (TN 25).

**Damage Rating (1–4):** On hit, deal 5 × Damage Rating, +5 per Raise. Ranged attack range = 10 × Threat Rating meters.

> *Example:* Pirates (Damage 3) roll 26 to hit (TN 20) = one Raise → 20 damage.

---

### Teaming Up with Minions

Characters add highest Threat Rating of allied minions to every skill roll. Cannot benefit from more minions than their Fellowship. Allied minions must be targeted separately; multiple can be felled for one Raise per minion beyond the first.

---

### Minions and Special Weapons

- **Flamers:** Minions roll to dodge (TN). Success = one survivor + one per Raise. Failure = all die (ammo may cook off on Checks). Minions with flamers: TN to dodge = 5 × number of minions, or spray 30° arc per minion at TN 10 with half damage.
- **Grenades:** Minions with grenades explode. Like giving the Three Stooges a lit stick of dynamite.
