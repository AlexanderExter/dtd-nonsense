:::note[Quick Reference]
Combat uses **structured time** divided into Rounds (~10 seconds each). Each character gets one Turn per Round. On your turn: one **Full Action** OR two different **Half Actions**, plus one **Reaction** per round and unlimited **Free Actions**. Attack TN = target's **Static Defense** (`10 + (Dex + Wis) × 3 − Size × 2`). Damage is reduced by **Armor** (physical) or **Aura** (magical), then compared against **Resilience** to determine Hit Points lost.
:::

---

## Narrative Time vs Structured Time

- **Narrative Time** — time passes as the story requires. No precise tracking needed.
- **Structured Time** — used for combat and complex encounters. Divided into **Rounds**, **Turns**, and **Actions**.

| Unit | Definition |
| ---- | ---------- |
| **Round** | Every character takes one Turn. ~10–12 seconds regardless of participant count. |
| **Turn** | One character's opportunity to act, resolved in Initiative order. |
| **Actions** | What a character does on their Turn (see Action Economy below). |

---

## Action Economy Summary

| Action Type | Allowance per Turn | Notes |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ----- |
| **Full Action** | 1 (instead of Half Actions) | Consumes entire turn |
| **Half Action** | Up to 2 different ones | Cannot take the same Half Action twice |
| **Reaction** | 1 per Round | Used outside your turn (Dodge, Parry, Refute) |
| **Free Action** | Unlimited | Trivial effort (speaking, dropping items) |
| **Extended Action** | Multiple rounds | Progress lost if interrupted |

:::note
**Action Priority:** All game events are sequential, never truly simultaneous. The SM determines priority order.
:::

## Combat Round Procedure

<Steps>

1. **Surprise** (first round only) — SM determines if anyone is caught unaware. Surprised characters lose their first Turn and grant combat advantage.
2. **Roll Initiative** — each participant rolls `1d10` + Dexterity + Composure. Highest goes first. Ties: higher die roll wins → higher Dex wins → reroll.
3. **Determine Initiative Order** — SM ranks all rolls highest to lowest.
4. **Combatants Take Turns** — from top of initiative, each character takes a turn as the **active character**.
5. **Round Ends** — end-of-round effects expire.
6. **Repeat** steps 4–5 until combat resolves.

</Steps>

## Surprise

Surprise only affects the first round of combat. The SM determines who is Surprised based on:

- **Hidden attackers** — successful Stealth Tests conceal combatants
- **Masking conditions** — rain, engine noise, explosions, etc.
- **Distractions** — target's attention is elsewhere

**Effects of Surprise:**

- Lose your Turn in the first Round (cannot act)
- Grant **combat advantage** to all non-Surprised attackers
- Recovery is automatic after the Surprise round resolves

**Immunity:** Paragons and characters with the **Danger Sense** Feat cannot be surprised. They should be informed their danger sense triggered, then determine what they know and will do before Initiative is rolled.

## Perception & Stealth

Rules for actively looking for things that can reasonably be missed. Spotting a camouflaged tank in the jungle is appropriate; spotting a drunk fan in an empty field is not. Don't penalize everyday Perception Tests for lacking skill dots — reserve the untrained penalty for special trials.

### Automatic Spotting

Characters automatically spot things at **Size × 10 meters**. Double this distance for each applicable factor:

- Open, clear terrain
- Nothing else nearby
- Only moving thing in view
- Large vehicle (Size 11+)

### Spotting TNs by Range

Beyond automatic range, base spotting TN is **10 at 100m**, +5 per additional 100m. Subtract **twice the target's Size** from the TN.

| ------- | --- |
| 50m     | 5   |
| 100m    | 10  |
| 200m    | 15  |
| 300m    | 20  |
| 400m    | 25  |
| 500m    | 30  |
| 600m    | 35  |
| 700m    | 40  |
| 800m    | 45  |
| 900m    | 50  |
| 1km     | 55  |
| 1–5km   | +5 per 200m (TN 95 at 5km) |
| 5–50km  | +5 per km (TN 140 at 50km)  |

| −`4k0`     | Extreme visual cover, short LoS, jungle             |

| −`0k3`     | Half moon, badly lit parking lot                    |
| −`0k4`     | Starlight only, barely see own body                 |
| −`0k5`     | Total darkness, zero visibility fog                 |

**Movement** (modifies spotting TN):

| Modifier | Condition                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| −5       | Target is continually moving                       |

| −15      | Target leaving a trail (exhaust, dust)             |
| −20      | Target is the only thing moving                    |

| Modifier | Condition                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| +15      | Extremely low contrast (black on black at night)   |
| +5       | Low contrast (camouflage, matching background)     |
| −5       | High contrast (hunter's orange safety vest)        |
| −15      | Extremely high contrast (lit up at night)          |

**Distractions:**

| Modifier | Condition                                          |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |

| +`4k0`     | Intense focus aid (watching for specific threat)    |
| +`2k0`     | Moderate focus (which window are shots from?)       |
| +`1k0`     | Blessed silence                                     |

### Perception vs. Stealth Resolution

Perception typically uses Wisdom; Stealth typically uses Dexterity. Alternate pairings:

- **Stealth + Intelligence** — deducing blind spots in security cameras
- **Perception + Willpower** — fighting boredom during long watches
- **Stealth + Constitution** — holding perfectly still for extended periods
- **Perception + Fellowship** — getting a good look without being rude

**Opposed Tests:** When range isn't a factor and the sneaker would be automatically spotted without active Stealth, use a straight opposed Test. When range modifiers also apply, choose one scaling option:

1. **Higher of** general spotting TN or Stealth result (easiest — recommended default)
2. Add **half** the Stealth result to the general spotting TN
3. Add the **full** Stealth result to the general spotting TN

**Incidental perception:** If a character isn't actively looking (driving past a shop, watching TV), halve rolled dice after modifiers.

**Hearing modifiers:** ×2 TN for each intervening wall, each layer of hearing protection, or background noise of a higher level. +5 TN for each intervening chunk of forest/hill, interior door, or background noise of the same level. −`1k1` (or +10 TN) per doubling of distance.

**Identification range:** Identifying a specific sound (caliber, engine type, words spoken) is at roughly ¼ to ⅕ of detection range, or 1/10 if unprepared or facing away.

**Silencers:** Reduce effective distance category by the silencer's quality level. A poor silencer moves pistol shots down to "light rioting" distances; best quality moves them to "crying baby" distances.

## Types of Actions

- **Full Action** — consumes the entire turn. Cannot also take Half Actions.
- **Half Action** — fairly simple. Take up to two different Half Actions per turn (never the same one twice).
- **Reaction** — responds to an event outside your turn. One per Round.
- **Free Action** — trivial effort (speaking, dropping items). Unlimited per turn.
- **Extended Action** — spans multiple rounds. All progress is lost if interrupted or abandoned.

### Action Subtypes

Every action has one or more subtypes (Attack, Movement, Melee, Ranged, Concentration, Miscellaneous, Defense). Subtypes clarify restrictions — e.g., an Immobilized character cannot perform Movement-subtype actions.

## Action Descriptions

### Aid Another

**Half Action** | Miscellaneous

Give an adjacent ally +`1k0` on their next Test. The ally must have at least one dot in the relevant skill (if it's a Skill Test). Cannot aid supernatural template abilities, Free Actions, or resistance Tests (disease, poison, Fear). Maximum two characters may assist on a single Test.

### Aim

**Half or Full Action** | Melee or Ranged

Half Action: +`1k0` on next attack. Full Action: +`2k0` on next attack. Benefits lost if next action isn't an attack or if you use a Reaction first. Works for both melee and ranged.

### All-Out Attack

**Full Action** | Attack, Melee

Melee attack at +`2k0` to hit and +`1k0` to damage. Lose all Reactions until your next turn.

### Brace

**Half Action** | Miscellaneous

Brace a heavy weapon for firing (stand, windowsill, wide stance). Cannot move after bracing. Firing a heavy weapon without bracing: −`3k0` to attack.

### Bull Rush

**Half Action** | Attack, Melee, Movement

Opposed Size + Strength Test against a melee-range target. On success, push target 2m + 2m per Raise.

### Called Shot

**Full Action** | Attack, Melee or Ranged

Declare a hit location, then attack at −`2k0`. On success, hit goes to the named location instead of rolling.

### Charge

**Full Action** | Attack, Melee, Movement

Target must be 4+ meters away. Move up to 2× Speed in a straight line, then attack at +`1k0`. May substitute a Grapple, Bull Rush, or Knock Down for the attack.

### Delay

**Half Action** | Miscellaneous

Save a Half Action for later. Use it any time before your next turn. If used in response to another's action, yours resolves first.

### Disarm

**Half Action** | Attack, Melee

Opposed Level + Weaponry Test. Disarm opponent if you win by two or more Raises.

### Dodge

**Reaction** | Movement

When attacked, Test Dexterity + Acrobatics. **Half** the result adds to your Static Defense against that attack. Must be aware of the attack.

:::note
The Feat "Combat Sense" allows Wisdom to replace Dexterity in Dodge Tests.
:::

### Feint

**Half Action** | Attack, Melee

Opposed Level + Weaponry Test. If you win, your next melee attack against that target cannot be Dodged or Parried. Advantage lost if next action isn't a melee attack.

### Fight Defensively

**Full Action** | Attack, Melee or Ranged

Attack at −`2k0`, but gain +`2k0` to all Dodge and Parry Tests until your next turn.

### Focus Power

**Variable Action** | Varies by power, Provokes

Cast a spell. The spell lists the TN and magic skill Tested.

### Full Auto Burst

**Full Action** | Attack, Ranged, Provokes

Requires a fully-automatic weapon. Test Ballistics at +`2k1`. Each Raise means an additional hit, adding +`1k0` damage each (up to weapon's Rate of Fire).

:::note
Weapons with the **Storm** property replace +`1k0` per raise with +`2k0` per raise (non-stacking).
:::

Moving up to Dexterity meters during this action replaces the +`2k1` bonus with a −`1k0` penalty. Pistol/basic class weapons only.

### Full Defense

**Full Action** | Melee

Make no attacks. Gain +10 Static Defense and one extra Reaction until your next turn.

### Grapple

**Half or Full Action** | Attack, Melee

**Initiating:** Half Action Brawl Test to hit. On success, you become the **controller**.

**Maintaining:** Controller spends a Full Action each turn (or grapple ends), then makes an Opposed Size + Strength Test. On success, choose one:

- **Attack** — damage with fists, claws, or one-handed melee weapon. No Reactions allowed from target.
- **Throw Down** — target becomes Prone.
- **Push** — Bull Rush (must move with target to maintain grapple, or let go).
- **Ready/Use Item** — ready or use an item (SM may allow grabbing opponent's item).
- **Stand** — regain feet (may drag target up).

**Escaping (as target):** Must declare Grapple as a Half Action first (the penalty for being grappled), then choose:

- **Break Free** — Opposed Size + Strength. On win, free + any Half Action.
- **Slip Free** — Athletics + Dexterity vs. controller's Athletics + Strength. On win, free + any Half Action.
- **Take Control** — Opposed Athletics + Strength. On win, become controller and immediately take one controller option.

### Healing Surge

**Half Action** | Miscellaneous

Spend Resource Points up to your Level to heal that many Hit Points. Gain +5 Static Defense until your next turn.

### Knock Down

**Half Action** | Attack, Melee

Opposed Size + Strength. Win: target knocked Prone. If target wins by 3+ Raises, **you** fall Prone instead.

### Move

**Half or Full Action** | Movement, Provokes

Half: move Speed meters. Full: move 2× Speed meters. Ending adjacent to an opponent engages melee. Moving away from a melee-engaged opponent provokes Opportunity Attacks.

### Multiple Attacks

**Full Action** | Attack, Melee or Ranged

Attack more than once. Requires two weapons or an appropriate Feat (Swift Attack, Lightning Attack). Spend one Reaction per additional attack beyond the first.

### Opportunity Attack

**Free Action** | Attack, Melee

When an opponent uses an action with the **Provokes** keyword while engaged in melee with you, make a Standard Attack against them. Once per turn, usable during any character's turn.

### Overwatch

**Full Action** | Attack, Ranged, Provokes

Establish a 45° kill zone and specify Full Auto Burst or Suppressing Fire with trigger conditions. Fire when conditions are met before your next turn. Performing any action or Reaction (except speech) cancels Overwatch.

### Parry

**Reaction** | Defense, Melee

When hit by a melee attack, Test Weaponry (kept dice = Weaponry dots, +Level`k0` if proficient). **Half** the result adds to Static Defense against that attack. Must be aware of the attack.

### Ready

**Half Action** | Miscellaneous, Provokes

Draw, stow, or prepare a weapon or item. Also covers applying bandages, coating blades with poison, etc. Dropping an item is a Free Action.

### Reload

**Variable Action** | Miscellaneous, Provokes

Reload a ranged weapon. Time depends on the weapon.

### Run

**Full Action** | Movement, Provokes

Move 6× Speed meters. Until next turn: −`2k0` to ranged attacks against you, +`2k0` to melee attacks against you.

### Shift

**Half Action** | Movement

Move Dexterity meters. Does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks.

### Stand

**Half Action** | Movement, Provokes

Stand up from Prone.

### Standard Attack

**Half Action** | Attack, Melee or Ranged

Make one melee or ranged attack.

### Suppressing Fire

**Full Action** | Attack, Ranged, Provokes

Requires fully-automatic weapon. Establish a 45° kill zone and fire a Full Auto Burst. Targets in the zone must pass a Pinning Test or become [Pinned](./16-Conditions.mdx). The Ballistics Test (TN 20) determines random hits — each Raise assigns another hit (up to RoF). SM assigns targets; attacker cannot choose to fail.

### Tactical Advance

**Full Action** | Movement, Defense

Move from cover to cover (up to 2× Speed). Retain benefits of original cover for the duration of the move.

### Use a Skill

**Variable Action** | Concentration, Miscellaneous

Use a skill. Duration and Test details per individual skill descriptions.

### Withdraw

**Full Action** | Movement

Break off from melee and move up to Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

## The Attack

Before attacking, verify prerequisites:

- **Melee** — must be engaged in melee with the target
- **Ranged** — cannot be engaged in melee (exception: pistol-class weapons)
- **Both** — must be aware of the target

### Attack Steps

**Step 1 — Apply Modifiers** to the attack Test (bonuses/penalties combine).

**Step 2 — Make the Attack Test.** Roll the appropriate skill (Weaponry for melee, Ballistics for ranged, Brawl for unarmed). Kept dice = skill dots. Add +Level`k0` if proficient with the weapon. No Characteristic is added.

The TN is the target's **Static Defense**:

`Static Defense = 10 + (Dexterity + Wisdom) × 3 − (Size × 2)`

**Dodge/Parry Reactions:** If hit, the target may use a Reaction to Dodge or Parry. Half the Reaction Test result adds to Static Defense for that attack.

**Step 3 — Determine Hit Location** (roll `1d10`):

| Roll | Location  |
| ---- | --------- |
| 1    | Left Leg  |
| 2    | Right Leg |
| 3–6  | Body      |
| 7    | Gizzards  |
| 8    | Left Arm  |
| 9    | Right Arm |
| 10   | Head      |

**Step 4 — Determine Damage.** Roll the weapon's damage dice. Melee weapons add Strength as rolled dice.

**Step 5 — Apply Damage.** Subtract **Armor Points** (physical attacks) or **Aura** (spells). If reduced to 0, attack is shrugged off. Remaining damage ÷ target's **Resilience** = Hit Points lost (round down, discard remainder).

:::note
Armor blocks physical damage. Aura blocks magical damage. These are distinct values.
:::

If the target is at 0 Hit Points, additional HP loss becomes **Critical Damage** instead.

### Unarmed Combat

Resolved like melee attacks but uses **Brawl** instead of Weaponry. Base damage: `0k1` + Strength (rolled). Cannot Parry weapon attacks without a Stunt. If a target loses at least 1 HP from an unarmed attack, also inflict 1 level of [Fatigue](./16-Conditions.mdx).

## Combat Situations

| Situation | Effect |
| --------- | ------ |
| **Combat Advantage** | Free raise to hit (target distracted, outnumbered, stunned) |
| **Cover** | Hit locations behind cover use cover's AP instead |
| **Concealment** | +5 or more to Static Defense |
| **Difficult Terrain** | −`1k0` or more to melee attacks and Dodging |
| **Engaged in Melee** | −`2k0` to Ballistics Tests (ignored if target grants combat advantage) |
| **Extreme Range** (>3× range) | −`3k0` to hit |
| **Long Range** (>2× range) | −`1k0` to hit |
| **Short Range** (&lt;½ range) | +`1k0` to hit |
| **Point Blank** (≤2m, not engaged) | +`2k1` to hit |
| **Ganging Up** (2:1) | +`1k0` melee to hit |
| **Ganging Up** (3:1+) | +`2k0` melee to hit |
| **Higher Ground** | +`1k0` melee to hit |
| **Off Hand** | −`2k0` (negated by Ambidextrous Asset) |
| **Two Weapon Fighting** | −`3k0` each (reduced by Ambidextrous/TWF Feat) |
| **Weapon Jammed** | More 1s than Ballistics skill = jam. Full Action TN 15 to clear. |

### Cover AP Values

| Cover Type | AP |
| ---------- | -- |
| Armor-glass, pipes, thin metal | 4 |
| Storage crate, sandbags, ice | 8 |
| Computer bank, stasis pod | 16 |
| Rockcrete, hatchway, thick iron, stone | 24 |
| Armaplas, bulkhead, plasteel | 32 |

### Falling

Take `1k1` damage per 2m fallen, add Size as rolled dice. Armor provides half AP. Acrobatics Test (TN 15): success = −`1k0` damage, each Raise = additional −`1k0`. Intentional jumps: ignore first 2m per Acrobatics dot. Drop: ignore 1m per Dexterity dot.

### Two Weapon Fighting Details

Attack with Multiple Attacks action. Each attack at −`3k0`. Modifiers:

- **Ambidextrous** Asset: reduce by `1k0` (to −`2k0`)
- **Two Weapon Fighting** Feat: reduce by `2k0` (to −`1k0`)
- **Both**: no penalty

## Injury

### Hit Points

`Maximum Hit Points = (Constitution + Willpower) × 2`

Feats, racial abilities, or template effects may increase this. When current HP drops to 0 or below, the character begins taking Critical Damage.

### Damage and Resilience

For every multiple of a character's **Resilience** taken in damage, she loses 1 Hit Point (remainder is wasted). If Resilience would be 0 or less, treat it as 1.

**Damage reduction:**

- **Armor Points** — reduce physical damage (ineffective against spells)
- **Aura** — reduces spell damage (ineffective against physical)

### Damage Types

| Type | Examples |
| ---- | -------- |
| **Energy** | Lasers, fire, plasma |
| **Explosive** | Grenades, bolt rounds |
| **Impact** | Clubs, bullets, falling |
| **Rending** | Swords, claws |

Unspecified damage is treated as Impact. Type determines Critical Effects.

### Critical Damage

When a character at 0 HP takes more damage, it becomes **Critical Damage** (cumulative). Each instance of Critical Damage triggers **Critical Effects** from the appropriate table (by body location + damage type). Critical Damage does not heal naturally — requires medical attention.

## Healing

| Wound State | Threshold | Natural Healing | Bed Rest |
| ----------- | --------- | --------------- | -------- |
| **Lightly Wounded** | HP lost ≤ Willpower | 1 HP/day | Constitution HP/day |
| **Heavily Wounded** | HP lost > Willpower | 1 HP/week | Constitution HP/week |
| **Critically Wounded** | Critical Damage taken | Does not heal naturally | 1 Critical Damage/week with medical attention |

Once a Heavily Wounded character's lost HP drops to Willpower or below, she becomes Lightly Wounded. After all Critical Damage is removed, a character becomes Heavily Wounded.

## Movement

### Narrative Movement

In Narrative Time, exact distances don't matter. General rate: 20× Speed per minute, Speed km/hour, 10× that per day. Difficult terrain halves this.

**Hurrying:** Double movement rate, but Test Constitution (TN 10, +5 per hour after the first) each hour or gain 1 Fatigue.

### Climbing

Requires Athletics. Simple climbs (fences, trees, boulders) are automatic if you have a free hand and 1+ Athletics dots and aren't under pressure. Under pressure or on difficult surfaces: Athletics Test (default TN 15). Success = ascend up to half Speed; each Raise = +1m. Failure = fall.

### Jumping

With a 4m+ running start, Test Acrobatics + Strength (TN 10). Success = leap Strength meters horizontally (+1m per Raise). Failure = half Strength meters. Vertical height = half horizontal distance. From standing start: TN 15 instead of 10.

### Swimming

Swim Test (Athletics + Strength, TN 10) required in hazardous conditions. Success = move at half Speed. Medium+ armor raises TN to 20. Must hold breath if fully submerged.
